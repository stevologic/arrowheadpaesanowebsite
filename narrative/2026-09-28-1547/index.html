Last game

What the tape said

A real review of the most recent result — not a box-score dump, and not a skip-ahead.

W
Miami Dolphins Week 3 · Sun, Sep 27 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 24–10

Kansas City scored 2:06 into the first quarter when Walker, with J. Moore reported eligible, went up the middle for a 10-yard touchdown after Mahomes hit Kelce for 48 yards, then spent the rest of the afternoon trading punches with a Dolphins offense that kept the ball. Miami tied it 7-7 on a 3-yard touchdown, the Chiefs answered with a 5-yard touchdown for 14-7, and the second half became a clock fight: Miami’s 37-yard field goal made it 14-10, Kansas City’s 34-yard field goal made it 17-10, and an 11-yard touchdown at 2:55 of the fourth quarter finally made it 24-10.

The opening series was the version of Andy Reid’s offense everyone came to see. Under center, a deep-middle shot to Kelce to the Miami 19, then Walker through the A-gap for the 10-yard score. From there the run game lost the line of scrimmage. Walker still finished 18 carries for 70 yards, but the team managed only 88 on the ground, and the drive that died at the Miami 12 — two Walker runs for no gain, the second with H. Nourzad eligible — was the snapshot. He also lost six yards on a right-end run in the fourth quarter, so this was not a between-the-tackles identity that simply got stacked. It was a run game that could not finish.

Mahomes’ 20-of-24 is the number that travels on television. The complementary number is the five QB hits he took to post 20-of-24 with zero sacks, and the interception he threw at the two-minute warning of the second quarter, a deep-middle ball for Rice that J. Rodriguez took back 18 yards to the Miami 47. Completions still created the look Kansas City wants: Thornton and Worthy both won 17-yard shots down the sideline, Gray’s 24-yarder set up the 34-yard field goal, and Rice’s 34-yard catch to the Miami 15 preceded the 11-yard touchdown that closed it. Accuracy covered protection. It will not cover Crosby.

Spagnuolo’s defense won the score and lost the field. After the Chiefs’ opening strike 2:06 into the game, Miami answered with a 17-play grind that ate nearly 9.5 minutes and tied the game, and Spagnuolo pulled the unit aside. Nick Bolton finished with 11 tackles and lived in the run fits; the Dolphins still ran for 119. The splash plays were real: L. Sneed forced and recovered Willis’s fumble after Kansas City challenged the down-by-contact ruling, and Karlaftis intercepted Willis short right with Chris Jones in the throwing lane to ice the possession fight. Those are championship takeaways. They had to be, because Miami converted 8-of-16 third downs and Kansas City converted 3-of-7.

The finishing tape is mixed on purpose. Harrison Butker’s 34-yarder made it 17-10; his 50-yarder at 0:57 sailed wide left. Miami turned it over on downs twice in incomplete-pass situations, once to M. Delane and once to N. Williams. Kansas City did not dominate a team it was supposed to put away. It made enough explosive throws and enough takeaways to leave 3-0 with a 24-10 road win that still requires a grown-up meeting before Las Vegas.

What held up

  • Mahomes’ 20-of-24, 246-yard, two-touchdown day, including the deep-middle shot to Kelce that set up Walker’s 10-yard score
  • Clean looks for Thornton, Worthy, Gray, and Rice down the field
  • L. Sneed forcing and recovering Willis’s fumble after the replay challenge
  • Karlaftis intercepting Willis at the Miami 36 to snuff a late drive
  • Rice’s 7 catches for 88 yards as the volume outlet when the run stalled

What broke

  • Team rushing stuck at 88 yards, with Walker stuffed for no gain on consecutive snaps at the Miami 12 and a turnover on downs
  • Third down at 3-of-7 while Miami went 8-of-16 and held the ball 34:21
  • The interception Rodriguez took off Mahomes on the deep-middle ball for Rice
  • Protection that still allowed five QB hits on a 20-of-24 afternoon
  • Penalties (Tr. Smith, R. Thomas, L. Sneed, Karlaftis) and Butker’s 50-yard miss that kept the clock conversation alive

Efficiency covered a lost clock

Mahomes at 20-of-24 for 246 and two touchdowns is how you win 24-10 with 25:39 of possession. Miami’s 34:21 and 19 first downs are how you get honest about the next opponent. Las Vegas will not gift that completion percentage if the run game stays at 88.

Takeaways, not third down, closed Miami

Sneed’s forced fumble and recovery, then Karlaftis intercepting Willis, are the two possessions Kansas City stole. The Dolphins still won third down 8-16 to 3-7. Spagnuolo can live with splash plays. He cannot live with that conversion gap against a 3-0 division rival.

The run game has an identity problem, not a back problem

Walker’s 70 yards on 18 carries included the 10-yard touchdown, interior work, and end runs that cut both ways, including the minus-6. The team number is 88. That is not enough to keep play-action honest when Crosby is the next man in the backfield.

Current state

Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

3-0

Kansas City is 3-0 with wins over Denver, Indianapolis, and Miami, sitting tied with Las Vegas atop the AFC West, and still carrying the same two open items from Hard Rock Stadium: a run game that finished with 88 yards and a defense that could not get off the field on third down. The quarterback is playing at a champion’s completion rate. The supporting tape is playing at a favorite’s, not a juggernaut’s.

What we need to work on

Get the run game past 88

Walker’s 70 on 18 is a feature-back line. The team’s 88 is a problem against a Raiders front that will let Crosby wreck early downs if Kansas City has to throw from behind the sticks. Inside zone has to win first down on Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT, or play-action becomes a costume.

Third-down offense and third-down defense

Going 3-of-7 while the opponent goes 8-of-16 is how you post 25:39 of possession in a win. Spagnuolo already had to pull the unit aside after Miami’s opening grind. Las Vegas will script the same long drives if the second level does not rally and if the offense cannot stay on schedule.

Keep Mahomes off the ground

Twenty-of-24 does not erase the five QB hits. Screens, boots, and a committed run game are how Bieniemy keeps the quarterback upright against Crosby. If the protection plan is five-man dropback, the 3-0 start will start quoting last year’s 6-11 in a hurry.

What to think about

Is 3-0 a style or a scoreboard?

The Colts game produced 152 rushing yards and 37:00 of possession. Miami produced 88 and 25:39. Both are wins. Only one of those tapes survives Crosby and a 3-0 Raiders locker room that just got a blunt warning about this trip.

How much blitz is too much from Sneed?

The Week 3 snap counts made L’Jarius Sneed a featured blitzer, and he forced the fumble that flipped a series. Simulated pressure is Spagnuolo’s signature. It also walked Kansas City into holding and illegal-use flags. The Raiders game is where that dial either becomes a weapon or a self-inflicted down-and-distance problem.

What the market is actually buying

ESPN FPI at 70.2 percent and Chiefs -5.5 on 47.5 is respect. Polymarket at 8.6 percent to win the Super Bowl and 16.0 percent to win the AFC is probation. A division road game against a 3-0 club is how that gap either shrinks or gets quoted back to the fan base.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Las Vegas Raiders

Both clubs are 3-0 and tied atop the AFC West, and Kansas City is a 5.5-point road favorite on CBS at Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT. The assignment is to bring a heavier early-down identity than the 88-yard Miami tape and to keep Maxx Crosby from turning Mahomes’ 20-of-24 efficiency into a survival drill.

How they match up

Kansas City wants to live in play-action and let Mahomes throw with a clean platform. Las Vegas wants to make every dropback a Crosby snap and dare the Chiefs to run into a loaded box. Walker versus that front is the leverage point: if he can push the team number north of Miami’s 88, Kelce and Rice become answers instead of necessities. If he cannot, Spagnuolo’s defense — which just spent 34:21 on the field — will be asked to win another low-possession game on the road.

Boot and screen Crosby, do not sit on him

Boot away from Crosby and RB screens off the aggressive upfield rush are the two ways to cash the Miami passing tape without asking the tackles to win a 24-attempt game. Mahomes left Miami with five QB hits and no sacks — keep that pocket against Crosby.

Win first down so third down is not 3-of-7 again

Inside zone to Walker, with an extra lineman when Reid wants the J. Moore-eligible look that scored from 10 yards in Miami, has to create 2nd-and-5. Mesh to Rice and Kelce is the third-down answer if Las Vegas sits man. The Chiefs cannot live in 3rd-and-long on the road in this division.

Get off the field without waiting on a takeaway

Sneed’s fumble and Karlaftis’s interception won Miami. They are not a plan. Cover-2 to take the lid off, then simulated pressure with Sneed and a dropping lineman on money downs, is how Spagnuolo shortens the Raiders’ drives before the clock looks like 34:21 again.

  1. Open in 13 personnel with an extra lineman and Walker on inside zone — copy the Miami scoring look without waiting until you trail the sticks.
  2. Second series: under-center play-action boot away from Crosby, Kelce leaking, Worthy as the deep-over answer.
  3. Early RB screen to Walker or the backup when Crosby wins upfield; make the Raiders rush pay for the same aggression that produced five QB hits in Miami.
  4. Third-and-medium: mesh with Rice and Kelce rubbing, Noah Gray sitting, Walker as the checkdown.
  5. Defensively, start in a Cover-2 shell on early downs to keep the ball in front after Miami just posted 19 first downs.
  6. On 3rd-and-5 or longer, zone-blitz with Sneed from the slot and a dropping end so Jones and Karlaftis are not asked to win every rush on their own.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

The scoreboard in Miami said 24-10 and the record says 3-0, but the possession tape said the Dolphins were the ones living in Kansas City’s huddle. Patrick Mahomes went 20-of-24 for 246 yards and two touchdowns, Kenneth Walker III added 70 yards and the 10-yard score that opened it, and still the Chiefs left Hard Rock Stadium with 18 first downs, 88 rushing yards, and 25:39 with the ball. That is the film Steve Spagnuolo and Eric Bieniemy have to carry into a division road game against a Raiders team that is also 3-0 and already hearing Maxx Crosby’s warning.

Miami finished with 19 first downs, 119 rushing yards, 8-of-16 on third down, and 34:21 of possession. The Dolphins did not win. They did win the math that usually decides January. Kansas City answered with explosive throws — Travis Kelce’s 48-yard strike on the opening series, Rashee Rice’s 34-yarder in the fourth quarter, Noah Gray’s 24-yarder to the 8 — and with two takeaways, L. Sneed’s fumble recovery and Karlaftis intercepting Malik Willis. Efficiency covered a run game that stalled and a defense that kept jogging back on the field.

That is the uncomfortable 3-0. Last year’s 6-11 club taught this building what happens when the supporting tape never catches the quarterback. This year’s supporting tape is closer: Walker is a real back, Rice is a real volume receiver, and the defense can still create the ball. It is not yet the tape of a team that can survive Crosby crashing the pocket while the offense is 3-of-7 on third down.

Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT in Las Vegas is the first true AFC West measuring stick of 2026. ESPN FPI posts Kansas City at 70.2 percent. DraftKings hangs the Chiefs -5.5 on a 47.5 total. Those are favorite numbers, not immunity. The Chargers already sit three games back, which makes this Raiders game a chance to own the division early or to hand a 3-0 rival the same seat. The assignment is simple to say and hard to run: get the ball back, run it better than 88, and keep Mahomes from repeating the five QB hits he took in Miami.

Film room

The X’s & O’s

Concepts drawn up on the board — what it is, why it works, and the coaching point.

Offense

Jumbo inside zone, copy the 10-yard scoring look

Situation 1st-and-10, opening series at Las Vegas, extra lineman in the game

Kansas City just ran for 88 as a team in Miami and still needed Mahomes at 20-of-24 to win. Walker’s 10-yard touchdown came with J. Moore eligible; of the two stuffed snaps at the Miami 12, only the Q2 6:15 snap had H. Nourzad reported eligible. Against a 3-0 Raiders front, early-down zone is how you keep Crosby from pinning Mahomes in 3rd-and-long.

Coaching point Walker has to press the heel of the center and take the one cut — the minus-6 on the right end is the cut you do not bring to this game. Nourzad and the extra lineman must win the double on the three-technique so Bolton’s counterpart cannot walk into the backfield free.

Offense

Play-action boot away from Crosby

Situation 2nd-and-5 after a Walker zone run, Crosby aligned over the left tackle

The Miami passing tape still produced the 48- and 24-yard shots, and Mahomes sat in the pocket through five QB hits and zero sacks. Booting him to the opposite hash turns Crosby’s upfield rush into a pursuit angle and recreates that same easy-throw platform.

Coaching point Bieniemy has to sell the zone fake with the same footwork as the inside-zone series or Crosby will not bite. Kelce leaks, Worthy runs the over, and Mahomes’ first read is the flat — throw it before the pursuit, do not reset into the rush he just escaped in Miami.

Offense

Mesh to Rice and Kelce on third down

Situation 3rd-and-6, Chiefs needing to bury the 3-of-7 Miami tape

Kansas City converted 3-of-7 in Miami and still won because Rice (7-88) and Kelce created chunk gains on schedule. If Las Vegas plays man to take away the deep shot, the rub between Rice and Kelce is the conversion answer that keeps Mahomes from another hit-and-hope dropback.

Coaching point Rice owns the right-to-left rub and has to flatten at five yards, not climb into the linebacker. Kelce takes the opposite mesh and sits if the Mike drops. Walker is the outlet — Mahomes checked it down in Miami when the pocket collapsed; take the same throw and live.

Offense

Walker screen when Crosby climbs

Situation 2nd-and-7, Raiders showing five-man pressure with Crosby wide

The Miami tape said Mahomes can complete anything if he is upright, and it also said he will not stay upright if the plan is five-man protection. A perimeter screen to Walker — who already handled 20 touches — punishes Crosby’s upfield takeoff and replaces some of the 88 rushing yards without asking the interior to win a phone booth.

Coaching point The left tackle must whiff on purpose and climb to the safety, not chase Crosby. Gray and the playside guard are the actual lead blockers. Walker has to let the throw arrive; the minus-6 end run is what happens when he accelerates before the wall is set.

Defense

Cover-2 shell, keep the ball in front

Situation 1st-and-10, Raiders coming off their own 3-0 tape, early down in Las Vegas

Miami just posted 19 first downs, 119 rushing yards, and 34:21 of possession, including a 42-yard shot to Dulcich through the middle of the field. Spagnuolo’s two-deep, five-under shell is how you take that shot away and make Bolton and Tranquill rally, instead of living on the splash plays from Sneed and Karlaftis.

Coaching point Safeties split the posts and do not cheat the run until the mesh point. Bolton fits inside-out and finishes the 11-tackle work he posted in Miami. Corners funnel to the two-deep; the miss is the vertical from the slot, not the tackle after the catch.

Defense

Sneed simulated pressure, drop the end

Situation 3rd-and-5, Raiders in shotgun, money down after Miami went 8-of-16

The snap-count tape made L’Jarius Sneed a featured blitzer, and he forced and recovered a fumble playing downhill. Spagnuolo cannot wait on another Karlaftis interception to get off the field. Showing heat, dropping a lineman, and sending Sneed from the second level is the call that shortens drives against a 3-0 offense without asking Jones to win every snap as a four-man rush.

Coaching point Sneed’s path is the A-gap or the slot seam. The dropping end has to land in the throwing lane Willis kept missing in Miami. Jones still occupies two blockers. Karlaftis rushes off the opposite edge so the hot throw has nowhere clean to go, and he has to stay legal doing it: the illegal-use flag at Q4 0:47 wiped Roland-Wallace’s interception.

The storylines

What we’re watching

QB

Mahomes was perfect enough, and still too available

Twenty-of-24 for 246 and two touchdowns is a clean passing afternoon, not a survival tape. The five QB hits he took to post it are the clip Crosby will watch. Kansas City can stay 3-0 on the road only if the answers are boots and screens.

RB

Walker’s 70 cannot stay a team 88

Eighteen carries, 70 yards, and the 10-yard touchdown are feature work. End runs cut both ways, including the minus-6, and the two stuffed snaps at the Miami 12 killed a red-zone series. Las Vegas is where the interior identity has to show up.

BALL

Sneed created one, Karlaftis closed one

L. Sneed forced and recovered Willis’s fumble after the challenge. Karlaftis intercepted Willis short right with Jones in the picture. Those two possessions are why 25:39 of offense still produced 24 points. They are not a substitute for getting off the field on third down.

DIVISION

Crosby already crashed the party

The Raiders are 3-0 and tied with Kansas City. Crosby made sure the celebration came with a warning about this Chiefs trip. That is the correct temperature for a road favorite that just lost the clock in Miami.

WEST

The Chargers are already three back

A win in Las Vegas would put Kansas City in position to own the AFC West before the Chargers even arrive in Week 6. A loss hands a 3-0 rival the same seat. The market’s 70.2 percent is not the same thing as a comfortable week.

Chess match

Coaching & adjustments

Reid and Bieniemy have to script the run they did not finish

The 10-yard jumbo score was the identity. The stuffed snaps at the Miami 12 were the week’s problem. Las Vegas is not the place to hope 20-of-24 shows up again without an early-down run plan.

Spagnuolo already held the meeting

He pulled the defense aside after Miami’s opening 17-play grind. The next version of that meeting has to happen on the sideline in Las Vegas, not after another 34:21 possession tape.

Crosby set the temperature

The Raiders’ 3-0 celebration came with Crosby’s warning about this Chiefs trip. Reid’s staff should treat that as the scouting report: the edge is coming, and the answers are boots, screens, and first-down runs.
