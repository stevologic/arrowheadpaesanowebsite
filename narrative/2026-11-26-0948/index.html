Short week, Walker out, seed on the line

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

8-2

Kansas City is 8-2 and a road underdog on a short week, with Walker out and the No. 5 seed in front of a Buffalo-Rams-Cincinnati stretch.

What we need to work on

Finish the run without Walker

Johnson's 105 yards carried the Arizona ground game inside a 118-yard team total. Bieniemy has to keep early downs on schedule with Jaydn Ott and Nate Carter as changeups, not as a replacement feature.

Keep Mahomes off the ground

One sack and five hits is manageable. A Thursday road start against Josh Allen does not need another grounding or a stalled red-zone series like the two Kansas City field goals of 40 and 44.

Hold the third-down gain

Arizona was 7-of-13 after Atlanta was 4-of-13. That gain has to show up again, because Buffalo can live on long fields if Kansas City starts stacking third-and-medium.

What to think about

How much is Johnson's game now

Arrowhead Addict has him in the top four of Offensive Rookie of the Year odds. Walker being out makes the touches a plan, not a cameo. Reid still has to decide how many of the 27 from Arizona he wants repeated.

Seed math versus the price

A 9-1 home favorite at minus 3 is a real problem. Winning moves the No. 5 conversation. Losing on a 45.5 total still leaves a Rams trip four days later.

Looking ahead

How Kansas City should play Buffalo

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Buffalo Bills

Beat a 9-1 Buffalo team on Thu Nov 26, 7:20 PM CT at NBC as a three-point underdog, with Walker out and FPI still leaning Bills at 52.6 percent.

How they match up

Kansas City's recent identity is Mahomes efficiency, Kelce in the middle, and Johnson as the early-down runner. Buffalo's identity, in durable terms, is Josh Allen plus a home favorite's margin. Arrowhead Pride frames this as the league's premier rivalry, 8-2 against 9-1. The leverage is down-and-distance: stay out of third-and-long, and do not give Allen a short field after a sack or a penalty.

Johnson on schedule

Inside zone and a screen keep Buffalo's front honest. If the 23-carry role shrinks, Mahomes will see more than the five hits he took against Arizona.

Defined throws to Kelce, Rice, and Downs

Kelce had six catches and two touchdowns. Rashee Rice had six catches. Josh Downs had four in this game and, per Arrowhead Pride, eight catches on 28 snaps across two games with Cyrus Allen also in the rotation. Use the rubs. Do not hunt a hero ball on every snap.

Two-deep, then the zone blitz as a call

Spagnuolo should take the deep shot away with Cover-2 and use a zone blitz on long downs. That is a call for Buffalo, not a claim about the Arizona sack-fumble, which Fryar recovered.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Johnson and force a light box before any shot play.
  2. On second-and-medium, mesh Kelce and Rice with Downs as the sit or corner tag and Johnson as the checkdown.
  3. Steal a possession with a screen if the rush shows heat after Mahomes' one sack and five hits last game.
  4. In the red zone, prefer the defined middle throw that became Kelce's 22-yard touchdown over an undefined fade.
  5. After a change of possession, start in Cover-2 so Allen does not get a free vertical on the first snap of a drive.
