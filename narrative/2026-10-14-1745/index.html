Last game

What the tape said

A real review of the most recent result — not a box-score dump, and not a skip-ahead.

W
Las Vegas Raiders Week 4 · Sun, Oct 4 · 3:25 PM CT
KC 30–27

At Allegiant Stadium, Las Vegas led 13-10 at halftime and 19-17 in the fourth quarter before Kansas City scored 13 points in a short window and held on, 30-27. Walker ran for 177 yards and two touchdowns on 22 carries. Thornton caught five passes for 111 yards and two touchdowns. Mahomes finished 15-of-30.

Las Vegas scored on the game’s opening touchdown, a 27-yard pass from Cousins to Ian Thomas at Q1 8:10, and the board read 0-7. Kansas City tied it with a 2-yard touchdown at Q1 2:23. The second quarter was a field-goal trade: Las Vegas from 32 at Q2 3:19 (7-10), Harrison Butker from 28 at Q2 1:22 (10-10), and Las Vegas from 48 as the half expired (10-13). No points were posted in the third quarter. The game was decided after the teams came out for the fourth.

Kansas City took the lead on an 8-yard touchdown at Q4 14:50 (17-13). Las Vegas answered with field goals of 44 yards at Q4 11:12 (17-16) and 48 yards at Q4 4:53 (17-19). A 2-yard Kansas City touchdown at Q4 3:21 made it 23-19. On the ensuing kickoff, Dareke Young fumbled; J. Bassa forced it and Jack Cochrane recovered at the Las Vegas 22. Walker then ran right end for 22 yards and a touchdown at Q4 3:02, and Butker’s extra point made it 30-19. Cousins found Brock Bowers for a 20-yard touchdown at Q4 1:46, and the two-point pass to Cody White cut it to 30-27.

The listed throws that traveled were not a volume parade. Mahomes hit Worthy on a 41-yard deep middle in the first quarter and Thornton on a 42-yard deep left before halftime, then a 55-yard deep left to Thornton at Q4 3:35, with Thornton injured on the play and Quay Walker flagged for unnecessary roughness. Incompletions went deep to Worthy, C. Allen, and Thornton. Team net passing stayed at 210. Do not read the 225-yard passer line as the team total, and do not import Miami’s passing box into this one.

Las Vegas lived on touches and clock. Ashton Jeanty had 21 touches (15 rushes, six catches). Michael Mayer caught eight. Bowers caught six, including the late touchdown. Mike Washington Jr. had 10 touches. Kansas City’s tackle chart was led by Mansoor Delane with 10. The two Las Vegas turnovers were the kickoff fumble — forced by J. Bassa, recovered by Jack Cochrane — and the short-left interception by X. Nwankpa, with George Karlaftis in on the play, at Q4 0:10. Kansas City turnovers on the box: zero. A late Creed Humphrey fumble was recovered by Mahomes.

What held up

  • Walker’s 177 rushing yards and the 22-yard right-end touchdown after Cochrane’s recovery
  • Thornton’s five catches, 111 yards, and two receiving touchdowns before he left injured
  • Zero Kansas City turnovers against two Las Vegas giveaways
  • Matt Araiza’s punt-formation 12-yard run and a five-punt inside-the-20 day that earned AFC special-teams honors

What broke

  • Third down at 5-of-13 and a 15-of-30 passing line
  • Two sacks and eight quarterback hits on Mahomes
  • Las Vegas holding 35:42 and stacking 28 first downs while Kansas City sat at 24:18 and 18 first downs
  • Penalties on N. Williams, P. Woods, Felix Anudike-Uzomah, and L. Sneed that wiped plays
  • Thornton injured on the 55-yard catch, which now reshapes the receiver rotation

The rush was the offense

Walker’s 22 carries and 177 yards, plus one catch, made 23 touches. Team rushing was 189 on 26 attempts. That is the identity Eric Bieniemy can sell on Sun Oct 18, especially with Thornton out of the vertical menu.

The passer line was uneven, not empty

Mahomes was 15-of-30 with two touchdowns, two sacks, eight quarterback hits, and a 97.2 rating. Kansas City was 5-of-13 on third down. The explosives to Worthy and Thornton covered a lot of the 210 net passing yards.

Spagnuolo squeezed the end, not the middle of the game

Cousins was 31-of-52 and Las Vegas netted 365 passing yards without being sacked. Kansas City still recorded 10 quarterback hits, then closed on the Cochrane recovery and the Nwankpa interception. Cover-2 and a zone blitz are calls for Herbert, not a retelling of how those takeaways happened.

Current state

Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

4-0

Kansas City is 4-0, sat the Week 5 bye on that record, and is treated like a top-tier club in the Week 6 rankings. The football to fix is third down, the hits on Mahomes, and a passing menu that just lost its leading receiver.

What we need to work on

Redraw the targets without Thornton

Thornton led the listed Kansas City skill touches in the passing game with five catches and 111 yards, and he had both receiving touchdowns. Worthy had four catches, Kelce two, Gray two. Arrowhead Pride’s rotation piece is the assignment: someone else has to threaten vertically or Bieniemy has to live in the intermediate and the run.

Finish pressures and clean the flags

Ten quarterback hits on Cousins produced a box that lists him without a sack. Eight hits and two sacks landed on Mahomes. L. Sneed’s defensive holding and Anudike-Uzomah’s roughing flag wiped late plays. Spagnuolo needs the second level to rush on schedule and the back end to tackle without handing Cousins-style drives extra snaps.

Keep the 189-yard rush identity while Simmons practices

The Miami game is a separate box: 88 rushing yards, a 246-yard passing day, 25:39 of possession. Allegiant was the correction on the ground. Reid has Simmons back on the practice field. Kahlil Benson’s role can shift if the left tackle sticks, but the Week 6 plan should not assume a full game of Simmons yet.

What to think about

Is 4-0 a ceiling or a sample?

Polymarket still has the Chiefs at 9.8 percent to win the championship and 17.5 percent to win the AFC. ESPN FPI likes this one game at 73.9 percent. The honest read is a home favorite whose last win allowed 365 Las Vegas net passing yards.

Trade buzz versus Watson

Arrowhead Pride floated a midseason receiver addition after Thornton’s timeline, and the club signed Justin Watson to the practice squad. Cap space on the Pride roster page was $3.5 million as of Tuesday, October 13. That is a constraint, not a blank check.

Linebacker rotation if Cooper McDonald is available

Arrowhead Addict notes McDonald could return and that the linebacker questions do not end there. Drue Tranquill, Nick Bolton, and the second level have to fit whatever Spagnuolo calls against Herbert.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Los Angeles Chargers

Home favorite, CBS, Sun Oct 18, 3:25 PM CT at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium. The assignment is to make Herbert throw into a two-deep shell, keep Walker’s downhill identity, and give Mahomes answers that do not require Thornton’s vertical.

How they match up

Kansas City brings a 4-0 record, a 189-yard rushing game fresh in the tape, and a quarterback who just absorbed two sacks and eight hits on 15-of-30. Los Angeles brings Herbert, a quarterback who can punish a single-high look the way Cousins did for 365 net yards when the rush did not finish. Simmons is practicing, not yet a declared every-down tackle. The receiver matchup tilts toward whoever Kansas City trusts opposite Worthy once Thornton is out.

Early-down Walker

Stay on schedule with inside zone and the touches Walker already proved he can carry. A 5-of-13 third-down day gets easier if second down is not 8.

Easy answers for a hit quarterback

Mesh and the screen are how Bieniemy keeps Mahomes off eight-hit afternoons without asking Worthy to be Thornton on every snap.

Two-deep, then the zone blitz

Spagnuolo’s Cover-2 takes the deep shot. The zone blitz is the change-up when Herbert settles into the five-under. It is a call for this Sunday, not a story about the Nwankpa interception.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Walker and a reported heavy look only on the snaps that actually declare it — the Allegiant tape had K. Tonga eligible at Q1 11:29 with H. Nourzad, and Tonga eligible again at Q1 6:01 without that same pair listed.
  2. After two successful runs, boot Mahomes to the perimeter with Kelce as the flat answer and Worthy clearing. Sell Walker; do not label a throw play-action unless the call sheet says play fake.
  3. On 3rd-and-medium, mesh Kelce and Gray and leave Walker as the checkdown so the eight-hit problem has an outlet.
  4. Call a spacing screen when the Chargers edge widens, with the line leading and Worthy or Walker in space.
  5. Defense: Cover-2 on early downs, Delane and the safeties rallying to Jeanty-style checkdowns if Herbert dumps it. Zone-blitz on a 3rd-and-6 in Chargers territory — show heat, drop a defensive lineman, rush a linebacker.
  6. Special teams: trust Araiza’s inside-20 habit and Butker’s short range. Field position mattered at Allegiant when the kickoff unit produced the Cochrane recovery.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

The 4-0 Chiefs did not own Allegiant Stadium’s clock and did not need a clean passing line to leave with a 30-27 win. Kenneth Walker III carried the afternoon, Tyquan Thornton scored through the air and then came off injured, and the next assignment is a home date with Justin Herbert on Sun Oct 18, 3:25 PM CT.

Kansas City’s box at Las Vegas was 18 first downs, 189 rushing yards on 26 attempts, 210 net passing yards, 399 total yards, 5-of-13 on third down, 12 drives, zero turnovers, and 24:18 of possession. Las Vegas posted its own line: 28 first downs, 72 rushing yards, 365 net passing yards, 437 total yards, 5-of-14 on third down, 12 drives, two turnovers, and 35:42 of possession. Those clocks stay with the teams on the box. The scoreboard still flipped late because Walker and the return unit turned a kick into points.

Patrick Mahomes was 15-of-30 for 225 yards and two touchdowns, with two sacks and a 97.2 rating. The opponent recorded eight quarterback hits on him. Kirk Cousins was 31-of-52 for an 85.8 rating and was not sacked, while Kansas City recorded 10 quarterback hits. That is the Steve Spagnuolo problem walking into Arrowhead: heat that has to finish, and a shell that has to take the deep shot away from Herbert the way it eventually squeezed Cousins.

The bye already sat between Allegiant and this Chargers date. Andy Reid said Josh Simmons (back) will practice ahead of Week 6. Thornton’s dislocated ankle carries a 12-to-16-week timeline, and the receiver rotation has to be redrawn around Xavier Worthy, Travis Kelce, Noah Gray, and a practice-squad signing in Justin Watson. Favored at home is the market. Replacing 111 yards and two touchdowns from Thornton is the football.

The X’s & O’s

Concepts drawn up on the board — what it is, why it works, and the coaching point.

Offense

Walker downhill on early downs

Situation 1st-and-10 at Arrowhead against the Chargers, before Herbert’s offense can flip the clock the way Las Vegas did

Walker just posted 177 on 22 carries and the team rushed for 189. With Thornton out, Bieniemy needs that identity so Mahomes is not living on 15-of-30 and eight hits.

Coaching point Zone steps, one cut, Walker presses the listed up-the-middle track from the Allegiant carries and bends only if the Chargers end crashes. Creed Humphrey has to be clean after the late fumble he put on the ground and Mahomes recovered.

Offense

Boot off the Walker sell

Situation 2nd-and-5 or better after a successful inside zone, Chargers edge widened

The run total is real enough to move the pocket. Mahomes took two sacks and eight hits staying in the pocket on a 15-of-30 day. A boot gives Kelce the easy flat and Worthy the deep clear without asking Thornton to be on the field.

Coaching point Reid and Bieniemy: sell Walker, get Mahomes to the perimeter, and throw the flat if the flat defender sits. This is a Sunday call, not a label for any Allegiant throw the play-by-play did not mark as play-action.

Offense

Mesh for the thinner receiver room

Situation 3rd-and-5 to 3rd-and-7 versus man, Thornton unavailable

Kelce and Gray each caught two balls at Allegiant and have to be the rub pair. Worthy’s four catches included a 41-yarder; he does not have to be the entire tree if the crossers are there. Walker is the outlet after 23 touches proved he can be on the field.

Coaching point Kelce and Gray cross at depth, Worthy sits the corner route, Walker checks it down if Mahomes feels the edge that produced two sacks. No hero ball on 3rd-and-long.

Offense

Screen to take the hits off the quarterback

Situation 2nd-and-8 after a Chargers pressure look, Mahomes coming off eight hits and two sacks

Easy offense is how a 210-net-yard passing day stays efficient when the deep threat is on a 12-to-16-week timeline. The line leads, Walker or Worthy catches in space, and the Chargers rush runs past the ball.

Coaching point Bieniemy: call it before the down becomes desperate. The Allegiant fake-punt run by Araiza showed the staff will use formation; this one is a real screen, with the receivers spacing the second level so Walker has a lane.

Defense

Cover-2 against Herbert’s shot

Situation 1st-and-10 and 2nd-and-long when Los Angeles wants the Cousins-style deep middle

Las Vegas netted 365 passing yards with Cousins at 31-of-52, including Thomas for 27, White for 27, Bowers for 20, and Tucker for 23. Two deep and five under is how Spagnuolo takes that shot menu away and lets Delane rally.

Coaching point Safeties stay deeper than Tucker’s 23-yarder. Corners jam and sink. Bolton and Tranquill fit the checkdown so Herbert cannot turn a safe throw into Jeanty’s 21-touch afternoon. Tackle after the catch.

Defense

Zone blitz as the third-down change-up

Situation 3rd-and-6 in Chargers territory, Herbert in rhythm against the five-under

Kansas City recorded 10 quarterback hits at Allegiant and still did not sack Cousins. The zone blitz is the call that rushes a linebacker and drops a lineman so Herbert’s hot read is a trap, not a free completion. It is a plan for Sun Oct 18, not a credit for any takeaway.

Coaching point Spagnuolo: show four, send Tranquill or Bolton, drop Karlaftis into the throwing lane. Anudike-Uzomah has to rush clean after the roughing flag. L. Sneed stays in the zone and tackles; no holding penalty that hands back a down.
