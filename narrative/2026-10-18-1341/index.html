Current state

Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

4-0

Kansas City is 4-0 after wins over Denver, Indianapolis, Miami, and Las Vegas, with a Week 5 bye already banked. The offense just proved it can win a one-score road game on the ground. The receiver chart and the left side of the line are the open files.

What we need to work on

Finish pressures as sacks, or as coverage wins

Ten hits that never finished let Cousins stack 365 net yards. Against Herbert, Spagnuolo needs Cover-2 rally tackles and a zone blitz that muddies the picture, because hits alone did not end drives in Las Vegas.

Redistribute Thornton’s eight targets

Thornton had eight targets, five catches, and 111 yards. Worthy, Kelce, Gray, Cyrus Allen, and the elevated Watson and Armstrong have to own those snaps. Fans already want more Allen. The staff has to decide if that is a package or a wish.

Third down without the chunk receiver

Kansas City was 5-of-13 on third down and had 18 first downs. Early downs for Walker and Johnson keep Mahomes off 30-attempt afternoons when the vertical answer is thinner.

What to think about

Is 4-0 a contender sample or a close-game sample?

Polymarket has the Chiefs at 9.8 percent to win the championship and 17.5 percent to win the AFC. The last game was 30-27 with the opponent holding the ball for 35:42. Home chalk at -7.5 should not erase that tape.

Deadline noise versus Sunday’s roster

Arrowhead Pride reports the club does not seem in a rush on a Tyreek Hill reunion, with a decision before November 10. Watson and Armstrong are the elevations that actually dress for the Chargers.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Los Angeles Chargers

Win the division game at home without treating 0-5 as a walkover. Los Angeles is averaging fewer than 17 points and rejecting trade calls. Kansas City is favored, short two skill starters and Josh Simmons, and coming off an afternoon in which the other quarterback was not sacked.

How they match up

Kansas City wants Walker’s inside and edge runs, Kelce on crossers, and Worthy on the vertical. The Chargers want Herbert to find explosives the way Cousins found Thomas, Bowers, White, and Tre Tucker. Spagnuolo’s two-deep shell is the counter. Bieniemy’s constraint is personnel: Rice out, Thornton sidelined, Simmons out, Watson and Armstrong elevated.

Stay on the ground early

Feed Walker and sprinkle Johnson so the Chargers’ front declares. The Las Vegas tape already showed 189 rushing yards can produce points when the pass game is 15-of-30.

Two-deep, then rally

Take the 20- and 27-yard shots away from Herbert and make the checkdown earn yards after catch. Delane’s 10-tackle game is the template for the second level.

Do not chase the number

DraftKings has Kansas City -7.5 with a 41.5 total. A low total and a thin receiver room argue for field position, Butker, and Araiza, not a track meet.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Walker and a Khyiris Tonga eligible snap.
  2. Call a boot off that run look so Mahomes is on the perimeter with Kelce and Worthy.
  3. Use mesh versus man for Kelce and Gray, with Johnson as the checkdown.
  4. Steal a screen for Walker when the Chargers chase eight hits the way Las Vegas did.
  5. Live in Cover-2 against Herbert’s deep left and deep right, the windows Cousins used.
  6. Call a zone blitz on a clear passing down to drop a lineman and rush a second-level defender.

Las Vegas Raiders Week 4 · Sun, Oct 4 · 3:25 PM CT
KC 30–27

At Allegiant Stadium, Las Vegas led 13-10 at the half behind a 27-yard touchdown at Q1 8:10 and field goals of 32 and 48 yards, then Kansas City closed 30-27 after Walker’s 22-yard touchdown and an interception by Nwankpa.

The opening score was a 27-yard Cousins touchdown to Ian Thomas at Q1 8:10, extra point good, Kansas City 0-7. Kansas City tied it at Q1 2:23 on a 2-yard touchdown. Harrison Butker’s 28-yard field goal at Q2 1:22 made it 10-10, and a 48-yard Raiders field goal at Q2 0:00 sent the clubs to the break at 10-13.

Walker was the offense. He had 22 carries for 177 yards and two rushing touchdowns, and 23 touches with one catch. The play-by-play shows him up the middle, left end, and, on the score that made it 30-19, right end for 22 yards. Mahomes was 15-of-30 for 225 yards and two touchdowns, rating 97.2, with two sacks. Net passing was 210. The opponent recorded 8 quarterback hits on him. Kansas City recorded 10 quarterback hits on Cousins. Tonga reported eligible twice in Las Vegas: Nourzad and Tonga reported together on the no-gain to the 50, and Tonga reported on the later Mahomes scramble.

Thornton caught five passes for 111 yards and two touchdowns, including a 42-yarder to the Las Vegas 5 and a 55-yarder to the Las Vegas 5 before Quay Walker’s unnecessary roughness. Xavier Worthy had four catches, including a 41-yarder to the Las Vegas 7. Travis Kelce and Noah Gray had two catches each. Emmett Johnson had one. That distribution will not be available the same way on Sunday.

Walker’s 2-yard score at Q4 3:21 flipped it to 23-19, then special teams broke it open. Butker kicked off, Dareke Young returned it, J. Bassa forced the fumble, and Jack Cochrane recovered at the Las Vegas 22. Walker scored from 22 yards on the right end. Cousins still hit Brock Bowers for a 20-yard touchdown at Q4 1:46, and the two-point pass to Cody White made it 30-27. Xavier Nwankpa intercepted a short throw intended for Michael Mayer at Q4 0:10. Las Vegas also failed on downs in the third quarter. Kansas City failed on downs in the first, Walker up the middle for 1 yard.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

The 4-0 Chiefs did not bury Las Vegas. They survived a 35:42 possession afternoon in which Kirk Cousins was 31-of-52 for 365 net passing yards, then stole the fourth quarter with Kenneth Walker III and a kickoff fumble. That is the tape Andy Reid, Eric Bieniemy, and Steve Spagnuolo carry into a home date with a winless Chargers team that still has Justin Herbert.

Kansas City’s box was efficient and short: 18 first downs, 26 rushes for 189 yards, 210 net passing yards, 399 total, 5-of-13 on third down, 12 drives, zero turnovers, and 24:18 of the clock. Las Vegas answered with 28 first downs, 437 yards, and four field goals before the late touchdown. The margin was real. The control was not.

The cost is the passing room. Tyquan Thornton’s five catches for 111 yards and two scores included a 55-yard catch on which he was injured, and he is now sidelined with a dislocated ankle. Rashee Rice is out with a hamstring. Josh Simmons is out. Justin Watson and Andrew Armstrong are up from the practice squad for Sunday.

Los Angeles is 0-5 and averaging fewer than 17 points, and Arrowhead Addict’s crew has little faith in an upset. Arrowhead Pride also has the Chargers rejecting trade calls because they still believe they are a team. Treat Herbert as a division quarterback, not a bye-week prop.

After this one the slate turns to Seattle on Sun Oct 25, 7:20 PM CT and Denver on Sun Nov 1, 3:25 PM CT. The November 10 deadline and a Tyreek Hill conversation can wait. The snap that matters is Sun Oct 18, 3:25 PM CT at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium.

The X’s & O’s

Concepts drawn up on the board — what it is, why it works, and the coaching point.

Offense

Walker inside zone to open the clock

Situation Early downs at Arrowhead against a winless Chargers front, before Herbert can flip the field

Walker just ran 22 times for 177 yards, including up the middle and left end, and cashed two rushing touchdowns. With Rice and Thornton unavailable and Simmons out, Bieniemy should keep the ball on the ground so Mahomes is not asked for another 30-attempt afternoon.

Coaching point Zone steps, one cut, and a declared double-team.

Offense

Boot off the Walker picture

Situation First-and-10 after a successful inside zone, Chargers linebackers fitting the run

The run identity is credible after 189 rushing yards. Moving Mahomes to the perimeter gives Kelce and Worthy defined reads while the replacement tackle, with Simmons out, is not asked to hold a static pocket for a deep drop.

Coaching point Sell the zone, boot away from the pressure side, and throw the flat or the cross on time.

Offense

Mesh for Kelce with Allen as the sit

Situation Third-and-medium versus man, receiver room missing Rice and Thornton

Kelce had two catches in Las Vegas and remains the reliable option. Arrowhead Pride’s Reacts poll wants more Cyrus Allen. A rub-plus-sit gives Allen a defined catch and gives Mahomes an answer that does not require Thornton’s 42- and 55-yard shots.

Coaching point Kelce and Gray cross. Allen sits in the hole. Johnson is the outlet. Mahomes throws the rub winner, not a hero ball into a replaced vertical.

Offense

Walker screen when the rush declares

Situation Obvious passing down if the Chargers hunt the Simmons replacement the way Las Vegas landed 8 hits

Mahomes was sacked twice and hit 8 times on a 15-of-30 day. A screen lets the line lead for Walker, who already had 23 touches, and punishes a rush that flies up the field.

Coaching point Slow the mesh, throw the screen on time, and let Walker make one cut. Johnson can take the same picture later so the Chargers cannot sit on one back.

Defense

Cover-2 against Herbert’s explosives

Situation Early downs and third-and-long, Chargers trying to steal a 20- to 27-yard shot

Cousins hit Thomas for 27 and a touchdown, White for 27, Tucker for 23, and Bowers for 20 and a touchdown. Two deep and five under takes those shots away and asks Delane, Bolton, and Tranquill to rally.

Coaching point Corners sink. Safeties stay on top. Linebackers fit the checkdown. Karlaftis rushes four unless the zone-blitz call is on. Do not turn a two-deep shell into a chase of one vertical.

Defense

Zone blitz on a declared pass down

Situation Second-and-long or third-and-long, Herbert in the gun, Kansas City needing a different picture than the Las Vegas tape

Ten quarterback hits did not stop 365 net passing yards. Spagnuolo should show heat, drop a defensive lineman, and rush a second-level player so Herbert throws into a rotated zone.

Coaching point Karlaftis can be the dropped lineman or the rusher depending on the call. Bolton or Tranquill inserts. The coverage behind it stays two-deep principles. Finish the rush or plaster the hot throw. A hit that does not change the down is what Las Vegas survived.
