The edge

ESPN FPI puts Kansas City at 78.1 percent to win in Atlanta, and DraftKings lists the Chiefs minus 3.5 on a 45.5 total — a road favorite whose 23-of-31, 117.9 line still came with two sacks, eight quarterback hits, and two turnovers.

Current state

A 6-2 club with a deadline and day-to-day tags

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

6-2

The Chiefs are 6-2, ESPN’s power ranking is unchanged at 6, and the next three kickoffs are Atlanta, Arizona, and Buffalo. The staff’s live questions are ball security, Mahomes’ day-to-day tag, and whether Veach adds a piece before the deadline cutoff Reid’s podium was already discussing.

What we need to work on

Protect the second sack

Mahomes was sacked twice and hit eight times. One of those sacks became a fumble McDonald recovered, and the play-by-play notes he was injured on that play. Atlanta will not need a exotic plan if the pocket looks like Q2 1:41.

Finish the possession that starts minus the ball

Fields’ fumble, with Nourzad reported eligible, handed New York the ball at the Kansas City 34. A 6-2 road favorite cannot donate a short field and then rely on five touchdowns to erase it.

Decide the Walker dose before Thursday in Buffalo

Walker already has a 22-touch game on the sheet. Arizona is Sun Nov 22, 12:00 PM CT, and Buffalo is Thu Nov 26, 7:20 PM CT. Bieniemy can feature him in Atlanta without emptying the tank if Johnson (6 touches) and Jaydn Ott (3) stay in the script, the way they did after the game was in hand.

What to think about

How much of 6-2 is the opponent

Arrowhead Addict argues the record deserves more respect than the critics allow, and also notes Kansas City is not the only club helped by schedule. FPI’s 78.1 percent in Atlanta is a strong number. It is not a certificate.

Whether the deadline is a receiver week

Arrowhead Pride reports smoke at wide receiver and quotes Reid saying Veach keeps his eyes open, with the cutoff described as Tuesday, November 10, at 3:00 p.m. Arrowhead time. Rice, Worthy, and Kelce just produced the catch counts. A deal would change the Atlanta menu. No deal is listed as completed.

What day-to-day means for the starter

Arrowhead Pride says Reid labeled three players day-to-day and names Mahomes in that group. Arrowhead Addict says Reid also addressed concerns about Mahomes, Butker, and mounting injuries. The quarterback finished the Jets game. The tag still belongs in the Atlanta meeting.

Looking ahead

What the 3.5 number actually asks in Atlanta

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Atlanta Falcons

Kansas City is a 3.5-point road favorite on Sun Nov 15, 12:00 PM CT on CBS, in a 45.5 total, with FPI at 78.1 percent. The assignment is to take the Jets’ efficient down-and-distance tape on the road without the two giveaways, and to make Spagnuolo’s rush a weekly habit rather than a home spike.

How they match up

Spagnuolo can lean on the 7 quarterback hits and Jones’ two sacks as the standard, not as a prediction of another five-sack day.

Live in inside zone before the shot

Walker’s up-the-middle carries were the profitable pattern on the Jets tape. In Atlanta, Bieniemy should stay on that downhill look on first down so Mahomes is not asked to create from a muddied pocket.

Give Kelce and Rice the third-down rub

Kelce’s 72 yards on four catches and Rice’s five receptions are the answers when the rush is closing. Mesh and a sit route beat hero ball on third-and-medium.

Rush the sticks, then drop into two-deep

The Jets managed 15 first downs and 2-of-12 on third down when the rush arrived. Cover-2 after a simulated pressure is the call that keeps a home underdog from stealing a 54-yard field-goal kind of drive.

Treat 22 touches as a plan, not a dare

Walker can lead the script. Johnson and Ott are already in the touch list. Saving a few early-down carries matters with Arizona and Buffalo on the next two lines.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Walker and a Mahomes keep only if the end crashes, the way his two rushes against the Jets stayed small.
  2. On the first third-and-medium, throw mesh to Kelce and Rice with Worthy clearing the corner, and keep Noah Gray as the check if the linebacker walls it.
  3. Boot off the run fake once Atlanta widens to Walker, with Worthy on the perimeter and Kelce in the flat.
  4. Use a Walker screen when the hit count starts to look like the eight from last game, and let the line lead.
  5. In the red zone, condense and hand to Walker up the middle before asking Mahomes for a fourth-down throw. The Jets tape finished with short touchdowns, including Johnson from the 6 with Tonga reported eligible.
  6. Defensively, open in Cover-2 on early downs and call a zone blitz on second-and-long, with Jones occupying and a second-level rusher free. Rally Bolton and the linebackers. Do not spend the takeaway on a blitz story the Jets tape does not need.
Last game

Five touchdowns after a 0-6 start

The previous result, with the details that matter to the next assignment.

W
New York Jets Week 9 · Sun, Nov 8 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 35–12

At Arrowhead, the Jets led 6–0 on field goals of 23 and 48 yards, the second of them at Q1 4:35, before Kansas City scored five touchdowns and won 35-12. New York’s other scores were a 49-yard field goal and a 54-yard field goal, both before halftime. Kansas City did not make a field goal.

The comeback was a drive story, not a trick. Net passing was 228. The rush total was 163 on 32 attempts. Possession sat at 28:54, so the Jets’ 31:06 did not come with control of the sticks. Smith finished 18-of-32 with a 70.4 rating and was sacked five times. Breece Hall had 18 touches and Braelon Allen 10, and the Jets still managed 106 rushing yards on 25 attempts. That is a front that bent and then ended drives.

Emmett Johnson’s 6-yard touchdown at Q4 3:35 came with Tonga reported eligible on that snap. Travis Kelce caught four passes for 72 yards. Rashee Rice had five catches, Xavier Worthy six. Those are the touches Bieniemy can actually pack.

The two giveaways are specific. At Q1 5:40, with Nourzad reported eligible, Fields went up the middle and lost the ball, recovered by Davis. At Q2 1:41, Bailey sacked Mahomes for a 10-yard loss and McDonald recovered. An earlier Bailey sack-fumble was recovered by Moore, so it stayed with Kansas City. Bolton forced Allen’s fumble at Q2 6:29 and Gilman recovered it. That is the Jets’ one turnover. Mahomes was also hit eight times and sacked twice. Kansas City recorded 7 quarterback hits. Harrison Butker missed a 51-yard field goal wide right; Sanders missed from 53, wide left. Jake Briningstool was flagged for offensive holding in the first quarter and J. Canady for offensive holding in the third. Those flags are on the sheet. They are not listed as wiped plays.

After halftime the scoreboard belonged to Kansas City: a 1-yard touchdown at Q3 10:20, Walker’s 2-yard score, and Johnson’s 6-yard score. New York’s late drives ended on downs, including Karlaftis’ sack of Smith at Q4 11:47. The tape says the run game and the red-zone finishes traveled from the second quarter into the fourth. It also says the protection and the ball security did not.

What held up

  • Walker’s 22 touches and a 163-yard team rush that included a 2-yard touchdown up the middle.
  • Mahomes at 23-of-31, 242 yards, two touchdown passes, rating 117.9, with Kelce at 4 catches for 72 yards.

What broke

  • Two turnovers, including a sack-fumble Mahomes lost, on a day he was also hit eight times and sacked twice.
  • Butker’s 51-yard miss wide right, and holding flags on Briningstool and Canady.
  • Kickoff-return ball security: Stiggers forced a fumble that Bassa recovered, so it did not become a turnover, but it was loose.

The run total is a team number

Kansas City rushed for 163 yards on 32 attempts. Walker’s 102 yards on 18 carries are the lead line inside that total, not a substitute for it. Pride’s backfield split of 27 carries for 155 yards is the same idea from the film desk.

Two giveaways sit on an otherwise clean count

The box lists two Kansas City turnovers and one for New York. Davis recovered the Fields fumble. McDonald recovered the Mahomes fumble Bailey forced. Gilman recovered the Allen fumble Bolton forced.
