Atlanta Falcons Week 10 · Sun, Nov 15 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 35–13

Kansas City beat the Atlanta Falcons 35-13 at Mercedes-Benz Stadium. The Chiefs posted 21 first downs, 122 rushing yards on 27 attempts, 339 net passing yards, 461 total yards, 13 drives, 28:36 of possession, and zero turnovers. Atlanta had 17 first downs, 100 rushing yards on 20 attempts, 270 net passing yards, 370 total yards, 15 drives, 31:24 of possession, 5-of-16 on third down, and two turnovers.

The scoring sequence belongs to the clubs on the ESPN lines. A 12-yard Kansas City touchdown with the clock at Q1 8:53 made it 7-0. Atlanta answered with a 28-yard field goal at Q2 7:12 to make it 7-3.

Mahomes was 22-of-35 with one sack and a 135.5 rating, and the leader line is 348 yards and 5 TD. The downfield completions that show up in the play-by-play are Downs for 21 and the 18-yard score, Allen for 39 and a score, Rashee Rice for 50, and Armstrong for 43 and a score. None of those throws is labeled play-action or play fake on the play-by-play, so the tape is shotgun and dropback, not a boot story. Atlanta recorded eight quarterback hits. That is the pressure count. It is not a sack total.

Johnson's 22 carries and 101 yards were the run identity: up the middle for 12, 4, 1, 4, 1, 2, and 3 on the listed snaps, plus right end for 11 and 2 and left end for 3. He also caught six passes, 28 touches in all. Justin Fields took a direct snap up the middle for no gain. Nate Carter had two rushes. Mahomes scrambled up the middle for 9 and left end for 6 on plays that stood. H. Nourzad reported as eligible on the Q4 6:38 Johnson carry up the middle for 5, and the eligible list also has Nourzad reports at Q2 14:53 and four other fourth-quarter times. Those are listed reports, not one repeated jumbo claim.

Steve Spagnuolo's unit did not win the clock — Atlanta had 31:24 and 15 drives to Kansas City's 28:36 and 13 — but it won the possession quality. Bijan Robinson had 16 rushes and four catches, 20 touches, including an 8-yard left end, a 17-yard up the middle, and a 5-yard up the middle. Drake London caught six passes, including 38 yards late in the second quarter. The two Atlanta turnovers are the ones ESPN lists: N. Bolton forced a fumble on a short middle completion to Kyle Pitts, and C. Conner recovered at the Atlanta 44; N. Williams intercepted a deep middle throw intended for Chris Blair and returned it 20 yards. Penix's aborted fumble in the second quarter was recovered by Penix. Johnson's fourth-quarter fumble, forced by A. Terrell, went out of bounds and is not a Kansas City turnover.

What held up

  • Mahomes at 22-of-35, 348 yards, 5 TD, and a 135.5 rating, with Downs, Allen, Rice, and Armstrong on the listed chunk plays
  • Johnson at 22 carries, 101 yards, and six catches in the start Arrowhead Pride called his first

What broke

  • 4-of-13 on third down, including a fourth-quarter turnover on downs on an incompletion to Cyrus Allen
  • Eight quarterback hits allowed, with Creed Humphrey's tripping penalty wiping a Mahomes scramble and Jaden Hicks flagged for holding
  • Atlanta held the ball for 31:24 and still hit London for 38 and Jahan Dotson for 37; C. Conner was also flagged for defensive pass interference

Explosives carried a stuck third-down offense

Kansas City was 4-of-13 on third down and still scored 35 points because the vertical catches showed up. That is a useful road win and a bad habit to pack for a 16.5-point home price.

The pocket count is eight hits and one sack

Mahomes took one sack and eight quarterback hits on 35 attempts. The hit number is the protection note, not a sack total.

Takeaways have names

C. Conner recovered the fumble N. Bolton forced. N. Williams intercepted Penix. Those are the credits on the play-by-play. Kansas City's own turnover count is zero.

Current state

7-2, the West tiebreaker, and the line that still leaks

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

7-2

Kansas City is 7-2 and, per Arrowhead Pride, back atop the AFC West because Denver was on a bye and the divisional tiebreaker now favors the Chiefs at 3-1 in the West. The Atlanta win was cleaner than the Jets game — zero turnovers and 461 yards against 391 yards and two giveaways in that 35-12 win over New York — and it still left third down and the pocket on the fix list.

What we need to work on

Third down before the explosives

4-of-13 will not be the plan against a team Kansas City is laying 16.5 to.

Hits, not a invented sack story

Arrowhead Addict's line piece matches the box: one sack on Mahomes and eight quarterback hits. Eric Bieniemy can steal rushers with Johnson's six-catch day and the screen game without pretending the pocket was clean.

Finish drives without the penalty tax

Hicks holding, Armstrong false start, Humphrey tripping, Chris Jones offside, and C. Conner's pass interference all wiped or extended plays. A 16.5 favorite does not need to donate those.

What to think about

How many Johnson touches if Walker is the veteran

Arrowhead Addict framed Johnson's Atlanta day as a luxury behind Kenneth Walker III. Twenty-eight touches in a start is a real role. Andy Reid still has to decide what that split looks like at home, not freeze the Atlanta usage chart.

Josh Simmons and the same protection question

Arrowhead Addict reports Simmons is eligible to return from injured reserve and that Reid has offered little clarity. Until there is a roster fact, the plan has to assume the current line and the eight-hit tape.

Looking ahead

Cardinals at Arrowhead on a 40.5 total

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Arizona Cardinals

The assignment is the Arizona Cardinals at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium on Sun Nov 22, 12:00 PM CT on CBS. Kansas City is a 16.5-point favorite on a 40.5 total, with ESPN FPI at 88.6 percent. The number is large. The total is not a 461-yard invitation. Play the favorite's game: own early downs, keep Mahomes off the eight-hit track, and make Arizona drive the length of the field.

How they match up

Kansas City brings a quarterback who just threw for 348 yards and 5 TD and a back who just handled 28 touches. Spagnuolo can play coverage-first and still rush Karlaftis, who Arrowhead Pride has on a tear since the bye. No Arizona skill names are in the supplied facts, so the plan is unit football: run the inside zone, stress man with mesh, take the deep middle when the shell softens, and screen the rush that showed up eight times in Atlanta.

Johnson on first down, not a 4-of-13 rerun

Twenty-two carries for 101 yards is the early-down answer.

Throw to the Atlanta completions, not a new mythology

Rice (5 for 82), Downs, Allen, and Armstrong are the verified targets. Use them on defined concepts. Do not relabel those throws as play-action.

Two-deep until Arizona proves it can drive

Atlanta still found London for 38 and Dotson for 37 while trailing. Cover-2 takes the cheap shot away and lets Bolton and the second level tackle a low-total game.

Pressure is a call, with the rush already on tape

Penix took three sacks and Kansas City recorded five quarterback hits. Karlaftis has the 1.5. Simulated pressure is available. It is a call for Arrowhead, separate from how the Atlanta takeaways happened.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Johnson and a Carter changeup. Win the first down before the shotgun menu.
  2. If Arizona stacks the box, mesh Rice and Travis Kelce with Johnson as the checkdown. Kelce returned in the Atlanta game and is a verified target.
  3. On second-and-short, throw four verticals: Downs and Xavier Worthy outside, Allen or Rice on the seam. Mahomes just completed those depths.
  4. On obvious rush downs, Johnson screen. Six catches in Atlanta is the outlet. Take the hit count down from eight.
  5. Defensively, start Cover-2 on early downs. Mix the zone blitz on second-and-medium once the shell is established, with Karlaftis and Ashton Gillotte as the rush pieces Pride already highlighted.

Always looking ahead

The Atlanta result is in the book at 35-13, and the desk turns to Arrowhead. Kansas City is 7-2, back atop the AFC West on tiebreakers, and DraftKings has the Chiefs laying 16.5 against the Arizona Cardinals on Sun Nov 22, 12:00 PM CT with a 40.5 total.
