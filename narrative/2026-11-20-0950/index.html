Current state

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

7-2

The live tape is Atlanta. The live question is whether Johnson, Downs, and a healthier third-down script can carry a 16.5 home favorite while Walker is out of practice and Kelce manages the ankle.

What we need to work on

Third-and-manageable before third-and-long

Johnson at 22 carries for 101 is the early-down answer.

Hit protection for a five-touchdown quarterback

Eight hits and one sack did not wreck the 135.5 rating. They are still the number to cut against Arizona, with Joshua Ezeudu's right-tackle case sitting in the building as a discussion, not a declared lineup change.

A real Worthy plan, not a snap-count apology

Reid defended the lack of production. The play-by-play shows incompletions to Worthy, and the touch list does not list a catch. Arizona week is the time to give him a vertical or a sit, on purpose, beside Downs and Rice.

What to think about

How many Kelce snaps on the 100th Arrowhead game

Pride expects him to play through the ankle, and he returned in Atlanta with two catches. Gray and Briningstool are the one-catch complements. The staff has to decide whether sentiment and availability are the same thing on a short week toward Buffalo.

Whether Justin Reid is a call or a distraction

Arrowhead Addict reports the former Chiefs safety asked the Saints for his release. Pride's roster snapshot listed about $3.1 million in cap space as of Wednesday evening. That is a conversation, not a signing, and it should not crowd the Arizona install.

Toub's returner decision in Downs' debut week

Toub said Downs is the punt returner right now after Nikko Remigio was waived. Four catches and an 18-yard touchdown already give Downs an offensive job. Adding returns is value only if it does not spend the same body Mahomes just found.

Looking ahead

Arizona at Arrowhead: cash the number without the explosives tax

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Arizona Cardinals

Host Arizona on Sun Nov 22, 12:00 PM CT at Arrowhead, CBS, as a 16.5-point favorite on a 40.5 total. FPI's 11.9 percent on Arizona is the upset path: turn a loud favorite into a third-down stall and a hit-filled pocket.

How they match up

Kansas City brings the more proven quarterback, a rookie back who just posted 28 touches, and a receiver group that just added Downs next to Rice, Kelce, Allen, and Worthy. Spagnuolo's leverage is the opposite of hero ball. Two deep, rally to the ball, and call a zone blitz on obvious passing downs so Arizona cannot live on the London- and Dotson-style explosives that still landed in Atlanta. Special teams matter because Downs is Toub's punt returner in the same week he becomes a four-catch offensive piece.

Win first and second down with Johnson

Feed the 22-carry, 101-yard back and keep Carter in the changeup. A 16.5 favorite that faces third-and-long is playing the underdog's game. Nourzad's eligible reports are a jumbo tool for specific snaps, including the five-yard inside run already on tape, not a personality for the whole offense.

Move Mahomes off the eight-hit track

Boot action and a Johnson screen are calls for Arizona, built to cut the contact that showed up once as a sack and eight times as a hit. Kelce, if the ankle lets him leak, is the easy answer. Rice on the cross is the explosive answer.

Put Worthy on a designated shot

Reid has already said the quiet Atlanta day is not the evaluation. One vertical beside Downs and Allen, or a sit against man, keeps the snap leader from becoming a decoy Arizona can ignore.

Rally the explosives, then heat the second level

Cover-2 takes away the unpaid deep shot. The zone blitz is a call on Arizona's obvious downs, with a lineman dropping and a second-level rusher coming. It is an install for this opponent, separate from how Williams' interception happened in Atlanta.

  1. Open in under-center inside zone to Johnson and make Arizona fit the run before any shot play.
  2. On the first second-and-short, boot Mahomes to the perimeter with Rice crossing and Kelce or Gray as the flat answer.
  3. Steal a third-and-medium with mesh: Downs and Rice crossing, Allen on the corner route, Johnson as the checkdown.
  4. Call four verticals once Arizona spins a single-high look, with Downs and Allen bending the seams and Worthy on an outside vertical.
  5. Screen Johnson on the first sign of a four-man heat that looks like the eight-hit Atlanta rush.
  6. On defense, open in Cover-2 and call the zone blitz on second-and-long and third-and-long, then tackle. Do not trade a 16.5 lead for a chunk play.

What the 35-13 Atlanta tape actually showed

The previous result, with the details that matter to the next assignment.

W
Atlanta Falcons Week 10 · Sun, Nov 15 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 35–13

At Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Kansas City beat Atlanta 35-13 with five touchdowns to Atlanta's one. Andrew Armstrong's 43-yard touchdown at Q4 4:18 closed it at 35-13.

The box is an explosives game, not a clock game. Kansas City had 21 first downs, 27 rushes for 122 yards, 339 net passing yards, 461 total yards, 13 drives, zero turnovers, and a 28:36 possession. Atlanta had 17 first downs, 20 rushes for 100 yards, 270 net passing yards, 370 total yards, 15 drives, two turnovers, and a 31:24 possession. Third down was 4-of-13 for Kansas City and 5-of-16 for Atlanta. Mahomes' 135.5 rating came with one sack and eight quarterback hits. Penix took three sacks, and Kansas City recorded five quarterback hits. George Karlaftis was the sack leader at 1.5.

Johnson was the sustainable piece: 22 carries, 101 rushing yards, six catches, 28 touches, and Pepsi Rookie of the Week honors. The listed rushes include a 12-yard gain up the middle on the opening drive and an 11-yard gain off right end in the second quarter. He also had a fumble forced by A. Terrell on an 18-yard left-tackle run, with the ball going out of bounds at the Kansas City 48, so it did not become a turnover. Nate Carter had two rushes. Justin Fields' direct snap up the middle gained nothing. Mahomes scrambled twice that counted, including nine yards up the middle and six yards off left end before Allen's score.

Downs caught a 21-yarder and the 18-yard score. Allen scored from 39. Armstrong scored from 43 on his one touch. Rashee Rice's line was five catches for 82 yards, including a 50-yarder to the Atlanta 25 in the fourth quarter. Travis Kelce had two catches and returned in the second quarter. Noah Gray and Jake Briningstool each had one catch. Worthy is on three incompletion lines in the play-by-play and is absent from the touch list. Reid's public defense of that production is the personnel note, not a made-up target total.

Atlanta's skill players earned the credit the score hides. Bijan Robinson had 20 touches, 16 rushes and four catches, including a 17-yard run up the middle. London had six catches, Pitts four, Dotson three. Penix aborted a fumble and recovered it himself in the second quarter, so that was not a takeaway. The takeaways that count are Bolton forcing the Pitts fumble that Conner recovered at the Atlanta 44, and Williams intercepting the deep middle throw for Blair and returning it 20 yards. Atlanta also failed on downs on an incomplete to Pitts. Kansas City failed on downs on an incomplete to Allen. Flags were a shared mess: holding on J. Hicks, a false start on Armstrong, tripping on Creed Humphrey, and a Chamarri Conner defensive pass interference that wiped a play. Hunter Nourzad was the eligible report on the six listed snaps, including the fourth-quarter rush where Johnson gained five up the middle.

What held up

  • Mahomes at 22-of-35, 348 yards, five touchdowns, 135.5, and zero Kansas City turnovers
  • Johnson's 22-carry, 101-yard, six-catch day inside a 122-yard team rushing total

What broke

  • 4-of-13 on third down across 13 drives and a 28:36 possession
  • Eight quarterback hits and one sack on Mahomes
  • Worthy's snap volume without a catch on the touch list, and Kelce limited to two catches while playing through the ankle
  • Johnson's Terrell-forced fumble out of bounds, plus the Humphrey tripping flag and the Conner pass-interference flag

Explosives carried a stuck third-down offense

Five touchdown passes and 461 yards can hide 4-of-13. Arizona will not need a perfect plan if Kansas City keeps living on 18-, 39-, 43-, and 50-yard shots after failed early downs. The fix is Johnson on schedule, then Mahomes on schedule.

The pocket was noisy, not collapsed

One sack and eight quarterback hits is the Atlanta rush line on Mahomes. That is enough contact to steal timing from Kelce's ankle and from a debutant in Downs. Screens and a moved pocket are the answers, not a speech about toughness.

Spagnuolo finished; he did not erase the deep ball

London, Dotson, and Pitts still found chunk yardage. Cover-2 and a called zone blitz are the Arizona install, aimed at that explosive tax.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

The assignment is Arizona at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium on Sun Nov 22, 12:00 PM CT on CBS, with DraftKings laying Kansas City 16.5 on a 40.5 total and ESPN FPI giving the Chiefs an 88.0 percent chance. That is a heavy home price for a 7-2 team coming off a 35-13 win at Atlanta, and it is also a price that can flatter a third-down offense that went 4-of-13 while Patrick Mahomes absorbed eight quarterback hits and one sack.

Mahomes was 22-of-35 for 348 yards and five touchdowns, Kansas City finished with 461 yards and zero turnovers, and Emmett Johnson carried 22 times for 101 yards with six catches on a 28-touch day.

Steve Spagnuolo's group got the result it wanted — Atlanta scored one touchdown and two field goals, Michael Penix Jr. Drake London's 38-yard catch, Jahan Dotson's 37-yarder, and Kyle Pitts' 28-yarder all came after Kansas City had control. Nick Bolton forced the Pitts fumble that Chamarri Conner recovered, and N. Williams intercepted Penix on a deep middle throw intended for Chris Blair. Those are finishes, not a reason to play Arizona loose.

Availability is the other half of the home plan. Arrowhead Pride's Thursday injury report has Ken Walker missing practice again, while Travis Kelce is expected to play through the ankle that has followed him since Week 6, on a Sunday that Pride notes is his 100th regular-season game at Arrowhead. Josh Downs, in his debut, is the new punt returner in Dave Toub's words and a four-catch piece of the passing game, including the 18-yard touchdown. Xavier Worthy led the snap count in Atlanta and did not show up on the touch list; Reid has already defended the quiet day. That is a usage question for Arizona, not a eulogy.

After this one the slate tightens: Thu Nov 26, 7:20 PM CT at Buffalo on NBC, then Thu Dec 3, 7:15 PM CT at the Rams on Prime Video. Arrowhead Addict framed the Cardinals game as the tilt before that gauntlet. A 16.5 number is not a bye. It is a chance to bank a win while installing the third-down answers Buffalo will not gift.
