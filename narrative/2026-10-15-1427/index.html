Last game

What the tape said

A real review of the most recent result — not a box-score dump, and not a skip-ahead.

W
Las Vegas Raiders Week 4 · Sun, Oct 4 · 3:25 PM CT
KC 30–27

At Allegiant Stadium, Las Vegas scored first with 8:10 left in the first quarter on a 27-yard touchdown, and Kansas City answered with 2:23 left in the quarter on a 2-yard touchdown to tie it 7-7. The Raiders led 13-10 at halftime. Kansas City then stacked a fourth-quarter surge — an 8-yard touchdown, a 2-yard touchdown, and Kenneth Walker III’s 22-yard touchdown — and held on after a 20-yard Las Vegas touchdown with 1:46 left.

The box is a split personality. Kansas City finished with 18 first downs, 399 total yards, a 5-of-13 third-down line, 12 drives, and 24:18 of possession. Las Vegas had 28 first downs, 437 yards, a 5-of-14 third-down line, 12 drives, and 35:42. Cousins was not sacked. The rush defense still held Las Vegas to 72 yards on 26 carries, with Ashton Jeanty at 21 touches and Mike Washington Jr. at 10.

Walker was the offense. Twenty-two carries, 177 yards, two touchdowns, plus one catch, for 23 touches. The listed rushes include an 11-yard left-end gain, a 5-yard carry to the 2, and the 22-yard right-end touchdown after the kickoff fumble. Mahomes added two rushes, including a 1-yard scramble on the snap where K. Tonga reported eligible. Khyiris Tonga and Matt Araiza each had one rush; Araiza’s was a 12-yard right-end gain out of punt formation.

The passing line was efficient in spots and incomplete in volume. Mahomes was 15-of-30 for 225 yards and two touchdowns, with 2 sacks and a 97.2 rating. Net passing was 210. Xavier Worthy had the 41-yard catch to the Las Vegas 7 and finished with 4 catches. Thornton’s 42- and 55-yard catches were the explosives; he also had an incompletion with Maxx Crosby in the picture and another incompletion in the deep middle. Travis Kelce and Noah Gray each had 2 catches. Las Vegas hit Mahomes 8 times. That is pressure, not a sack parade, and it is not a reason to romanticize 15 completions.

Special teams and the secondary closed it. Harrison Butker’s 59-yard kickoff was returned by Dareke Young, forced by J. Bassa, and recovered by J. Cochrane at the Las Vegas 22. Walker scored on the next snap, right end, 22 yards. Cousins then found Brock Bowers for a 20-yard touchdown and converted the two-point try to Cody White. X. Nwankpa intercepted the short throw intended for Michael Mayer. George Karlaftis was in on that play. Credit the interceptor, not a blitz label.

What held up

  • Walker’s 23-touch day and the 189-yard team rush total
  • Zero turnovers and a fourth-quarter sequence that turned 17-19 into 30-19
  • Ten quarterback hits and a run defense that held Las Vegas to 72 yards
  • Araiza’s punt-formation gain and the AFC special-teams weekly honor for five punts inside the 20

What broke

  • Possession at 24:18 and third down at 5-of-13
  • Two sacks and 8 hits on Mahomes on a 30-attempt day.
  • Zero sacks despite 10 hits.
  • Late flags on Felix Anudike-Uzomah for roughing the passer and L. Sneed for defensive holding, plus earlier N. Williams unnecessary roughness and defensive holding

The run game carried the scoreboard

Walker’s 177 yards and two scores, inside a 189-yard team rushing line on 26 attempts, produced points while the passing game stayed at 15 completions. Bieniemy can live with that mix if third down improves.

The clock belonged to Las Vegas

Thirty-five minutes and 42 seconds, 28 first downs, and 365 net passing yards is a real Raiders afternoon even in a loss. Cousins at 31-of-52 with zero sacks and an 85.8 rating is the warning for any quarterback who can stretch the field.

Takeaways had names

J. Bassa forced the kickoff fumble and J. Cochrane recovered it. X. Nwankpa intercepted Cousins. Kansas City’s turnover count was zero. Those are the facts, not a scheme slogan.

Current state

Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

4-0

Four wins, an early bye, and a top-of-the-AFC seat are real. So is the personnel dent: Thornton is out for the foreseeable future, Rice did not practice Wednesday, and the offense Mahomes wants to take to the next level just lost its 111-yard vertical piece from Allegiant.

What we need to work on

Stay on the field on third down

Five conversions in 13 tries and 24:18 of possession cannot be the weekly diet against a quarterback who can match Cousins’ volume. Early-down runs have to create third-and-manageable, not third-and-hope.

Replace Thornton’s vertical stress without inventing a clone

Worthy’s 41-yard catch showed the deep middle is still on the sheet. Kelce and Gray are the trust throws. Mahomes is already testing young wideouts; the install has to be specific, not a wish.

Turn hits into disruption without the late flags

Ten hits and zero sacks is unfinished work. Anudike-Uzomah’s roughing flag and L. Sneed’s holding flag wiped plays in the final minutes. Spagnuolo can heat Herbert and still keep the down-and-distance.

What to think about

How many snaps does Simmons actually take?

Reid said the left tackle will practice for the first time since camp. Practice is not a start. Kahlil Benson’s role can shift if Simmons is in, and the staff should not pretend the edge plan is settled until the week’s report says so.

Is 15-of-30 the sustainable Mahomes, or the Walker game?

Nine touchdowns and two interceptions through four games is the season line Arrowhead Pride cited. Allegiant was 225 yards, two scores, two sacks, and a 97.2 rating. The next step is completion volume once defenses sit on Walker.

Linebacker rotation if Cooper McDonald is back

Arrowhead Addict notes a possible return still leaves bigger linebacker questions. Nick Bolton and Drue Tranquill were all over the Allegiant tackle sheet. Depth is a plan, not a press-conference name.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Los Angeles Chargers

Divisional home game, CBS, Sun Oct 18, 3:25 PM CT, Chiefs -8.5. The assignment is to keep the 189-yard run identity, give Mahomes answers without Thornton, and make Herbert throw into a two-deep shell instead of a 35-minute track meet.

How they match up

Kansas City’s current edge is downhill rushing and a defense that can rally to the run, as it did against 72 Raiders rushing yards. The stress point is the pass-game ceiling without Thornton and with Rice not practicing, plus a pass rush that hit Cousins 10 times and sacked him zero times. Herbert is the opponent’s lever: if the Chargers possess the ball the way Las Vegas did, a 42.5 total and a 73.9 percent FPI tag will not feel like a blowout price.

Feed Walker before the scoreboard requires hero throws

Inside zone on early downs keeps Mahomes out of third-and-long, where he took a share of the 8 hits. The Chargers will sell out to the back if Kansas City tips it; mix the screen and the mesh so the fit cannot be static.

Two-deep, then rally

Cover-2 takes the cheap shot away from a quarterback who can live on intermediate volume the way Cousins did to Bowers, Mayer, and Tucker. Spagnuolo’s zone blitz is a call to change the picture for Herbert.

Protect the young catch tree

Worthy, Kelce, Gray, and whoever wins the next outside snaps have to be the answers. Do not ask a practice-squad reunion story to be the game plan unless the roster move actually happens.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Walker and a Tonga eligible report only on the snaps that actually use it — the Allegiant sheet had two different Kansas City eligible looks, not one repeated jumbo.
  2. On second-and-medium, mesh Kelce and a young wideout with Worthy as the sit or corner tag so Mahomes has a defined read if the rush shows eight-hit energy again.
  3. Steal a possession with a spacing screen to Walker or a back in the flat when the Chargers rush past the pocket.
  4. One four-verticals shot to Worthy in the deep middle once the run fit compresses, not as a Thornton replacement myth.
  5. Defense: Cover-2 on standard downs, zone blitz on obvious passing downs, with the rule that a hit is not a celebration if the flag follows.
  6. Special teams: trust Araiza’s field-position habit and Butker’s operation; the Allegiant swing started with a kickoff, not a gadget.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

Andy Reid’s club is 4-0 and coming off the Week 5 bye with an offense Arrowhead Pride is calling elite again, and the tape from Allegiant Stadium explains both the praise and the caution. Kenneth Walker III carried 22 times for 177 yards and two touchdowns, Patrick Mahomes went 15-of-30 for 225 yards and two scores, and Kansas City still trailed for long stretches while Las Vegas held the ball for 35:42.

The ground game is the identity. Twenty-six rushes produced 189 yards and Walker finished with 23 touches. That is how a 5-of-13 third-down afternoon and 18 first downs still became 30 points and zero turnovers. Eric Bieniemy does not need a nostalgia speech. He needs that downhill menu to travel when the vertical piece is missing.

Tyquan Thornton’s 5 catches for 111 yards and two touchdowns were the air complement, including a 42-yard catch to the Las Vegas 5 and a 55-yard catch that ended with him injured. Arrowhead Pride reports he dislocated his ankle and is out for the foreseeable future. Mahomes has been testing the young wideouts in his place, and Rashee Rice did not practice on the Wednesday report.

Steve Spagnuolo’s group recorded 10 quarterback hits and zero sacks on Kirk Cousins, who still went 31-of-52 with 365 net passing yards. The takeaways that mattered were a kickoff fumble forced by J. Bassa and recovered by J. Cochrane, then an interception by X. Nwankpa. That is the defensive homework before Justin Herbert walks into Arrowhead: affect the quarterback without handing him the clock.

Josh Simmons is back on the practice field for the first time this season, per Andy Reid. That is the protection variable for Sun Oct 18, 3:25 PM CT. Seattle is the week after, on Sun Oct 25, 7:20 PM CT. This edition is about the Chargers, not a parade.

The X’s & O’s

Concepts drawn up on the board — what it is, why it works, and the coaching point.

Offense

Walker downhill on first down

Situation First-and-10 at home against the Chargers, before the scoreboard forces 15-of-30 heroics

Walker’s 22-carry, 177-yard, two-score tape is the identity Bieniemy should re-run while Thornton is out and Rice is not practicing.

Coaching point Zone steps, one cut, and a hat on the linebacker. Walker does not need a invented gap name. He needs the same early-down trust that produced 189 team rushing yards.

Offense

Mesh for the thinner room

Situation Second-and-6 to second-and-8 when Los Angeles sits on Walker

Kelce and Gray were the two-catch trust targets at Allegiant, and Mahomes is already testing young wideouts with Thornton gone.

Coaching point Rub the crossers, sit Worthy if the defender chases, and give Mahomes the checkdown before the 8-hit rush arrives. Defined reads beat a scramble drill.

Offense

Screen to steal a possession

Situation After a Chargers pressure look, or on second-and-long following a modest Walker gain

Mahomes took 2 sacks and 8 hits on 30 attempts. A screen lets the line lead and keeps the 24:18 possession problem from repeating.

Coaching point Walker or the back in the flat, linemen to space, no hero throw. This is how you punish an aggressive rush without asking a young wideout to win a 50-50.

Offense

One vertical stress to Worthy

Situation After two successful inside-zone fits compress the Chargers safeties

Worthy already won a 41-yard deep-middle catch to the Las Vegas 7. That is the available shot with Thornton on the shelf.

Coaching point Seams bend to the hash, outsides run past the corners, and Mahomes throws it on time. Do not label it play-action. The run fake is not required for the concept to stress Cover 3.

Defense

Two-deep against Herbert’s volume

Situation Standard downs when Los Angeles wants the 12-drive, long-clock game Las Vegas played

Cousins produced 365 net passing yards and 28 first downs without a sack. Cover-2 is how Spagnuolo takes the shot away and rallies to the tackle.

Coaching point Two deep, five under. Corners sink, linebackers fit the intermediate cross, and the rush still has to be disciplined after Anudike-Uzomah’s late roughing flag.

Defense

Show heat, drop a lineman

Situation Obvious passing downs against Herbert, especially third-and-medium

Ten hits and zero sacks is the unfinished rush. A simulated pressure is a call to change Herbert’s picture, not a receipt for Nwankpa’s interception.

Coaching point Show heat, drop a defensive lineman, rush a second-level player, and rally. Karlaftis and the edge have to win without a named blitz the Allegiant sheet does not give us as a template.
