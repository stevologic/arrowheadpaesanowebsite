Last game

What the tape said

A real review of the most recent result — not a box-score dump, and not a skip-ahead.

W
Las Vegas Raiders Week 4 · Sun, Oct 4 · 3:25 PM CT
KC 30–27

Kansas City answered with a 2-yard touchdown, a 28-yard field goal, then a fourth-quarter burst — an 8-yard score, a 2-yard score, and Kenneth Walker III’s 22-yard touchdown — to make it 30–19 before Brock Bowers’ 20-yard touchdown and a two-point play set the final at 30–27.

The box is a split personality. Kansas City ran for 189 yards on 26 carries and passed for 210 net, 399 total, 18 first downs, 5-of-13 on third down, 12 drives, 24:18 with the ball, no turnovers. Las Vegas ran for 72 yards on 26 carries, passed for 365 net, 437 total, 28 first downs, 5-of-14 on third down, 12 drives, 35:42 with the ball, and two turnovers. That is a defense that leaked yards and first downs and still found the two possessions that decided it: J. Bassa forced Dareke Young’s fumble on the kickoff, J. Cochrane recovered at the Las Vegas 22, and Walker walked in from the right end; later Xavier Nwankpa intercepted Cousins short left intended for Michael Mayer.

Walker was downhill identity, not a cameo. Twenty-two carries, one catch, 23 touches, 177 yards, two rushing scores. The complementary shot was Thornton: deep left for 42 yards in the second quarter, then deep left for 55 in the fourth before the ankle. Xavier Worthy’s 41-yard catch in the first quarter set up the tying score. Mahomes did not dink the Raiders into submission — 15-of-30 with two sacks and eight hits — but the completions that landed were explosives, and the two rushing scores plus two Thornton touchdowns were enough.

Steve Spagnuolo’s side lived in a long game. Ashton Jeanty handled 21 touches (15 rushes, six catches) and still the Raiders offense finished with 72 rushing yards as a team; Mike Washington Jr. added 10 touches. Michael Mayer caught eight, Bowers six, Tre Tucker four. Cousins was hit 10 times and never sacked, which is the warning clip for Justin Herbert. Nick Williams drew unnecessary roughness and defensive holding, F. Anudike-Uzomah drew roughing the passer, L. Sneed drew defensive holding, and the flag parade kept Las Vegas in a game it was losing on the ground. Matt Araiza’s 12-yard run out of punt formation was the early field-position swing; Khyiris Tonga reported eligible on separate first-quarter snaps, once with H. Nourzad and once on a Mahomes scramble.

The fourth quarter was the whole season in miniature. Kansas City trailed 17–19 after Las Vegas field goals of 44 and 48 yards, then scored twice in 19 seconds of game clock — Thornton’s 55-yarder plus a 2-yard touchdown, the Bassa-Cochrane takeaway, Walker’s 22-yarder — and still had to survive Bowers from 20 yards and a two-point play. Mansoor Delane led the defense with 10 tackles. It was a 30-point afternoon that never became comfortable, and the Chargers will see every snap of the 35:42 they were on the field.

What held up

  • Kenneth Walker III’s 22-carry, 177-yard, two-touchdown, 23-touch day as the offense’s spine
  • Tyquan Thornton’s five catches, 111 yards, and two scores before the ankle
  • Zero Kansas City turnovers against two Las Vegas giveaways (Bassa/Cochrane fumble, Nwankpa interception)
  • 189 team rushing yards on 26 attempts versus 72 allowed
  • Chunk shots to Worthy (41) and Thornton (42, 55) that flipped field position

What broke

  • 15-of-30 passing and 5-of-13 on third down
  • 24:18 of possession against 35:42 and 28 first downs allowed
  • Ten hits on Cousins without a sack
  • The penalty list: N. Williams twice, P. Woods, N. Remigio, F. Anudike-Uzomah, L. Sneed
  • Losing Rashee Rice (hamstring) and Tyquan Thornton (dislocated ankle) from the starting receiver group

Walker is the live identity

Twenty-two carries, 177 yards, two rushing touchdowns, 23 touches. When the receiver room shrank, Bieniemy did not hunt a new passing personality — he handed the game to Walker and let Mahomes take shots off that gravity.

Explosives covered a 15-of-30 day

Mahomes’ 225 yards sat on a handful of chunk plays: Worthy for 41, Thornton for 42 and 55. That is not a volume passing tape. It is a shot-play tape that will need 12 personnel and the run game if Rice and Thornton are limited.

Yards allowed, takeaways collected

Las Vegas had 28 first downs, 437 yards, and 35:42. Kansas City had the Bassa force, the Cochrane recovery, and the Nwankpa interception. Spagnuolo can live with ugly yardage for one game. He cannot live with it against Herbert if the takeaways dry up.

The line conversation is now public

Mike Caliendo started at left guard over Kingsley Suamataia, Mahomes took two sacks and eight hits, and Reid would not explain the swap. That is a bye-week problem with a pass rusher on the other sideline in Week 6.

Current state

Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

4-0

Kansas City is 4-0, AFC West control is live, and the roster is not the same one that kicked off at Allegiant. The bye before the Chargers is for the receiver room, the left-guard answer, and a defense that has now won two straight while losing the clock.

What we need to work on

Rebuild the pass-game math

Rice exited with a hamstring and Thornton with a dislocated ankle. Worthy (four catches), Kelce (two), Gray (two), and whoever inherits Thornton’s vertical must cover 15-of-30 efficiency that already needed explosives to beat Las Vegas.

Settle left guard

Caliendo started; Suamataia did not. Mahomes took two sacks and eight hits. Reid’s “between Kingsley and I” line does not travel against a Chargers pass rush that will test the same edge.

Finish pressures

Kansas City recorded 10 hits on Cousins and did not sack him. Herbert will make that miss louder unless Karlaftis and the second level turn hits into disruptions on third down.

Stop the free first downs

Unnecessary roughness, defensive holding, illegal formation, an invalid fair catch, and roughing the passer kept Las Vegas on schedule. A 5-of-13 third-down offense cannot donate extra downs.

What to think about

How much 12 personnel is the new base?

Kelce and Gray are the cleanest way to replace lost wideout snaps without emptying the backfield and daring a pass rush that already hit Mahomes eight times.

Is the Caliendo start a week or a change?

Reid will not discuss Suamataia. The staff has one bye to decide whether the protection issue is a player or a plan before the Chargers see the same left side.

What does 4-0 actually price?

Polymarket still has Kansas City at 8.8 percent to win the Super Bowl and 18.5 percent to win the AFC. That is respect for the record, not a bye-week crown, after a 30–27 road win that lost two receivers and the possession fight.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Los Angeles Chargers

Week 6 at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium is the first true 4-0 stress test: a division opponent, a thinned wideout room, and a quarterback in Justin Herbert who will attack the same coverage space Cousins just occupied for 365 net yards.

How they match up

Kansas City wants to be a Walker run team that takes shots; Los Angeles wants to be a Herbert dropback team that wins on early downs. The leverage is inverted from Allegiant: the Chiefs have the home crowd and the bye, the Chargers have the healthier skill picture against a Kansas City group missing its two most productive wideouts from Week 4. Spagnuolo has to take away the deep ball he just gave up; Bieniemy has to keep Walker in the 20-carry range so Mahomes is not asked to go 15-of-30 again without Thornton.

Make Walker the first answer

Twenty-three touches just produced 177 yards. If Los Angeles loads the box, that is the invite for play-action boots and spacing screens. If they sit back, Walker runs the game and the Chargers cannot drop eight on Worthy and Kelce.

Move Mahomes off the launch point

Two sacks and eight hits are the protection tape. Boot, sprint-out, and screens keep Caliendo and Kahlil Benson from a pure dropback fight on every early down.

Cover-2 first, simulated pressure second

Cousins’ 365 net yards and 28 first downs are the warning. Two-deep takes away Herbert’s shots; the zone blitz is the third-and-long changeup, not a promise that a blitz will create a takeaway.

12 personnel as the WR replacement

Kelce and Gray let Kansas City stay in run looks without asking a short-handed wideout group to win empty-set downs against a pass rush.

  1. Open in under-center inside zone to Walker and force Los Angeles to declare box count
  2. Script play-action boots on early downs so Mahomes is not a stationary target after eight hits
  3. Keep 12 personnel (Kelce-Gray) as the base changeup for lost Rice/Thornton snaps
  4. Use spacing screens to Walker as the pressure release on obvious pass downs
  5. Start Herbert in Cover-2, then mix the zone blitz on third-and-long
  6. Clean up the flags — no donated first downs after the Allegiant penalty list

The story so far

Always looking ahead

Four-and-oh is the live record, and it is louder than last year’s 6-11 tape, but the Allegiant film is not a coronation. Kansas City beat Las Vegas 30–27 with 399 yards, 189 on the ground, and zero turnovers, then walked out with a thinner wideout group, a left-guard debate Andy Reid will not explain, and a defense that won the takeaway column while losing the clock 24:18 to 35:42. The next Sunday is the Chargers at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium, and the story is whether Eric Bieniemy’s run identity can keep carrying Patrick Mahomes while Steve Spagnuolo answers 365 net passing yards allowed.

Kenneth Walker III was the entire offensive personality: 22 carries, 177 yards, two rushing touchdowns, 23 touches. Tyquan Thornton matched him as the vertical answer — five catches, 111 yards, two scores — until the 55-yard catch in the fourth quarter ended with a dislocated ankle and a cart. Rashee Rice had already left with a hamstring on the second possession. That is the price of 4-0, and it is the tape Los Angeles will put on the projector.

Patrick Mahomes finished 15-of-30 for 225 yards and two touchdowns, took two sacks and eight hits, and still found the shots that flipped the fourth quarter. The Chiefs did not win a possession fight. They won a sudden-strike fight: an 8-yard touchdown to open the fourth, a 2-yard score after Thornton’s 55-yarder, then Walker’s 22-yard touchdown off a kickoff fumble that J. Bassa forced and J. Cochrane recovered. Kirk Cousins went 31-of-52, was not sacked, and still lost because Xavier Nwankpa closed it with an interception.

The bye is the first real install week of the live 4-0 season. Mike Caliendo started at left guard over Kingsley Suamataia, and Reid called that “between Kingsley and I.” The Chargers do not care about the privacy of that conversation. They care whether Creed Humphrey’s left side holds, whether Travis Kelce and Noah Gray can replace lost wideout snaps in 12 personnel, and whether Spagnuolo can take away explosives after Las Vegas posted 28 first downs and 437 yards. ESPN FPI at 72.0 percent is a home-favorite number, not a bye-week nap.

Concepts drawn up on the board — what it is, why it works, and the coaching point.

Offense

Under-center inside zone

Situation 1st-and-10, opening script vs. Los Angeles, Walker in the backfield

Walker just posted 177 on 22 carries; make the Chargers defend the downhill run so they cannot sit on a thinned wideout group.

Coaching point Humphrey and Caliendo zone-step together, Walker one-cuts, and Mahomes does not need to hunt 15-of-30 volume if the first down is honest.

Offense

Play-action boot off Walker

Situation 2nd-and-6 after a Walker inside-zone look, early series vs. Chargers

Mahomes took eight hits and two sacks at Allegiant; boot him off the launch point and give Benson and Caliendo an easier set.

Coaching point Sell Walker, move the pocket to Mahomes’ right, Kelce hinge, Worthy over the top — easy read, no stationary drop.

Offense

Four verticals to Worthy

Situation 2nd-and-medium vs. Chargers Cover-3, 11 personnel with Thornton limited

Thornton’s 111 yards came on verticals; Worthy has to stress the deep middle so Los Angeles safeties cannot squat on Kelce.

Coaching point Seams bend to the hash, Worthy past the corner, Mahomes hits the hole or lives with the incompletion — do not force the 15-of-30 contested ball.

Offense

Walker spacing screen

Situation 3rd-and-5 to 3rd-and-7, obvious pass down vs. Chargers pin-the-ears rush

Eight hits on Mahomes is the tell; get Walker the ball in space and make the Chargers rush pay for leaving the second level.

Coaching point Line leads, Walker catches on the move, Worthy and Gray wall the first wave — this is the pressure release, not a junk play.

Defense

Spagnuolo Cover-2 shell

Situation 1st-and-10 vs. Herbert, first two series at Arrowhead

Cousins just posted 365 net passing yards and 28 first downs; two-deep vs. Herbert takes away the shot and forces the rally tackle.

Coaching point Delane and the corners squat the five-under, safeties split the deep halves, Karlaftis rushes with contain — no free vertical from the hash.

Defense

Simulated pressure / zone blitz

Situation 3rd-and-7 or longer vs. Herbert, second-half adjustment

Call it to change Herbert’s launch point after 10 hits and no sack on Cousins — a pressure look, not a takeaway credited to Las Vegas.

Coaching point Show heat, drop a lineman, rush a second-level player, and play the hole behind it. Spagnuolo’s signature is the disguise, not a promised sack.
