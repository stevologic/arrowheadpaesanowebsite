Walker, Kelce, and a thin Wednesday

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

Third down after 4-of-13

What Downs is in the rotation

The fourth-and-fifth trade is already on the field: four catches, including an 18-yard touchdown and a 21-yard gain. Kelce's public endorsement, per Arrowhead Addict, is a locker-room signal. The staff still has to decide his every-down share if Kelce's snaps drop.

Whether the No. 1 seed talk changes Sunday

Arrowhead Pride posed the No. 1-seed question with Kansas City in that group. A 15.5 home price does not add a win by itself. A sloppy third-down day at home would.

Looking ahead

Cardinals at Arrowhead on a 15.5 number

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Arizona Cardinals

Host Arizona on Sun Nov 22, 12:00 PM CT on CBS as a 15.5-point favorite on a 40.5 total. ESPN FPI has Kansas City at 88.3 percent.

How they match up

Kansas City's leverage is Mahomes at 135.5 with Downs, Rice, Allen, and Armstrong all producing explosives in Atlanta, plus Johnson as a 28-touch back if Walker sits. Arizona's leverage is the total: 40.5 says the market does not expect another 35-point track meet, and a club that just went 4-of-13 on third down can make a large spread sweaty if drives stall. Spagnuolo gets a defense that posted five quarterback hits and faced 100 rushing yards from a 20-touch back. Bolton's Wednesday limitation is the personnel wrinkle on that side.

Early downs to Johnson, not to hope

If Walker cannot go, feed the back who just had 22 carries and 101 yards. Inside zone and a screen keep Mahomes off eight-hit volume and give third down a chance to climb off 4-of-13.

Defined throws for Downs and Rice

Downs already has the 18-yard score and the 21-yard catch. Rice has the 50-yarder and 82 yards on five grabs. Mesh and verticals are the answers if Arizona sits in man or two-high. Do not wait on Kelce to be the chain-mover.

Cap explosives, then rush on your terms

Atlanta still hit throws of 38, 37, and 28. Cover-2 is the shell. The zone blitz is a third-down call if Bolton cannot play every snap, with Karlaftis as the rush anchor from a 1.5-sack game.

Win, then stop

A 15.5 price on a 40.5 total is not a mandate to play Walker or to stack Kelce snaps in a blowout script. Reid's caution on the groin is the correct tie-breaker.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Johnson and a Carter change-of-pace carry before any trick snap.
  2. On second-and-medium, mesh Rice and Downs with Noah Gray or Jake Briningstool as the sit if Kelce is on a pitch count.
  3. Steal one four-verticals shot to Allen or Armstrong once the safeties squeeze the seams. Rice is the middle alert off the 50-yard catch.
  4. Call a Johnson screen after any sequence that looks like the eight-hit Atlanta rush picture.
  5. Defense: Cover-2 on early downs, zone blitz on third-and-medium, with a dropped lineman replacing a second-level rusher if Bolton is limited.
  6. If the lead reaches two scores, Nourzad can report eligible in the run game the way he did on six Atlanta snaps. Do not chase a fifth touchdown throw with Kelce.

Atlanta tape behind a 35-13 road win

The previous result, with the details that matter to the next assignment.

W
Atlanta Falcons Week 10 · Sun, Nov 15 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 35–13

Kansas City beat Atlanta 35-13 at Mercedes-Benz Stadium, scoring 6:07 into the game on a 12-yard touchdown for a 7-0 lead. Mahomes threw five touchdowns on a 22-of-35, 348-yard, 135.5 day. The Chiefs had 461 yards, zero turnovers, and went 4-of-13 on third down while Atlanta held a 31:24 to 28:36 possession edge.

Emmett Johnson was the run game with Walker inactive: 22 carries, 101 yards, six catches, 28 touches, inside a team line of 27 attempts for 122 yards. Nate Carter had two carries. Mahomes scrambled twice and Justin Fields took one direct snap up the middle for no gain. Net passing was 339. Rice's 50-yard catch and the Downs, Allen, and Armstrong scores were the explosives. Kelce had two catches. Harrison Nourzad reported as eligible on the six listed snaps, including Johnson's 5-yard carry up the middle at Q4 6:38.

Steve Spagnuolo's group recorded five quarterback hits. Bijan Robinson had 20 touches, and Atlanta rushed 20 times for 100 yards inside 370 total yards, 17 first downs, and 5-of-16 on third down. A fourth-quarter Johnson fumble forced by A.J. Terrell went out of bounds and did not add to Kansas City's zero turnovers.

The lag is down-and-distance and protection, not explosives. Mahomes took one sack and eight quarterback hits on 35 attempts. Third down was 4-of-13. Atlanta still created explosives: Drake London for 38, Jahan Dotson for 37, Kyle Pitts for 28. Creed Humphrey's tripping penalty wiped a Mahomes scramble, and Conner's defensive pass interference extended an Atlanta drive. That is the film to carry forward, not a box-score victory lap.

  • Zero turnovers and 461 yards, with Johnson at 101 rushing yards

What broke

  • 4-of-13 on third down and 21 first downs against 31:24 of Atlanta possession
  • Eight quarterback hits on Mahomes with one sack taken
  • Explosives allowed to London, Dotson, and Pitts, plus a late Atlanta touchdown and field goal

Mahomes was 22-of-35 for 348 yards and five touchdowns. Downs, Allen, and Armstrong each caught a scoring throw of 18, 39, and 43 yards, and Rice added a 50-yard catch. Arrowhead Pride's deep-ball note matches the tape.

Johnson held the run game

With Walker out, Johnson took 22 of 27 team rushes and 101 of 122 rushing yards, plus six catches. The staff has a real carrier if Walker cannot work this week.

Third down and hits still sit on the sheet

Kansas City was 4-of-13 on third down, possessed the ball for 28:36, and allowed eight quarterback hits with one sack. Atlanta's 31:24 and 370 yards are the reminder that 35 points did not mean a clean complementary day.
