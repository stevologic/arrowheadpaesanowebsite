DraftKings posting Chiefs -5.5 on a 45.5 total and ESPN FPI at 72.6 percent is a favorite’s number, not a coronation — Polymarket’s 8.6 percent Super Bowl price is what you get when a 3-0 team wins by 14 while losing first downs, third downs, and nine minutes of football.

Last game

What the tape said

A real review of the most recent result — not a box-score dump, and not a skip-ahead.

W
Miami Dolphins Week 3 · Sun, Sep 27 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 24–10

Kansas City scored on its opening drive, answered Miami’s 3-yard touchdown, and left Hard Rock Stadium with a 24-10 win that never became a track meet. Mahomes finished 20-of-24 for 246 yards, two touchdowns, and one interception; Walker carried 18 times for 70 and a score; Rashee Rice caught 7 for 88. The Dolphins out-gained nobody — 329 yards to 334 — but they kept the ball for 34:21, converted half their third downs, and still lost because they turned it over twice and failed on downs twice.

The Chiefs’ offense was a completion machine, not a drive machine. Eighteen first downs and 3-of-7 on third down is how you post 246 passing yards and still lose the clock by nine minutes. Mahomes was decisive in the short and intermediate game — Rice as the volume target, the football out on time — and the one deep-middle shot intended for Rice was intercepted by J. Rodriguez with help over the top. That is the only time Kansas City asked the vertical shot to carry a drive, and Miami took it. Everything else was efficiency without occupancy.

The run game did not re-create the Indianapolis tape. Walker’s 18/70 is honest work and a rushing touchdown, but it is not a downhill identity when you also turn it over on downs at the Miami 12 with extra eligibility in the game. Eighty-eight team rush yards against a Dolphins front that was supposed to be the soft part of the field means Bieniemy spent the afternoon asking Mahomes to win on 24 dropbacks of quality instead of 35 dropbacks of necessity. That works until the other club stops punting the football away.

Spagnuolo’s defense won the score and lost the down. Nick Bolton’s 11 tackles are a tell: Kansas City was tackling because Miami was on the field. The Dolphins posted 19 first downs, 8-of-16 on third down, and 119 rushing yards, and they still scored 10 because Drue Tranquill blew up a Willis keeper that became a fumble Kansas City challenged, because Miami turned it over on downs in the second quarter and again in the fourth, and because Karlaftis intercepted Willis with Chris Jones in the picture. That is complementary football by takeaway, not by first-down defense. A division opponent will try to keep the 119 and lose the gifts.

The finishing sequence was Chiefs football and 2025 football stacked on top of each other. A 34-yard field goal made it 17-10, an 11-yard touchdown made it 24-10 after the Karlaftis interception, and Harrison Butker then missed a 50-yarder wide left. Kelce’s traffic catch put the game on ice, per Arrowhead Pride, which is the closer this offense still has. The missed kick did not matter. The 25:39 of possession will matter on Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT.

What held up

  • Mahomes’ 20-of-24, 246-yard, two-touchdown efficiency and a 119.8 passer rating in a true road environment
  • Explosive enough finishing: touchdowns of 10, 5, and 11 yards, plus Kelce’s traffic catch to ice it
  • Rice as the chain-mover (7 for 88) when the run game stalled
  • Two Miami turnovers — Willis fumble (Tranquill) and Karlaftis interception (Jones in the play) — plus two Dolphins failures on downs
  • Spagnuolo’s unit holding Miami to 10 points and 210 net passing yards despite losing the clock

What broke

  • Third-down offense at 3-of-7, which is how you post 334 yards and still sit for 34:21
  • The ground game: 88 team rushing yards, Walker stuffed on downs at the Miami 12 with Nourzad eligible
  • Mahomes’ deep-middle interception intended for Rice
  • First-down and possession fights (18-19 first downs, 25:39 of clock)
  • Run defense allowing 119 yards and 8-of-16 third downs to a Dolphins offense starting Malik Willis
  • Butker’s 50-yard miss wide left, a leftover scar on an otherwise closed game

Mahomes can win ugly if the ball comes out on time

Twenty of 24 is a rhythm start, not a volume start. When Kansas City stayed in defined throws, Miami could not cover enough of the field. The Rodriguez interception on the deep middle to Rice is the exception that proves the rule: do not hunt explosives when the easy game is already paying.

Takeaways covered a defense that was on the field too long

Bolton leading the team with 11 tackles is production and a warning. Miami’s 34:21 and 8-of-16 third-down day should have been 20 points. Willis’ fumble and the Karlaftis interception are why it was 10. Spagnuolo cannot budget two opponent giveaways every week in the AFC West.

The run game did not travel

Walker’s touchdown keeps him in the A-gap conversation, but 70 yards on 18 carries and a stuffed extra-eligibility run at the 12 is not the Indianapolis identity. If Las Vegas loads the box, Bieniemy needs answers that are not another 24-pass, 88-rush split.

Current state

Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

3-0

The Chiefs are 3-0 with a 31-10, 33-30, and 24-10 scoring trail that looks like a playoff club on Sunday and like 2025 on the cut-up. The AFC West already has a gap if the Chargers’ start is as grim as the local wire says, but this roster has not yet proven it can occupy the football for four quarters against a division front.

What we need to work on

First-down run game that actually moves the chains

Walker’s 18/70 in Miami is not a failed back; it is a failed complementary script. Las Vegas will dare Kansas City to live in obvious pass so Maxx Crosby can hunt. Inside zone has to win early downs on Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT or Mahomes will be asked to repeat 83 percent in a louder pocket.

Third-down offense (3-of-7 is the quiet crisis)

You cannot take a 3-of-7 day on the road in the division and assume the other club will go 8-of-16 and still score 10. Mesh, boot, and the Rice/Kelce two-man game have to become automatic before the Raiders pin this offense in third-and-long.

Occupy the ball — 25:39 will not survive the AFC West

Miami’s 34:21 was a blueprint, not a fluke. Spagnuolo can live with 19 first downs if the takeaways keep coming. He cannot live with it if Las Vegas finishes those drives. Winning first down on both sides of the ball is the entire week of practice.

Run defense and the second-level fit

Bolton’s 11 tackles mean the ball was getting to him. Against a division opponent that will run to keep Mahomes off the field, the edge has to set and the interior has to squeeze. The Willis keeper that became a fumble is the standard; the 119 yards are the problem.

What to think about

Is 3-0 the new identity or a cleaner 6-11?

Arrowhead Pride’s rapid reaction said last year’s Chiefs almost came out in Miami. The staff has to decide whether the Colts overtime explosion or the Dolphins possession loss is the real tape — because the Raiders will prepare for the latter.

How much Walker is enough when Mahomes is this accurate?

Twenty-of-24 tempts you to throw first. Crosby and a division front will punish that temptation. The debate is not Walker versus Mahomes; it is whether Bieniemy will keep calling the run after a stuffed extra-eligibility play at the 12.

What does 8.6 percent Super Bowl actually mean at 3-0?

Polymarket at 8.6 percent, ESPN FPI at 72.6 percent to beat Las Vegas, DraftKings at -5.5 / 45.5: the market likes Sunday’s favorite and still will not buy the long ticket. A division road game after losing the clock is exactly how that gap either closes or widens.

Secondary math after Chamarri Conner sat in Miami

Conner was officially ruled out against the Dolphins, with Josh Simmons, Diego Pounds, Jared Wiley, Jack Pyburn, and Tyreke Smith also down. There is no Week 4 report on this wire. If the safety room is still thin, Spagnuolo’s call sheet tilts toward two-deep and simulated pressure instead of man-to-man heroics.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Las Vegas Raiders

Division road game, Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT, CBS. Kansas City is a 5.5-point favorite on a 45.5 total with a 72.6 percent ESPN FPI nod — which is another way of saying the Raiders are live if they copy Miami’s occupancy plan and refuse to donate the football.

How they match up

Styles collide on the thing Miami already exposed: first down. Kansas City wants defined throws for Mahomes, Walker as a between-the-tackles hammer, and Spagnuolo creating negative plays with Chris Jones and George Karlaftis. Las Vegas, at home in the division, will try to win the clock, set Maxx Crosby loose on obvious pass, and make a 3-0 favorite play from behind the sticks. The leverage is simple: if the Chiefs run for more than 88 and stay out of third-and-long, Mahomes’ efficiency travels. If they don’t, Crosby becomes the game and 25:39 becomes the sequel.

Make Walker the first-down answer, not the footnote

Open in 12 personnel and force Las Vegas to tackle inside. Play-action boot only after the run fake is real. The extra-eligibility stuff at the Miami 12 cannot be the last memory this offensive line carries into a division trench.

Protect the edges so Mahomes can stay at 20-of-24

Crosby is the Raiders’ best player and the reason a 5.5-point road favorite can still lose. Chips, tight boots, and screens to Walker are how you keep Mahomes from having to be a hero. Josh Simmons was inactive in Miami — whoever is at tackle has to win the first two quarters without help arriving late.

Spagnuolo: two-deep early, simulated pressure later

Do not let Las Vegas dink its way to Miami’s 8-of-16. Cover-2 rallies Bolton to the ball; zone blitz with a Jones drop recreates the Karlaftis/Jones negative play without emptying the box against the run. Takeaways are the plan; 119 rush yards are the restriction.

Finish drives. Empty trips inside the 15 are how favorites become 5.5 instead of 10.

Kansas City already left points on the field with a turnover on downs at the 12 and a 50-yard miss. Kelce in traffic is still the closer. Use him before fourth down, not as a recovery throw.

  1. 1st-and-10, 12 personnel: under-center inside zone to Walker, tight end down, aim to start 2nd-and-5 instead of 2nd-and-9.
  2. 2nd-and-medium: play-action boot to Mahomes’ right, Kelce leak, Rice post-off — defined throw, no deep-middle hunt.
  3. 3rd-and-5: mesh with Rice as the primary rub winner and Walker as the checkdown; this is the 3-of-7 fix.
  4. Obvious pass / Crosby pin-the-ears: spacing screen to Walker, linemen in space, take the free yards Miami never gave you on the ground.
  5. 1st-and-10 defense: Cover-2 shell, Bolton and the WILL rally, corners squat — no shot over the top, no bounce to the edge.
  6. 3rd-and-7: Spagnuolo simulated pressure, Jones as the dropper or the looper, Karlaftis and a second-level rusher — hunt the Willis-style negative play without selling the run.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

Kansas City left Hard Rock Stadium unbeaten and unconvinced. The 24-10 final on Sun Sep 27, 12:00 PM CT was real — Patrick Mahomes was 20-of-24 for 246 yards and two scores, Kenneth Walker III punched in a rushing touchdown, and Steve Spagnuolo’s defense took the ball away twice — but Miami won the physics: 19 first downs to 18, 8-of-16 on third down to 3-of-7, 119 rush yards to 88, and 34:21 of possession to 25:39. That is the 2025 echo in a 2026 win, and it is the exact tape a division opponent will try to copy.

Arrowhead Pride called it what it looked like: last year’s Chiefs almost coming out, still good enough to grind a 24-10 result because Mahomes was ruthlessly efficient and the Dolphins handed over a Malik Willis fumble plus a George Karlaftis interception. Travis Kelce finished the afternoon with a highlight-reel touchdown in traffic. The problem is not the scoreboard. The problem is that Eric Bieniemy’s offense lived in a low-volume, high-completion box (334 total yards, 88 on the ground) and got stuffed on downs at the Miami 12 when Hunter Nourzad reported eligible and Walker was met in the hole.

Mahomes has already had to remind a young roster it cannot relax, which is the correct temperature for 3-0. Undefeated is a standing; it is not an identity. Identity is first down, third down, and the clock. Miami held all three and still scored 10 points. Las Vegas will not volunteer the same finishing errors.

Next is a division road game at the Raiders on Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT on CBS, with ESPN FPI at 72.6 percent and DraftKings at Chiefs -5.5 on a 45.5 total. That number assumes Kansas City is the cleaner team. The Miami film assumes the Raiders can win if they run it, convert, and keep Mahomes on the sideline. Week 6’s Chargers visit is already being framed as an AFC West separator after Los Angeles’ nightmare start; none of that equity cashes if this trip turns into a possession sequel.

Walker, one cut, no sequel at the 12

Walker must press the heels of the guards and take the A or B gap that opens — the extra-eligibility stuff at the 12 was indecision, not a scheme loss. Interior OL: stay on double teams a count longer than Miami; the second-level linebacker is the tackle that ended drives.

Offense

Sell the run, move Mahomes, take the easy one

Situation 2nd-and-5 or 2nd-and-6 after a Walker zone gain, plus splits, Raiders DE crashing

Mahomes was 20-of-24 in Miami when the play was defined and the pocket had an answer. Boot puts him on the perimeter against a Raiders rush that will pin its ears back as a 5.5-point home underdog. Kelce is the first read; Rice occupies the deep third so the deep-middle INT to Rodriguez does not get rerun.

Coaching point Reid/Bieniemy: the fake has to hold Crosby or the contain end. Mahomes hitch and throw — do not outrun the launching point and do not hunt the shot. Kelce: leak to 8 yards vs zone, run through the hook vs man. Rice: sell the post off so the safety cannot drive the boot.

Eric Bieniemy’s downhill identity is on the table

Steve Spagnuolo: 10 points is not the same as a stop
