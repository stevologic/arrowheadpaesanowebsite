Last game

What the tape said

A real review of the most recent result — not a box-score dump, and not a skip-ahead.

W
Miami Dolphins Week 3 · Sun, Sep 27 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 24–10

Kansas City went to Hard Rock Stadium and came home 3-0 with a 24-10 win. Mahomes was 20-of-24 for 246 yards, two touchdowns, and one interception with zero sacks and a 119.8 rating. Walker carried 18 times for 70 yards, added a 5-yard catch at Q2 12:58, and finished with 73 scrimmage yards and two touchdowns. Rice caught 7 for 88, and Bolton led the defense with 11 tackles. Miami outgained almost nobody (329 to 334) and still held the football 34:21 to Kansas City’s 25:39.

The opening score was a drive, not a trick. At Q1 14:55 Mahomes hit Kelce deep middle for 48 yards to the Miami 19. At Q1 12:54, 2:06 into the game, J. Moore reported in as eligible and Walker went up the middle for 10 yards and the touchdown. Kansas City 7-0. Miami answered with a 3-yard touchdown at Q1 3:27 to tie it 7-7, then Kansas City took a 14-7 lead on Walker’s 5-yard catch at Q2 12:58. From there the game became a possession argument Miami was winning on paper and losing on the scoreboard.

Mahomes’ 20-of-24 was the separator. He was not running for his life — zero sacks — but Miami recorded 5 QB hits on Mahomes. Kansas City recorded 1 QB hit. The ball still came out on time to Rice (7 for 88), Gray (the 24-yarder to the Miami 8 at Q4 13:47), Worthy, and Thornton. The miss that hurt was the Q2 2:00 interception: shotgun, deep middle for Rice, J. Rodriguez, 18-yard return. The red-zone stall that hurt was Q2 6:15, H. Nourzad eligible, Walker up the middle for no gain, turnover on downs at the Miami 12.

Steve Spagnuolo’s front did not get Willis on the ground — 20-of-36, zero sacks, 61.1 rating — and Ollie Gordon II worked 17 rushes plus 3 catches for 20 touches while De'Von Achane was limited to 3 rushes. Miami still turned it over twice. L. Sneed forced and recovered the Willis fumble at Q2 3:16 after Kansas City challenged the down-by-contact ruling. Karlaftis intercepted Willis short right at Q4 2:03, intended for Ryan Miller, with Chris Jones on the play. A later C. Roland-Wallace interception was wiped by a Karlaftis illegal-use-of-hands penalty. Miami’s 37-yard field goal at Q3 10:10 made it 14-10; Kansas City’s 34-yard field goal at Q4 11:35 made it 17-10; an 11-yard touchdown at Q4 2:55 closed it 24-10. Harrison Butker then missed a 50-yarder wide left at Q4 0:57.

The third-down and clock tape is the part that will not flatter anyone in Las Vegas. Kansas City went 3-of-7 and ran 25 times for 88 yards. Miami went 8-of-16, ran 31 times for 119, and sat on 34:21. Both clubs ran 9 drives. Efficiency beat volume in Miami. Crosby will not volunteer that trade.

What held up

  • Mahomes 20-of-24, 246 yards, two passing touchdowns, 119.8 rating, zero sacks
  • Walker’s 10-yard touchdown 2:06 into the game after Kelce’s 48-yard catch
  • Rice’s 7 catches for 88 yards, including the 34-yarder to the Miami 15
  • L. Sneed forcing and recovering the Willis fumble; Karlaftis intercepting Willis at Q4 2:03
  • Gray’s 24-yard catch to the Miami 8 to set up the 34-yard field goal

What broke

  • Team rushing stuck at 88 yards on 25 carries
  • Third down at 3-of-7 against Miami’s 8-of-16
  • Possession at 25:39 against Miami’s 34:21
  • Mahomes’ interception deep middle for Rice; turnover on downs at the Miami 12
  • Five QB hits on Mahomes; Butker’s 50-yard miss; Karlaftis’ penalty wiping the late interception

Efficiency covered a lost clock

Mahomes’ 20-of-24 for 246 and the explosive shots produced 24 points on 18 first downs. Miami’s 19 first downs and 34:21 of possession did not produce a second touchdown after Q1. That math travels until an opponent finishes the red zone Kansas City left on the 12.

The run game showed the identity, not the volume

Walker’s 18 carries for 70 yards and the 10-yard touchdown are the feature. The team number is 88 yards on 25 attempts. Johnson added 6 rushes. Against a 3-0 Raiders front, 88 is a starting point, not a finished product.

The defense created the margin without a sack

Zero sacks, 1 QB hit, and still two Miami turnovers. Credit L. Sneed for the forced and recovered fumble and Karlaftis for the interception. Bolton’s 11 tackles were the tackling tape. The next snap is Crosby and a quarterback who will make Kansas City prove it can finish drives and get off the field on third down.

Current state

Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

3-0

The live record is 3-0. The live questions are the ones Miami left on the table: 88 rushing yards, 3-of-7 on third down, 25:39 of possession, a left tackle in Josh Simmons who has not debuted because of a bulging disk, and a division road game against a 3-0 Raiders club Reid already called aggressive.

What we need to work on

Win first down so third down is not a passing down

Kansas City was 3-of-7 on third down in Miami and ran for 88 yards. Allegiant with Crosby on the edge is where obvious passing downs get expensive. Walker’s 20 touches have to start drives, not just finish them.

Protect the pocket without turning Mahomes into a statue

Crosby is a different rusher than what Miami just put on the tape. Boots, slides, and chips are the Week 4 install, not a hope that 20-of-24 repeats itself in a clean pocket.

Get off the field on third down

Miami converted 8-of-16 and held the ball 34:21. Spagnuolo recorded 0 sacks and 1 QB hit. Cover-2 and the zone blitz are on the call sheet because Las Vegas will keep the football if Kansas City lets another offense live on third-and-medium.

Left tackle plan without Simmons

Reid said Simmons has a bulging disk in his lower back and the 2026 debut has not happened. Crosby is the next snap. That is a protection problem and a run-game problem on the same side of the line.

What to think about

Is Rice the WR1, or is the WR1 still a committee?

Rice went 7 for 88 in Miami and looks like himself again without the offense crowning a single alpha. Worthy, Thornton, Kelce, and Gray all took shots. Allegiant will tell you if that spread is a feature or a tell.

How much Johnson next to Walker

Walker handled 18 rushes and 2 catches. Johnson had 6 rushes. The Indianapolis game was 152 rushing yards as a team; Miami was 88. The staff has to decide if 24 combined carries is enough when the Raiders are 3-0 and playing downhill.

Sneed’s availability after a limited Wednesday

Sneed forced and recovered the Willis fumble, left, and returned in Miami. He was limited on the Wednesday report. A 3-0 Raiders passing game with Brock Bowers in the building is a bad week to guess on a corner.

What 3-0 is actually worth

Last year’s 6-11 is history, not the live record. Polymarket at 8.6 percent to win the Super Bowl still treats three wins as a sample. A road division game against another 3-0 club is how that sample either holds or starts leaking.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Las Vegas Raiders

Week 4 at Las Vegas is 3-0 versus 3-0 for the AFC West lead through four games, CBS, Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT. Reid called the Raiders aggressive. Mahomes wants bragging rights over Crosby. Kansas City is a 70.0 percent FPI favorite and -4.5 at DraftKings. That number assumes the Miami efficiency travels and the Miami clock does not.

How they match up

Both clubs are 3-0. Kansas City’s offense is Mahomes plus a Walker-led run game that just posted 88 yards; Las Vegas’ defense is Crosby and an aggressive front under Klint Kubiak. Kansas City’s defense is Spagnuolo, Jones, Karlaftis, and Bolton, coming off 0 sacks and 1 QB hit against Willis. Las Vegas could be short a blocker on Jones’s side. The leverage points are Crosby versus Kansas City’s unprotected left edge, Jones versus that interior, Walker versus Las Vegas’ early-down defense, and whether Spagnuolo can keep Bowers and the Raiders’ backs from turning third down into another 34-minute afternoon.

Move Mahomes off the spot

Five hits in Miami with zero sacks is the warning, not the comfort. Boot, slide, and get Crosby playing contain. If Kansas City sits in a static pocket, Allegiant becomes a hitting drill even if the sack column stays clean.

Run Walker early so the play-action boot is earned

Team rushing was 88 in Miami. Crosby will not respect play fake if the under-center zone is a give-up down. Walker’s 18-carry tape has to show up on first down, with Johnson as the change-up.

Jones into the interior, Karlaftis into the plan

The Raiders could be missing a key blocker against Jones. That is the one-on-one Kansas City should hunt. Do not wait for 0 sacks to become the weekly identity.

Take away the explosive to Bowers

Miami’s 42-yarder to Greg Dulcich was the deep-middle reminder. Cover-2 and two-high answers keep Bowers from ending drives. Make Las Vegas drive the field the way Miami did — then finish the stop Kansas City did not always get on third down.

Win the possession fight you just lost

Kansas City had the ball 25:39 in Miami. Nine drives each, and the Dolphins still sat on 34:21. Against another 3-0 club, that is how favorites become 3-1.

  1. Open in 12 personnel with Walker on under-center inside zone; four yards is a win if it keeps Crosby honest.
  2. Second-and-medium: play-action boot away from Crosby, Kelce and Gray as the easy reads on the move.
  3. Third-and-5: mesh to Rice and Kelce, Johnson as the checkdown if Kubiak walks a fifth rusher.
  4. If Crosby wins upfield, spacing screen to Walker or Johnson and let the line lead in space.
  5. Second-and-long vs Las Vegas: Spagnuolo zone blitz — show heat, drop a lineman, Jones and a linebacker exchange. Steal the down; do not hunt a takeaway that is not there.
  6. Third-and-7+: Cover-2 shell, Bolton and Drue Tranquill rallying to Bowers and the back, two-deep to take away the shot.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

Kansas City is 3-0, and the AFC West is still a two-club argument. The 24-10 win at Hard Rock Stadium was real — Mahomes 20-of-24 for 246 yards with two passing touchdowns, Kenneth Walker III’s 10-yard run 2:06 into the game, Rashee Rice’s 7 catches for 88 yards — and it was also incomplete. Eighteen first downs, 88 rushing yards, 3-of-7 on third down, and 25:39 of possession are the numbers that actually travel. The 3-0 Raiders and first-year head coach Klint Kubiak are next on Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT, with the West lead through four games on the table.

Miami won the ugly fights and still lost the scoreboard. The Dolphins posted 19 first downs, 119 rushing yards on 31 carries, 8-of-16 on third down, and 34:21 of possession on nine drives. Kansas City answered with 334 total yards, the explosive shots (Travis Kelce 48 yards down the middle, Rice 34, Noah Gray 24, Xavier Worthy 17, Tyquan Thornton 17), a L. Sneed strip-and-recover on Malik Willis, and a Karlaftis interception. That is how you leave 24-10 without winning the clock.

The Indianapolis tape is a different movie and has to stay in its own box: 523 yards, 29 first downs, 371 net passing, 152 rushing, 37:00 of possession in a 33-30 overtime win. Miami squeezed that same offense to 88 on the ground and 25:39 with the football. Eric Bieniemy’s room now has a division road game to decide whether Walker’s 20 touches and Emmett Johnson’s 6 rushes are enough volume when Maxx Crosby is the one walking the edge.

Andy Reid already called Las Vegas ‘aggressive football,’ and Patrick Mahomes is talking ‘bragging rights’ with Crosby. Josh Simmons has a bulging disk in his lower back and has not debuted in 2026. L'Jarius Sneed was limited on the Wednesday report after playing through Miami. ESPN FPI hangs a 70.0 percent number on Kansas City; DraftKings has the Chiefs -4.5 on 47.5. That is a favorite’s price, not a sealed West. Allegiant is where 3-0 either starts looking like a real club or starts quoting last year’s 6-11 as a ghost story.

Film room

The X’s & O’s

Concepts drawn up on the board — what it is, why it works, and the coaching point.

Offense

Walker under-center inside zone

Situation 1st-and-10 vs. Las Vegas, 12 personnel, early in the series

Kansas City ran 25 times for 88 yards in Miami; Walker needs those early-down yards in Allegiant so Crosby is playing the run instead of hunting Mahomes on second and third down.

Coaching point Walker, one cut, pad level. Johnson is the next-man change-up, not a committee argument. Extra linemen (Nourzad, Caliendo, Moore) only on the snaps they report — win the down, keep the series on schedule.

Offense

Boot Mahomes away from Crosby

Situation 2nd-and-6 vs. Las Vegas, Crosby aligned over the left edge

Five QB hits in Miami with zero sacks is the warning label. The boot puts Mahomes on the perimeter, turns Crosby into a contain player, and gives Kelce and Gray the easy moving-pocket reads.

Coaching point Sell Walker. Mahomes gets depth, then width. Kelce over the ball, Gray in the flat, Rice on the deep crosser. If Crosby crashes, throw the flat; if he contains, hit the intermediate window behind him.

Offense

Mesh to Rice and Kelce

Situation 3rd-and-5 vs. Las Vegas, Kubiak walking extra hats

Kansas City was 3-of-7 on third down in Miami. Rice’s 7 catches and Kelce’s deep-middle presence give Mahomes a rub answer when the Raiders play the aggressive football Reid already flagged.

Coaching point Rice and Kelce cross at six yards and pick clean. Gray sits, Johnson is the outlet. Mahomes takes the first opening — do not hold for the shot when a 6-yard win keeps Crosby off the next down.

Offense

Walker / Johnson spacing screen

Situation 2nd-and-8 vs. Las Vegas, Crosby upfield after a win on first down

If Las Vegas sells out to put hits on Mahomes, the screen is how Walker’s 20-touch role and Johnson’s 6-rush change-up punish that aggression without asking the left tackle to win a vacuum.

Coaching point Mahomes sells the drop. The line gets out in space. Walker or Johnson catch, north, and let Rice and Gray finish the perimeter blocks. This is easy offense when Crosby’s ears are pinned.

Defense

Cover-2 vs. Bowers on third down

Situation 3rd-and-7, Raiders 11 personnel, Bowers in the slot

Miami converted 8-of-16 third downs, hit Dulcich for 42 down the middle, and held the ball 34:21. Two-deep, five-under takes away the shot to Bowers and makes Las Vegas drive the field.

Coaching point Safeties split the posts. Corners squat. Bolton and Tranquill rally to Bowers and the back. Tackle, get off the field, and do not donate another 34-minute possession.

Defense

Spagnuolo zone blitz, steal the down

Situation 2nd-and-10 vs. Las Vegas, Jones inside, a linebacker walking

The zone blitz is the call to use against Kubiak’s aggressive offense after Kansas City posted 0 sacks and 1 QB hit in Miami — show heat, drop a lineman, make Las Vegas throw to a window Spagnuolo owns.

Coaching point Jones stabs. A linebacker comes. A lineman drops into the throwing lane. Karlaftis and Bolton exchange jobs on the call. Hunt a hurried throw and a long third down, not a takeaway that is not on the card.

Coaching & adjustments

Reid vs. Klint Kubiak

Reid called the 3-0 Raiders ‘aggressive football.’ Kubiak’s first-year staff will test Kansas City’s left edge and third-down defense. The Chiefs’ answer has to be structure — boots, extra hats on reported snaps, Cover-2 — not a 20-of-24 rerun in a static pocket.

Bieniemy’s early-down identity

Walker is the feature (18 carries, 70 yards, 20 touches in Miami) and the team still posted 88 rushing yards. Inside zone and the play-action boot are how Bieniemy keeps Crosby from turning Allegiant into a passing-down game.

Spagnuolo’s third-down answers

Zero sacks and 1 QB hit against Willis is not the look Kansas City wants heading into a 3-0 opponent. Cover-2 to take away Bowers and the zone blitz to steal second-and-long are the two calls that have to show up on the sheet.
