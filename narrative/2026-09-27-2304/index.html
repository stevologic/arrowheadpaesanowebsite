Last game

What the tape said

A real review of the most recent result — not a box-score dump, and not a skip-ahead.

W
Miami Dolphins Week 3 · Sun, Sep 27 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 24–10

Kansas City left Hard Rock Stadium 3-0 after a 24-10 win that was cleaner on the scoreboard than on the ground. Patrick Mahomes went 20-of-24 for 246 yards, two touchdowns, and one interception; Kenneth Walker III ran 18 times for 70 and a score; Rashee Rice led the receivers with seven catches for 88 yards. Miami still posted 19 first downs to Kansas City's 18, converted 8-of-16 third downs, rushed for 119, and held the ball 34:21 to 25:39. Two Dolphins turnovers and a late Travis Kelce touchdown in traffic turned a possession loss into a 14-point road win.

Start with the Mahomes tape, because it is the reason this did not get away. Twenty completions on 24 attempts is not a vertical explosion; it is an on-schedule offense living in the short-to-intermediate windows Rice and the tight end room can win. A 119.8 passer rating (Arrowhead Pride) with only 24 attempts means Bieniemy was asking him to be a closer, not a volume thrower. That works when you are converting early downs. It becomes a tightrope when you go 3-of-7 on third down and hand the opponent nine extra minutes. The completion percentage traveled. The third-down chart did not.

The run identity that bludgeoned Denver in Week 1 (220 rush yards) was a rumor in Miami. Walker still got the carry volume — 18 attempts is a feature back's workload — but 70 yards is 3.9 a pop, and the team total of 88 means nobody else was breaking the picture. When inside zone does not move the line of scrimmage, play-action becomes a look instead of a threat, and Mahomes is asked to be perfect on a short field position cycle. He mostly was. One interception kept it honest. Two field goals (37 and 34) were the tax for drives that got close and stalled.

Spagnuolo will take 10 points allowed on the road every week of his life, and he should. He should not frame it as a defensive masterpiece. Miami netted 210 through the air and 329 total, stayed on schedule, and made Bolton the busiest man on the field. Eleven tackles from the middle linebacker is a production stat and a warning label: too much of the Dolphins' run game was reaching him instead of dying in the hole. The pass-rush conversation that followed the Colts tape did not get a clean answer here either. Miami converted half its third downs. The Chiefs won because the Dolphins handed them two turnovers and because Kelce finished the night, not because Kansas City consistently got off the field.

Sequence matters. Kansas City punched first (10-yard touchdown, 7-0), Miami answered (3-yard touchdown, 7-7), and the Chiefs went back in front 14-7 on a 5-yard score. From there it was a field-goal war until Kelce's 11-yarder at 2:55 of the fourth. That is complementary football in the ugly sense: enough explosives to stay ahead, not enough early-down wins to put the opponent away. Last year's 6-11 club lost those games. This one cashed it. Vegas will not be confused about which version to attack.

What held up

  • Mahomes' 20-of-24 accuracy and two touchdown throws, including Kelce's 11-yarder that iced it
  • Rice as the volume chain-mover (7 for 88) when the run game stalled
  • Two Miami turnovers and a 10-point ceiling despite 19 Dolphins first downs
  • Walker still getting 18 carries — the feature-back declaration survived a 3.9-yard night

What broke

  • Third-down offense at 3-of-7, which turned efficient completions into punts and field goals
  • Team rushing at 88 yards after the 220-yard Week 1 identity
  • Time of possession at 25:39 against Miami's 34:21
  • Getting off the field: Miami 8-of-16 on third down and 119 rush yards into Bolton

Efficiency is not possession

Mahomes at 20-of-24 for 246 and two scores is a winning quarterback tape. Pair it with 3-of-7 third down and 25:39 of clock and you have a team that is asking its closer to cover for an offense that cannot stay on the field. That is a Week 4 problem, not a footnote.

Walker still has the job. He did not have the lanes.

Eighteen carries for 70 yards is commitment without juice. Bieniemy's downhill identity only scares a division front if the first three steps of inside zone actually move people. Allegiant will sit on the pass if 3.9 yards a carry is the look.

Ten points allowed is the win. Eight-of-16 is the film.

Spagnuolo's unit created two turnovers and kept Miami out of the end zone after the first quarter. It also let the Dolphins post 19 first downs and 119 rush yards. Bolton cannot be the entire second-level answer again in a West game.

Current state

Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

3-0

The Chiefs are 3-0, sitting on a real AFC West cushion, and carrying a Miami tape that looks too much like 2025 for a club that just beat two contenders in September. The standings will not punish them for winning ugly. Las Vegas will try. The next week of practice is about early downs, the run game, and whether a young roster heard Mahomes when he said they cannot relax.

What we need to work on

Third-and-manageable, not third-and-pray

Kansas City went 3-of-7 on third down at Miami because too many early snaps lost. Bieniemy has to win first and second down with Walker and play-action so Mahomes is not asked to be 20-of-24 just to keep the chains from rusting. A West road game will not gift you 83 percent forever.

Reinstall the downhill identity

Week 1 was 220 rush yards. Week 3 was 88. That is not a rounding error; that is the picture changing. If inside zone does not land in Las Vegas, Reid's play-action menu shrinks and the Raiders can hunt the passer with a clean conscience.

Get off the field without waiting for a gift

Two takeaways covered for Miami converting half its third downs. Spagnuolo needs a third-down plan that is not 'hope they drop it' — simulated pressure mixed with a Cover-2 shell so Bolton is not tackling on the 8-yard marker for 60 minutes.

Finish in the red area

Two field goals (37 and 34) in a game you otherwise controlled on the scoreboard is how 14-point wins start feeling like 4-point wins. Mesh, boot, and Walker downhill have to cash from the 15-in instead of kicking.

What to think about

Is 3-0 masking the 6-11 habits?

Arrowhead Pride's postgame said last year's Chiefs almost came out in Miami. The market agrees in the long ticket: 8.8 percent on Polymarket to win the Super Bowl is not how the world prices a runaway. The staff has to decide what is scheme and what is personnel before the West starts keeping receipts.

Did the young roster hear Mahomes?

Patrick Mahomes reminded this team it cannot relax after the first road trip (Arrowhead Pride). That quote only matters if practice this week is sharper than the 25:39 possession tape. Allegiant is a loud building that punishes casual communication.

How much of the West is already over?

The Chargers' nightmare start has Kansas City three games clear before they even meet in Week 6 (Arrowhead Addict). That is a massive edge. It is also how 3-0 teams sleepwalk into a division road game at -5.5 and let the under hit because they brought the Miami clock with them.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Las Vegas Raiders

Division road game at Allegiant Stadium, Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT on CBS. Kansas City is a 72.6 percent ESPN FPI favorite and -5.5 at DraftKings on a 45.5 total — favored, not crowned — and the assignment is to reverse the Miami possession tape against a rival that will try to steal the clock the same way.

How they match up

This is not a mystery matchup of styles. Kansas City wants to be a Mahomes play-action team sitting on a Walker downhill identity; Las Vegas, at home in a West game, wants to dirty first down, win the possession math, and make a 3-of-7 third-down offense live there for four quarters. Miami just showed the blueprint without the talent to finish it. The Raiders do not have to out-skill Rice, Kelce, and Mahomes. They have to make Bolton tackle on schedule and force Kansas City into the same 25:39 night. The leverage for the Chiefs is early-down run plus Spagnuolo mixing simulated pressure with two-deep so Las Vegas cannot both run clock and throw explosives. The -5.5 / 45.5 number says the market already expects a grind, not a track meet.

Win first down so third down is a choice

Script Walker on inside zone and let Mahomes boot off it. If Kansas City is 2nd-and-5, Rice and Kelce can work. If they are 2nd-and-9, Allegiant will pin its ears back and dare a 3-of-7 unit to beat the rush.

Do not let the Raiders rent the clock

Miami held it 34:21 and still lost because of two turnovers. Spagnuolo has to mix zone blitz on early downs with a Cover-2 shell on obvious shots so Las Vegas cannot live in 8-of-16 third-down math. Bolton needs help in the hole, not another 11-tackle cleanup night.

Cash from the 15-in

Two Miami field goals kept the Dolphins in earshot until Kelce ended it. Against a division rival at home, field goals are how -5.5 becomes a one-score sweat. Mesh versus man and Walker on the goal line have to finish.

Treat -5.5 like a trap number, not a bye

A 45.5 total and a 72.6 percent FPI nod are the right temperature for a 3-0 favorite that just lost the underneath fights. If Kansas City brings the Miami possession tape, the Raiders do not need to be the better team. They need to be the more patient one.

  1. Open under center with Walker inside zone to put the Week 3 88-yard rushing tape in the trash before Las Vegas sits on Mahomes.
  2. Second series: play-action boot to the field, Kelce in the flat, Rice over the ball — cash the look Miami never had to respect.
  3. On 3rd-and-5 or less, mesh Rice and Kelce rather than isolating Mahomes in an empty drop that just went 3-of-7.
  4. Spagnuolo: simulated pressure on 2nd-and-6 to wreck the possession steal, then drop into Cover-2 on 3rd-and-long so the deep shot is not the Raiders' out.
  5. Red zone: Walker downhill first, mesh second, field goal only if the down-and-distance is already broken.
  6. If Kansas City wins the toss, take the ball and make Allegiant defend a lead; do not donate another 34-minute opponent the first possession and a clean script.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

Three weeks into 2026, Kansas City is 3-0 and already looking down at the AFC West, and that is the part the standings will print without an argument. The part the film room will print is narrower: after a 31-10 opener and a 33-30 overtime grind, the Chiefs went to Hard Rock Stadium and won 24-10 while losing the possession fight, the first-down fight, and the third-down fight. Patrick Mahomes was surgical. The supporting math was last year's math. Andy Reid's club now takes that split personality into Allegiant Stadium, where a division opponent does not need to out-talent Kansas City — it only needs to do what Miami just did, and finish it.

The Miami night was a reminder dressed up as a result. Mahomes needed only 24 attempts to throw for 246 and two scores; Rashee Rice caught seven for 88; Travis Kelce ended the argument with an 11-yard touchdown in traffic. Kenneth Walker III still got 18 carries. Steve Spagnuolo's defense allowed 10 points and took the ball twice. That is how 3-0 teams keep being 3-0. It is not how 3-0 teams look when they are actually in control. Miami ran 119 yards, converted 8-of-16 third downs, and sat on the clock for 34:21. Nick Bolton had to make 11 tackles because too many runs were arriving at the second level on schedule.

Arrowhead Pride's rapid reaction called it what the building already knew: after two games that looked reinvented, last year's Chiefs almost came out in South Florida and still left with a win. Mahomes, to his credit, did not let the young roster treat 3-0 as a hammock. The next test is not a vibe check. It is a West game on the road against a rival that will have this tape up by Tuesday — 3-of-7 on third down, 88 rush yards, 25:39 of possession — and will try to make Kansas City live there for four quarters.

The West itself is already tilting. The Chargers' start has the Chiefs three games clear before the Week 6 meeting at Arrowhead, which is a real edge and also a trap if it makes this locker room casual. ESPN FPI still makes Kansas City a 72.6 percent favorite in Las Vegas, and DraftKings is only at -5.5 on 45.5, which is not a coronation line. It is a 3-0 tax with a low total. The assignment for Reid, Eric Bieniemy, and Spagnuolo is simple to say and harder to install: win early downs, get Walker downhill, and stop letting opponents rent the clock just because Mahomes can cash a two-minute drill at the end.

Film room

The X’s & O’s

Concepts drawn up on the board — what it is, why it works, and the coaching point.

Offense

Walker downhill, early script

Situation 1st-and-10, opening two series at Allegiant, vs. a Raiders front that has the Miami 88-yard rushing tape

Kenneth Walker III just got 18 carries for 70 yards and the team ran for 88. Las Vegas will sit on Mahomes unless Bieniemy re-establishes first contact. Inside zone is the identity that produced 220 in Week 1 and vanished in Week 3; a West road game is where you either put it back on the field or admit you are a dropback team at 3-of-7 on third down.

Coaching point Andy Reid and Eric Bieniemy: under center, zone steps, Walker one cut. If the first three plays lose, do not panic into empty. Stay in 12/21 and make the Raiders defend the run fake they just watched Miami ignore.

Offense

Boot off the zone look

Situation 2nd-and-5 or 1st-and-10 after a Walker run has landed, field side at Allegiant

Mahomes was 20-of-24 when the offense stayed on schedule in Miami. Play-action boot is how Reid turns that accuracy into leverage: move the pocket, give Kelce an easy out, and let Rice work over the ball. Las Vegas cannot both crash the run and cover the boot if Walker has already made them honest.

Coaching point Mahomes: sell the run, get the edge, throw on time. Kelce, you are the first look in the flat; Rice, you are the over. Do not hold it in a loud building — the Miami interception is the reminder that even 83 percent comes with a tax if you hunt.

Offense

Stress the deep middle when they load the box

Situation 2nd-and-6 or 3rd-and-5, Raiders in single-high after sitting on Walker

Kansas City's 334 yards in Miami were efficient, not explosive. If Las Vegas copies the Dolphins and packs the box to dare the 88-yard run game, four verticals puts seams on the hashes and makes Cover 3 defend Rice, Kelce, and a shot at the same time. A 45.5 total says the market does not expect this; that is why you carry it.

Coaching point Bieniemy: only off a real run look. Mahomes, the middle seam is the answer versus three-deep; outside gos are alerts if the corners bail. Do not force the Miami-style dink if the safety has cheated into the box.

Offense

Red-zone rubs instead of another field goal

Situation 1st/2nd-and-goal or 3rd-and-4 inside the 15, vs. likely man in a West red area

Kansas City settled for a 37-yarder and a 34-yarder in Miami and needed Kelce's 11-yarder to make it 24-10. Mesh puts Rice and Kelce on rub crossers with Walker as the outlet — the exact answer to a defense that will not let you isolate one guy in a loud road red zone. This is how -5.5 stops becoming a one-score sweat.

Coaching point Rice and Kelce: sell the rub, do not get stacked. Walker, chip and leak. Mahomes, take the first open crosser; the Miami night was won on timing, not hero balls. Reid should not leave this drive with a kick unless the down is already broken.

Defense

Two-deep when Las Vegas hunts the shot

Situation 3rd-and-7+ or ahead in the second half, protecting a lead the way they protected 14-7 in Miami

Miami still posted 210 net passing and 19 first downs while scoring only 10. The next opponent will take the shot Kansas City left on the field. Spagnuolo Cover-2 takes away the explosive, rallies to tackle, and asks Bolton to play downhill instead of covering grass 20 yards deep. With Chamarri Conner already ruled out for the Miami game, two-deep also simplifies a secondary that just played a man short on the back end.

Coaching point Spagnuolo: corners squat, safeties split the post, front seven rally. Bolton, you are the hook defender and the run thumper — 11 tackles is enough cleanup. Do not let a West quarterback beat you over the top while you chase the clock fight.

Defense

Simulated heat on early downs

Situation 2nd-and-6, Raiders trying to copy Miami's 8-of-16 third-down math

The pass-rush conversation did not get a clean Miami answer, and Las Vegas will stay on schedule if the Chiefs only rush four on early downs. Spagnuolo's signature — show heat, drop a lineman, send a second-level runner — speeds up the throw without emptying the box against the run that just gained 119. This is how you flip 34:21 of opponent possession without gambling every snap.

Coaching point Show nickel pressure, drop the end into the throwing lane, send Bolton or the nickel off the edge. The point is not a highlight sack; it is a 3rd-and-11 the Raiders did not script. Do not over-blitz into the run game that just made Bolton the tackling leader.

Eighteen carries, 70 yards, one score is a feature back on a night the line did not win. Week 1's 220-yard identity is the standard he was hired into. A West road game is where 3.9 yards a carry gets you third-and-long and a loud building.

WR

Rice is the possession answer until the explosives return.

Seven for 88 was the Miami chain. If Las Vegas brackets him, Kelce and the play-action boot have to be the second answer. Kansas City cannot live on one volume receiver and 88 rush yards in a division game.

LB

Bolton's 11 tackles are production and a flare.

When the middle linebacker leads the team in tackles on a 119-yard night allowed, the front is losing first contact. Spagnuolo needs the run fit tighter in Las Vegas or Bolton will play another 60-minute cleanup shift.

Chess match

Coaching & adjustments

Andy Reid vs. a West road script

Reid's opening script has to choose: re-establish Walker or hide him. Miami proved you can complete 83 percent and still lose the clock. Allegiant is the wrong building in which to open in empty.

Eric Bieniemy's identity check

The OC sold downhill in Week 1 and watched it shrink to 88 yards in Week 3. If inside zone does not land by the second series in Las Vegas, the play-action menu Reid wants is a look, not a threat.

Steve Spagnuolo's third-down desk

Ten points allowed travels. Eight-of-16 on third down does not. Mix zone blitz on early downs with Cover-2 on the shot downs so the Raiders cannot copy Miami's possession game and also throw explosives.
