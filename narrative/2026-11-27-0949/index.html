Buffalo Bills Week 12 · Thu, Nov 26 · 7:20 PM CT
KC 10–13

At Highmark Stadium, Buffalo beat Kansas City 13-10 by keeping the ball and winning the last kick. Kansas City scored 10 points on 241 yards, went 2-of-11 on third down, and never turned it over. Buffalo gained 230 yards, rushed for 166, and possessed it for 36:55.

The first score was Buffalo's. At Q2 14:32, J. Cook ran up the middle for 5 yards and a touchdown, and the extra point made it 0-7. Kansas City's answer before halftime was a 26-yard field goal at Q2 0:02, set up by Kelce's 24-yard catch to the Buffalo 40 and his 21-yard catch to the Buffalo 8. That is a real two-minute sequence from Mahomes to Kelce. It produced three points.

From there the offense could not stack first downs. Ten drives produced 13 first downs. Johnson's 72 yards were almost the entire 74-yard team rushing total on 20 attempts. Mahomes finished 16-of-29 for 174 yards with one sack and six quarterback hits. Cyrus Allen's 19-yard catch in the fourth quarter was a chunk. It did not become a scoring drive.

Buffalo did not need Allen to throw it around. Cook's 32 rushes and Allen's seven rushes sat inside a 43-carry, 166-yard ground plan. The two turnovers were both interceptions: Gilman in the second quarter, Williams in the third.

The finish was a kicking game and a penalty game. Buffalo's 54-yard field goal at Q4 11:58 tied it at 10-10. Butker then missed from 46, wide right, at Q4 6:01. Kansas City flags wiped plays across the night, including holding on J. Ott and K. Suamataia, an ineligible-downfield kick on J. Cochrane, unnecessary roughness on Suamataia, offensive pass interference on Kelce, holding on H. Nourzad, and a false start on F. Johnson. Reid told reporters Rashee Rice's overturned catch was not clear and obvious.

What held up

  • Zero giveaways, with Mahomes recovering both aborted snaps
  • Kelce's four catches for 71 yards, including the 24- and 21-yard gains before the 26-yard field goal

What broke

  • 2-of-11 on third down and 13 first downs
  • 74 team rushing yards against a 43-carry Buffalo plan
  • Six quarterback hits and one sack on Mahomes
  • Butker's 46-yard miss wide right while the score was 10-10

The run clock was the game

Two interceptions did not flip the down-and-distance script.

Ten points on zero giveaways

Mahomes recovered both aborted snaps, and the team turnover count is zero. The miss was conversion rate: 2-of-11 on third down, 13 first downs, and a 46-yard field goal wide right while the score was 10-10.

Johnson carried the run, Kelce carried the pass

Johnson had 21 touches, 72 rushing yards, and the rushing touchdown. Kelce had four catches for 71 yards. The rest of the skill group was sparse: Rice two catches, Gray three, and one each for Downs, Royals, and Cyrus Allen.

Current state

8-3, a game back, and the third-down stall

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

8-3

The slate record is 8-3 after the loss in Buffalo. Arrowhead Pride's playoff picture put Kansas City a game behind Denver in the AFC West, with Buffalo strengthening its hold on the top of the conference. The tape that has to travel is a 241-yard, 2-of-11 night, not the Arizona afternoon.

What we need to work on

Third down before the explosives

Kansas City was 2-of-11 and finished with 13 first downs. Kelce's 24- and 21-yard catches and Allen's 19-yard catch were real. They did not replace a third-down plan against a defense that can sit on obvious passing downs after six quarterback hits.

Early-down run share

Johnson had 17 of 20 team rushes and 72 of 74 rushing yards. That is a workable identity if the line can finish blocks. It is not a 43-carry counter if the opponent wants to hold the ball the way Buffalo did for 36:55.

Operational flags and the kicking operation

Suamataia had a holding penalty and an unnecessary-roughness penalty. Butker told the building, through Arrowhead Pride, that his confidence is still high after the 46-yard miss. The operation still has to be trustworthy on a 44.5 total.

What to think about

How much of Buffalo was the opponent's plan

Allen at 10-of-21 and 64 net passing yards is a Spagnuolo night. Cook at 32 rushes inside 166 team rushing yards is the part that has to be answered before Sean McVay's offense gets a short-week script.

Rice on the late down Reid would not concede

Reid said the overturned Rice catch was not clear and obvious. Rice still finished with two catches. The staff has to decide how much of the third-down menu stays on him next to Kelce's four-catch, 71-yard line.

Who is available on the short week

Jalen Royals left with an ankle injury and was ruled out. Kenneth Walker did not dress against Buffalo. Reid also addressed Trey Smith after the game. Thursday's plan cannot assume a full skill group or a settled right guard until the next report says so.

Looking ahead

Los Angeles minus 1.5 on a short week

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Los Angeles Rams

Kansas City visits the Rams on Thu Dec 3, 7:15 PM CT on Prime Video as a DraftKings 1.5-point underdog on a 44.5 total. ESPN FPI has Kansas City at 45.0 percent and Los Angeles at 54.6. After a 10-13 loss built on possession, the assignment is to win early downs and keep McVay from playing the same keep-away game Buffalo just played.

How they match up

Buffalo showed the cost of a one-dimensional response. Spagnuolo can bother a quarterback — Allen was 10-of-21 with two sacks and an 18.3 rating — and still lose the night if the run game faces 43 carries and a 36:55 clock. Bieniemy's counter is Johnson on early downs, Kelce and Rice in the pattern, and easier throws after Mahomes took six hits and one sack. The Rams are a small favorite in their building. Kansas City does not need a shootout on a 44.5 total. It needs more than 13 first downs.

Make Johnson the first-down answer

He just carried 17 times for 72 yards and scored the 1-yard touchdown. Inside zone on early downs keeps Mahomes out of the third-and-long pocket that produced one sack and six hits.

Do not ask the defense to play another 36-minute game

Cover-2 and a zone-blitz change-up are the calls if Los Angeles wants explosives and then the run. Buffalo's 9-of-16 on third down is the reminder that tackles after the catch and on the ground decide the clock.

Treat four points as live

Butker made the 26-yard field goal and missed the 46-yarder wide right while tied. On a 44.5 total, a make in that range changes who is playing from behind in the fourth quarter.

  1. Use a screen on an obvious rush down so the six-hit pocket from Buffalo is not the only answer.
  2. Take a deep shot to Kelce once the run has Buffalo-style linebackers stepping, without labeling every dropback as play-action.
  3. Spagnuolo in Cover-2 on early downs, then a zone blitz on a medium third down to change the protection picture.
  4. If the game is inside a score late, the field-goal operation has to be cleaner than the 46-yard miss at Q4 6:01.
