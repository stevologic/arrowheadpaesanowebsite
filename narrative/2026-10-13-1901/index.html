The edge

Current state

Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

4-0

Kansas City is 4-0, sat the Week 5 bye at that record, and is ranked near the top of the national lists. The live problems are third down, possession, and a receiver rotation that just lost its yardage leader.

What we need to work on

Third down without Thornton

5-of-13 will not travel if the 42- and 55-yard answers are on a 12-to-16-week timeline. Reid has pointed Allen toward a larger share, and Watson is a practice-squad bridge, not a finished replacement.

Protect the 15-of-30 chart

Two sacks and eight hits are the hit count to coach, not a myth about a clean pocket. Kingsley Suamataia is due back in at left guard after Mike Caliendo started the entire Las Vegas game.

Get off the field on early downs

Cousins needed 52 attempts because the rush was stuck at 72 yards, and he still posted 365 net passing yards. Spagnuolo’s next install has to tackle the tight end and the checkdown before the deep shot.

What to think about

Is Walker the identity or the supplement?

Arrowhead Pride has him at 621 scrimmage yards, six touchdowns and 6.2 yards per carry through the opening month. Against Los Angeles the question is whether Bieniemy keeps feeding 22-carry volume or asks Mahomes to throw the game open.

Familiarity or a bigger swing at receiver

The club passed on the Marvin Harrison Jr. market and signed Watson. Fans can debate the price. The staff has to decide whether Worthy, Allen, Travis Kelce and Noah Gray can cover Thornton’s 111-yard afternoon.

How much Nwankpa plays

He intercepted Cousins and was in on the kickoff tackle before the fumble. Arrowhead Addict is arguing for a larger safety role after the bye. That is a snap-count decision, not a press-release.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Los Angeles Chargers

Week 6 is the 0-5 Chargers at GEHA Field at Arrowhead on Sun Oct 18, 3:25 PM CT, on CBS. Kansas City is favored by 8.5 with a 42.5 total. The assignment is to keep Walker’s downhill identity, give Mahomes defined answers without Thornton, and make Herbert throw into two deep safeties.

How they match up

Kansas City’s best recent offense was a 189-yard rush game attached to selective explosives, and Mahomes still went 15-of-30 in that win. The Chargers under Jim Harbaugh still want to run and play from a compact passing menu with Herbert. Spagnuolo’s edge is the same one that held Las Vegas to 72 on the ground while failing to sack Cousins. If the Chargers steal early downs on the ground, Herbert’s play-action becomes the problem. If Walker wins first down, Bieniemy can live in inside zone, mesh, and screens instead of asking a thinned receiver room to win 1-on-1 all afternoon.

Feed Walker before the script gets cute

Twenty-two carries worked. Early downs against a Chargers front should look like inside zone with K. Tonga as the reported extra blocker on the snaps that call for him — and those reports have to match the actual snap, the way Q1 11:29 listed both H. Nourzad and Tonga while Q1 6:01 listed Tonga.

Define the throws for Worthy, Allen, Kelce and Gray

Worthy’s 41-yard catch and four receptions are the vertical proof. Kelce and Gray had two catches each. Mesh and a screen keep Mahomes out of an eight-hit pocket while Allen takes the intermediate snaps Reid already promised.

Two-deep, then rally

Las Vegas scored touchdowns of 27 and 20 and still moved the ball to 365 net passing yards. The zone blitz is a call to steal a down. It is not a story about how Nwankpa intercepted a Las Vegas pass.

  1. Open in under-center inside zone to Walker; ask Suamataia, back in at left guard, to climb to the linebacker.
  2. On a plus down, throw mesh with Kelce and Gray crossing and Walker as the checkdown.
  3. Once the run is respected, boot Mahomes to the boundary with Worthy on the wheel or flat — a call for this week, not a label on the Allegiant throws.
  4. Steal a third-and-medium with a Walker screen so the eight-hit pocket from Las Vegas does not become the story again.
  5. Against Herbert, start in Cover-2 on obvious pass downs and rally Delane and Nick Bolton to the tight end.
  6. Call a simulated pressure with a rusher dropping; do not tell the room the interception by Nwankpa of a Las Vegas pass was a blitz.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

The 4-0 Chiefs did not win a track meet at Allegiant Stadium. They won a 30-27 game in which Las Vegas held the ball for 35:42, posted 28 first downs and 437 yards, and still left with two turnovers. Kansas City’s 189 rushing yards and a clean sheet — zero giveaways — were the margin. The Week 5 bye did not change that tape. It changed the receiver room that has to repeat it.

Patrick Mahomes was 15-of-30 for 225 yards and two touchdowns, with two sacks and eight quarterback hits, and the net passing line was 210. That is an efficient enough afternoon when Kenneth Walker III carries 22 times for 177 and two scores and the two takeaways are the interception by Nwankpa plus the kickoff-coverage fumble Bassa forced and Cochrane recovered. It is not a blueprint you can photocopy against Justin Herbert if the explosives have to come from someone other than Tyquan Thornton.

Thornton’s five catches went for 111 yards and two touchdowns, including the 42-yard push to the Las Vegas 5 and the 55-yarder to the 5 that drew the unnecessary-roughness flag. Arrowhead Pride, citing Ian Rapoport, has the ankle dislocation on a 12-to-16-week timeline. Andy Reid has already said Cyrus Allen will work into the offense a little bit more, and Justin Watson is back on the practice squad. Familiarity is the bridge. It is not 111 yards.

The standings still offer a real window. Arrowhead Pride’s Week 6 roundup has the Chiefs nearly unanimous at No. 2 after sitting 4-0 through the bye, and the same site has mapped a three-week AFC West stretch that starts with the 0-5 Chargers at GEHA Field at Arrowhead. Favored by 8.5 at home is the market’s read. The football read is whether Walker’s identity and Steve Spagnuolo’s two-deep answers travel when the passing menu is shorter.

The X’s & O’s

Concepts drawn up on the board — what it is, why it works, and the coaching point.

Offense

Walker inside zone, Suamataia climbing

Situation 1st-and-10 early, home, against a Chargers front that will try to set the edge and force Mahomes into eight-hit pockets

The 30-27 win was built on 189 rushing yards and Walker’s 22 carries for 177. With Thornton out, early-down runs keep Allen and Worthy out of low-percentage isolation and let Suamataia, back in at left guard, play downhill instead of in pass protection all series.

Coaching point Walker: one cut, don’t dance. Suamataia and the center combination block the first down lineman and climb. Tonga reports eligible on the snap that actually lists him — do not assume the Q1 11:29 double report repeats.

Offense

Four verts with Worthy as the stretch

Situation 2nd-and-short after a Walker gain, Chargers spinning a single-high look

Worthy’s 41-yard catch to the Las Vegas 7 is the explosive that is still on the roster. Allen is the extra vertical Reid said would play more. Kelce can bend a seam so Mahomes is not hunting Thornton’s 55-yard shot.

Coaching point Worthy: win outside leverage and stack the corner. Allen: settle into the void if the safety stays on Worthy. Mahomes: if the middle is capped, throw the hash, don’t force the double move.

Offense

Mesh for Kelce and Gray

Situation 3rd-and-5, a third-down distance from Kansas City's 5-of-13 afternoon at Allegiant

Kelce and Gray combined for four catches in a game that needed a third-down answer besides Thornton. Rubs give Mahomes a defined throw when the hit count starts climbing toward eight.

Coaching point Cross at depth, don’t widen the rub into a hold. Walker sits as the outlet if both linebackers run with the tight ends. Mahomes: throw the first open window, this is not a scramble-first down.

Offense

Boot off the Walker action

Situation 1st-and-10 in Chargers territory after two inside-zone looks have the edge crashing

This is a call for Sun Oct 18, not a description of the Allegiant throws. Selling Walker’s 177-yard identity moves the pocket away from the rush that put eight hits on Mahomes and gives Worthy a boundary answer while Allen and Kelce work the middle.

Coaching point Mahomes: sell the mesh point, then get to the edge with two hitches. Worthy: flat or wheel, eyes up. The tackle on the boot side has to reach the end — Suamataia’s return is the left-side version of that assignment.

Defense

Cover-2 versus Herbert’s shot

Situation 2nd-and-long after a stuff, Herbert looking downfield the way Cousins did on throws of 27, 23 and 20

Las Vegas scored from 27 and from 20 and still finished with 365 net passing yards. Two deep safeties take the throw Spagnuolo cannot keep giving up, and Delane’s 10 tackles are the rally standard underneath.

Coaching point Corners jam and sink. Safeties stay deeper than the deepest. Bolton and the nickel rally to the tight end. Do not vacate the deep half to chase a glance route.

Defense

Zone blitz as a down-stealer

Situation 2nd-and-7, Herbert in the shotgun, Kansas City needing a third-and-long

Cousins was hit 10 times and sacked zero times, so pressure that does not finish is not a plan. A simulated pressure — show heat, drop a lineman, rush a second-level player — is a call to change Herbert’s picture. It is not a claim that a blitz produced either Las Vegas takeaway.

Coaching point Karlaftis can be the declared rusher or the dropper; the picture has to be opposite of the last snap. The dropping lineman walls the tight-end cross. If the ball comes out hot, tackle. Do not celebrate a call that has not happened yet.
