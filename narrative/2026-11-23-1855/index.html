34-7 film: Johnson's 27 touches, Kelce's two scores

The previous result, with the details that matter to the next assignment.

W
Arizona Cardinals Week 11 · Sun, Nov 22 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 34–7

The fourth-quarter 18-yard touchdown made it 34-7. Both clubs finished with zero turnovers.

The opening script was a run game with a quarterback attached, not a track meet. Johnson's first snap went right end for 16 to the Kansas City 44, then the carries stacked up the middle: 4, 5, 7, and a no-gain right end to the Arizona 8 before the 8-yard score. A later drive put him up the middle for 2, 4, 8, and the 3-yard touchdown. That is 23 carries, 105 yards, one touchdown, plus four catches, 27 touches. Ott added five rushes and Carter one. The team line was 31 attempts for 118 yards.

Mahomes was 27-of-39 for 324 yards, three touchdowns, one sack, and a 120.0 rating. The team net passing line is 317. Rashee Rice also had six catches. Josh Downs had four. Noah Gray had three. Xavier Worthy and Cyrus Allen had two apiece, including Allen's 26-yard catch to the Arizona 18 with 0:04 left in the third quarter, ahead of the 18-yard touchdown at Q4 14:54. Deep shots were not automatic: incomplete deep right to Downs, deep left to Allen, deep left to Worthy, and another deep right to Worthy.

Josh Johnson finished 18-of-31 with three sacks and an 82.1 rating. The Cardinals managed 14 first downs, 75 rushing yards on 21 attempts, 130 net passing yards, and 205 total yards in 27:34. Trey McBride had 10 touches. Tyler Allgeier had seven rushes. Bam Knight had eight touches. Two Arizona drives ended on downs: Simi Fehoko right end for no gain at the Arizona 37, and a Johnson incompletion to Harrison Wallace. Kansas City had 27 first downs, 7-of-13 on third down, 435 yards, 10 drives, and 32:26 of possession.

Karlaftis posted 2.5 sacks inside a defense that put seven quarterback hits on Josh Johnson. On the third-quarter sack at the Arizona 45, Karlaftis forced the fumble and J. Fryar recovered it for Arizona, so the strip did not become a Kansas City takeaway. Mahomes was hit five times and sacked once. The third quarter stayed off the scoreboard.

What held up

  • Johnson's up-the-middle and right-end carries, including the 16-yard right end and the 3-yard touchdown
  • Kelce's six catches, 74 yards, and two touchdowns, with the 22-yard score named in the play-by-play
  • Mahomes at 27-of-39, 324 yards, three touchdowns, 120.0, and zero interceptions
  • A defense that held Arizona to 205 yards, 5-of-13, and two failed fourth downs
  • Karlaftis at 2.5 sacks with seven team quarterback hits

What broke

  • No points in the third quarter after a 27-7 halftime lead
  • Five quarterback hits and one sack on Mahomes
  • Intentional grounding before the half-ending 44-yard field goal, plus Hicks' illegal block
  • Deep incompletions to Downs, Allen, and Worthy
  • Gray's injury on a day he caught three passes, with the tight-end room already thin for Thursday

Johnson was the down-to-down offense

Twenty-three carries, 105 yards, a 3-yard touchdown, and four catches gave Kansas City an early-down identity Arizona never flipped. The team still finished at 118 rushing yards on 31 attempts, so the backfield was more than one highlight carry.

Third down climbed without a giveaway

Seven of 13 is a different afternoon from the 4-of-13 in Atlanta, and the turnover column stayed at zero. Ten drives and 32:26 let the score grow from 14-0 to 27-7 before the half and 34-7 after the fourth-quarter touchdown.

The pass rush won the down, not the ball

Karlaftis' 2.5 sacks and the seven quarterback hits sat on an Arizona offense that managed 130 net passing yards. The forced fumble went back to Arizona via Fryar. That is pressure production with the ball staying put.

Current state

8-2, a thin tight-end room, and a West tiebreaker to chase

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

8-2

Kansas City is 8-2, tied with Denver in the loss column, and behind the Broncos on the tiebreakers Arrowhead Pride laid out after both clubs went to 3-1 in the division. The next three listed games are at Buffalo, at the Rams, and at Cincinnati. The Arizona win is the proof of focus. It is not the opponent.

What we need to work on

Tight-end snaps without Gray

Arrowhead Addict reports Gray's status as uncertain after the Arizona win, with the room dangerously thin ahead of the hardest stretch. Kelce's six catches and two touchdowns cannot be the whole inline plan if Gray cannot go. Bieniemy has to decide who replaces those three catches in the pattern, not just who stands next to the tackle.

Third-down stay-ahead against a better front

Seven of 13 against Arizona fixed the 4-of-13 Atlanta line. Buffalo will not live on 205 yards. The staff should treat 7-of-13 as the floor to protect, especially after a third quarter that did not score.

Hits on Mahomes on a short week

Five quarterback hits and one sack is the Arizona number. Write that, not a story about a clean pocket. Johnson, Ott, and a quick answer have to be available when Buffalo shows heat, because Thursday does not come with a long recovery window.

What to think about

How much of Johnson's 27 touches repeats

Walker remains an injury the club is managing, per Arrowhead Addict. Johnson just posted 23 carries and four catches. Ott had five rushes and Carter one. Reid has to decide whether that split is the Thursday plan or whether the lead back needs a lighter early script so he is available late.

Whether 8-2 is a West lead or a West tie

Denver holds the tiebreaker edge with matching 8-2 and 3-1 division marks. Fans can enjoy 34-7 and still track that the division is not a cushion. Buffalo is the national stage. The Broncos are the column that decides home-field math if both keep winning.

Looking ahead

Thursday in Buffalo: a 47.1 percent FPI underdog with a real script

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Buffalo Bills

Thursday, Thu Nov 26, 7:20 PM CT, on NBC, Kansas City is a three-point underdog at Buffalo on a 50.5 total. ESPN FPI lists the Chiefs at 47.1 percent and Buffalo at 52.5. The assignment is to play a complete road game against a 9-1 opponent without asking the Arizona margin to do the work.

How they match up

Kansas City just won the possession game, 32:26 to 27:34, and the yardage game, 435 to 205, behind Johnson's 27 touches and Mahomes' 27-of-39. Buffalo is the favorite because the Bills' offense is the problem Arizona's 130 net passing yards were not. Spagnuolo can steal the structure that limited Josh Johnson — rally tackles, seven quarterback hits, three sacks — and still needs a plan for a quarterback who will not operate on an 82.1 rating script. On offense, Bieniemy has a proven early-down runner, Kelce and Rice as the volume catchers, and a question at the second tight end. Reid's veterans avoided a letdown before this trip, which Arrowhead Addict noted. Avoiding a letdown against Arizona is the entry fee. Winning a minus-3 road game is the course.

Stay on schedule with Johnson before the down gets long

Against Buffalo, those early wins matter more, because a third-and-8 against this pass rush is where the five hits on Mahomes become a drive-killer. Give Johnson the early down. Use Ott as the changeup, not the rescue.

Make Kelce and Rice the answers if Gray cannot play

Kelce already has the two touchdowns and 74 yards. Rice has six catches with no yardage total supplied, so the plan is volume and leverage, not a made-up average. Crossers and sit routes keep Mahomes from holding the ball into a second hit. Worthy and Downs remain the vertical stress, used when the shell cheats to Kelce.

Rush Josh Allen's launch point without abandoning the deep half

Karlaftis has 2.5 sacks in this game and eight on the season, per Arrowhead Pride, with Chris Jones already on the Arizona tackle chart. The call is heat that still has two deep defenders. Arizona's 2-yard touchdown came on a drive that included a 25-yard completion to Wallace. Buffalo will hunt that explosive. Cover-2 and a zone blitz are the two answers, used as calls, not as a retelling of the Fryar recovery.

  1. Open under center with inside zone to Johnson and force Buffalo to declare a front before Mahomes throws.
  2. On the second third-and-medium, mesh Kelce and Rice with Ott as the checkdown so a five-hit pocket does not become a sack.
  3. Steal a down with a Johnson or Ott screen if the Bills' edge crashes inside.
  4. Take one four-verticals shot to Worthy or Downs once the run has Buffalo in a single-high look.
  5. On Buffalo's first third-and-long, start in Cover-2 and rally the flats instead of trading a deep shot for a pressure.
  6. Dial the zone blitz on a second-and-medium after a modest gain, with Karlaftis and Jones as the rush threats and a second-level defender replacing a dropped lineman.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

The Cardinals afternoon did what a heavy home price is supposed to do: Kansas City led 14-0 before Arizona scored, closed the half at 27-7, and finished 34-7 with four touchdowns, two Harrison Butker field goals, and a clean sheet on giveaways. The story that matters now is the short trip to Buffalo, where DraftKings has the Bills minus 3 and ESPN FPI has Kansas City at 47.1 percent.

Andy Reid's group is 8-2 and has won three in a row, including 35-13 at Atlanta and 35-12 against the Jets before this 34-7. Arrowhead Addict's read is that this roster cashed a softer stretch instead of playing down to it. That is useful context, not a Buffalo game plan. The Bills are 9-1 in the framing Arrowhead Pride used for this rivalry week, and the market treats Thursday as a toss-up with the home side leaned.

The Arizona tape still has transferable pieces. Emmett Johnson carried 23 times for 105 yards and a touchdown and finished with 27 touches. Patrick Mahomes was 27-of-39 for 324 yards and three touchdowns with one sack and a 120.0 rating. Travis Kelce caught six passes for 74 yards and two touchdowns, including the 22-yard score with 1:54 left in the second quarter. Steve Spagnuolo's defense held Arizona to 205 yards, 14 first downs, and 5-of-13 on third down, with George Karlaftis at 2.5 sacks.

What has to travel is the correction, not the margin. Third down moved from 4-of-13 in Atlanta to 7-of-13 against Arizona, and Kansas City held the ball for 32:26 on 10 drives. The third quarter produced no points. Noah Gray's injury leaves the tight-end room thin behind Kelce on the week Eric Bieniemy would most like a second inline option. Kenneth Walker's injury remains a managed absence, with Johnson, Jaydn Ott, and Nate Carter splitting the backfield work that is actually on the tape.

Denver is also 8-2 and, per Arrowhead Pride, leads the AFC West on tiebreakers with both clubs 3-1 in the division. A road result in Buffalo does not fix that tiebreaker by itself. It does tell you whether an 8-2 built on 435 yards and zero turnovers against Arizona can stand up when the opponent is favored.
