The edge

Current state

Walker out, Gray uncertain, Nourzad still in the mix

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

8-2

Kansas City is 8-2 with a third straight win, a humming skill group, and a short-week injury list that already changes the plan for Buffalo.

What we need to work on

Edge protection against Rousseau

Mahomes just took five hits and one sack. The chip, the slide, and the quick answer have to be installed before Thursday, with Nourzad's emergency start part of that conversation.

Run balance without Walker

Walker remains sidelined. Bieniemy has talked about balancing Kenneth Walker and Emmett Johnson, but the available tape is Johnson at 27 touches, Ott at five rushes, and Carter at one. The staff has to decide how much of that 23-carry load repeats on a short week.

Tight-end depth if Gray cannot go

Gray caught three passes against Arizona and is now an uncertain availability piece. Kelce's six catches and two scores are the proven answer. The rest of the tight-end plan cannot be assumed.

What to think about

Is 8-2 priced correctly as a road underdog?

FPI says 46.6 percent for Kansas City and 53.1 for Buffalo. DraftKings says Bills minus 3, total 47.5. The Arizona yardage total of 435 does not override a market that still likes the home team.

How much of the Arizona pass rush translates?

Karlaftis had 2.5 sacks and Kansas City put seven hits on Josh Johnson. Josh Allen is a different quarterback to contain. Spagnuolo's plan has to be about fits and rally tackles, not a highlight from a 205-yard opponent.

Looking ahead

How Kansas City attacks a Bills team favored by a field goal

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Buffalo Bills

Win the edges, stay on schedule, and treat Buffalo as a field-goal favorite on Thu Nov 26, 7:20 PM CT, not as a sequel to a 34-7 home afternoon.

How they match up

Kansas City's offense just produced 317 net passing yards and 118 rushing yards with zero turnovers. Spagnuolo's defense just held an opponent to 205 yards and 5-of-13 on third down. That opponent was Arizona. Josh Allen and a Bills offense that plays at home on a 47.5 total are the step up. Kansas City can win the skill matchups if Kelce and Rice stay the answers and Johnson stays the early-down hammer. Buffalo can win the game in the pocket and on the scoreboard margin the market already prices at three points.

Do not let Rousseau turn five hits into a lost down

Slide the protection, use a back or Kelce as a chip, and have a screen or a quick crosser ready. Mahomes was efficient at 27-of-39. He does not need to hold the ball against a 10-sack edge.

Stay ahead of the chains with Johnson

Walker is sidelined. Johnson's 105 yards on 23 carries is the available identity. Early-down runs keep third down from becoming a Rousseau down.

Make Kelce the middle of the field

Gray is uncertain. Kelce already has the 22-yard touchdown and a second score from the Arizona game. Rice's six catches give Mahomes a second answer if Buffalo sits on 87.

Rally tackles in a two-deep shell

Arizona gained 130 net passing yards. Buffalo will threaten more vertically. Cover 2 takes the deep shot away and asks the linebackers to fit the checkdown.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Johnson and force Buffalo to declare its run fits before Rousseau can pin his ears back.
  2. On the first obvious pass-rush down, throw the screen and let the line lead. Five hits on Mahomes last game is the caution light.
  3. Use mesh with Kelce and Rice as the crossers once Buffalo starts playing man on the perimeter.
  4. Take a vertical shot to Worthy or Downs only after the run and the crossers have the safeties leaning.
  5. Call what the available players can repeat: Johnson downhill, Kelce in the middle.
  6. On defense, start in Cover 2 and save the zone blitz for a defined passing down, with a dropped lineman and Karlaftis or Chris Jones as the designated rusher.
Last game

What the 34-7 tape actually showed

The previous result, with the details that matter to the next assignment.

W
Arizona Cardinals Week 11 · Sun, Nov 22 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 34–7

At Arrowhead, Kansas City led 14-0 after two first-quarter touchdowns, answered Arizona's only score, and closed a 34-7 win with 27 first downs, 7-of-13 on third down, 10 drives, and 32:26 of possession.

Patrick Mahomes found Travis Kelce for an 8-yard touchdown at Q1 9:46 to make it 7-0. Emmett Johnson up the middle for 3 yards at Q1 0:48, with Harrison Butker's extra point, and the scoreboard read 14-0. Arizona's touchdown came at Q2 9:48 from 2 yards, cutting it to 14-7.

Kansas City then stacked a 40-yard field goal at Q2 4:32 for 17-7, Mahomes to Kelce on a deep-middle throw for 22 yards and a touchdown at Q2 1:54 for 24-7, and a 44-yard field goal at Q2 0:00 for 27-7. The 22-yard throw is not labeled play-action or play fake on the play-by-play. Mahomes was flagged for intentional grounding at Q2 0:16 before that last field goal. The fourth Kansas City touchdown, 18 yards at Q4 14:54, made it 34-7.

Johnson carried the run tape: 23 rushes and four catches, 27 touches, 105 rushing yards, and the 3-yard score. Team rushing was 118 on 31 attempts, so Jaydn Ott's five rushes, Nate Carter's one, and Justin Fields's two rushes are part of the 118, not a separate team total. Kelce finished 6 catches for 74 yards and two touchdowns. Rashee Rice also had six catches. Josh Downs had four. Cyrus Allen's 26-yard catch at the end of the third quarter moved the ball to the Arizona 18.

Arizona managed 14 first downs, 75 rushing yards on 21 attempts, 130 net passing yards, 205 total yards, 5-of-13 on third down, nine drives, and 27:34 of possession, with zero turnovers. Josh Johnson was 18-of-31 with three sacks and an 82.1 rating. Trey McBride had 10 touches. Kansas City recorded seven quarterback hits. George Karlaftis was credited with 2.5 sacks. On one of them Johnson fumbled and J. Fryar recovered for Arizona. Both clubs finished with zero turnovers. Arizona also failed on downs twice, including Simi Fehoko stopped at the Arizona 37.

What held up

  • Zero giveaways and a 32:26 to 27:34 possession edge against Arizona
  • Kelce as a scoring target: 6 catches, 74 yards, two touchdowns, including the 22-yard score
  • Johnson downhill, including the Q1 0:48 touchdown, inside a 31-carry team run plan
  • A pass rush that sacked Josh Johnson three times and hit the quarterback seven times

What broke

  • Five quarterback hits on Mahomes, a count that Rousseau's season line says can grow
  • Intentional grounding on Mahomes before the half-ending field goal
  • Nourzad's start in place of Trey Smith still untested against a top edge rusher

Third-down bump, still not a finished product

Kansas City was 7-of-13 on third down against Arizona after going 4-of-13 at Atlanta. That is a cleaner down-and-distance day on a separate box, with 27 first downs and 10 drives. Buffalo will not hand out the same intermediate windows.

Johnson is the volume back while Walker sits

Twenty-three carries and four catches is a full workload, and the team still only rushed for 118 yards.

Five hits is the pressure number that matters

Mahomes took one sack and five quarterback hits on a 27-of-39, 120.0 day. Karlaftis's 2.5 sacks and the seven hits Kansas City recorded are the rush identity to carry forward.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

The next assignment is Buffalo, not another home afternoon against a team Kansas City can pull away from. Andy Reid's club is 8-2, DraftKings has the Bills minus 3 on a 47.5 total, and ESPN FPI puts Kansas City at 46.6 percent to win on Thu Nov 26, 7:20 PM CT on NBC.

The Arizona result is useful context, not the lead. Kansas City scored 34, allowed 7, finished with 435 yards and zero turnovers, and moved to a third straight win. Patrick Mahomes was 27-of-39 for 324 yards and three touchdowns with a 120.0 rating and one sack. The team net-passing line was 317, with 118 rushing yards on 31 attempts.

The travel problem is personnel as much as the price. Ken Walker remains sidelined, so the run plan sits with Emmett Johnson, who had 23 carries for 105 yards and a touchdown plus four catches. Noah Gray's status is uncertain after a three-catch afternoon, which tightens the tight-end picture around Travis Kelce. Hunter Nourzad started in place of Trey Smith and now has to be judged against a better edge.

Eric Bieniemy has already flagged Gregory Rousseau, second in the league with 10 sacks and leading the league with 29 quarterback hits. Arizona put five hits on Mahomes. That number is the relevant pressure line heading to Orchard Park, not a story about survival.

After Buffalo, the slate does not soften: Thu Dec 3, 7:15 PM CT at the Rams on Prime Video, then Cincinnati. This is the stretch Arrowhead Addict framed as the real tests after a softer run of opponents. The 8-2 record is live. The market says the Bills are the better side on Thursday by a field goal.

Cover 2 to take the deep shot away

Situation 1st-and-10 against Josh Allen, Kansas City protecting a lead or a one-score game

Arizona managed 130 net passing yards and 5-of-13 on third down. Buffalo will threaten over the top more often. Two deep and five under asks Bolton and Tranquill to rally and keeps a bust from becoming a home-run score on a 47.5 total.

Zone blitz on a defined passing down

Situation 2nd-and-8 or longer, Josh Allen in the shotgun, Kansas City showing heat and dropping a lineman

This is a call to use against Buffalo, built on Karlaftis's 2.5 sacks and seven quarterback hits from the Arizona game. Show six, drop a tackle into the throwing lane, and rush the second level so Allen has to throw before the rush arrives.

Coaching point Spagnuolo picks the dropped lineman before the snap. Karlaftis or Chris Jones is the long rusher. The dropped player has the intermediate crosser. The call stands on its own as a pressure answer.
