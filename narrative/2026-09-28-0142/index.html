Last game

What the tape said

A real review of the most recent result — not a box-score dump, and not a skip-ahead.

W
Miami Dolphins Week 3 · Sun, Sep 27 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 24–10

The Dolphins out-gained nobody — 329 yards to 334 — but they won the downs and the clock.

The opening sequence was the version of this offense people came to see. Mahomes hit Travis Kelce for 48 yards to the Miami 19, and Kansas City cashed a 10-yard touchdown for 7-0. Miami answered with a 3-yard touchdown to tie it at 7-7, and Kansas City went back in front 14-7 on a 5-yard touchdown. Those were not empty calories: Mahomes later dropped a 17-yarder to Thornton, a 17-yarder to Xavier Worthy, a 24-yarder to Noah Gray, and a 34-yarder to Rashee Rice. He was accurate and on time. He was not, for long stretches, complementary to a run game that finished with 88 team rushing yards.

The second quarter is the film session. Miami turned it over on downs, Kansas City answered by burning a short-yardage chance — Hunter Nourzad reported eligible, Walker took it up the middle for no gain at the Miami 12, turnover on downs. Rodriguez at the Miami 29. You cannot call that a complementary sequence. You can call it the 2025 habit: explosives and negative plays living in the same quarter.

Spagnuolo won the scoreboard anyway. Karlaftis picked Willis two snaps of game clock later. Harrison Butker then missed a 50-yarder wide left, which did not change the result and did put a messy stamp on a messy close. Miami still ran 119 yards and sat on the ball for 34:21. Ten points is the grade. The snap count is the homework.

What held up

  • Mahomes’ accuracy (20-of-24, 246 yards, two passing touchdowns) and the deep-middle/deep-sideline shots to Kelce (48), Gray (24), and Rice (34).
  • Takeaways: Sneed forcing and recovering Willis’ fumble, Karlaftis intercepting Willis with Chris Jones in the play.

What broke

  • Team rushing (88 yards) after 152 against Indianapolis, with Walker at 70 on 18 carries.
  • Third down (3-of-7) and possession (25:39 against 34:21).

Mahomes was efficient. The offense was not durable.

20-of-24 for 246 and two touchdowns is a winning quarterback line. 3-of-7 on third down, 18 first downs, and 25:39 of possession is not a winning drive chart against a division opponent. The Raiders will play the chart, not the rating.

The defense took the ball and still had to live in a long game.

Sneed forced and recovered a Willis fumble, Karlaftis intercepted Willis, and Miami scored 10. That is a Spagnuolo win. It is also 34:21 of Dolphins possession and 119 rushing yards allowed, which is how a 10-point game still feels like a grind.

Red zone and short yardage left points on the grass.

Walker was stuffed on fourth down at the Miami 12. Those are the snaps Bieniemy has to clean before Allegiant Stadium makes them expensive.

Current state

Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

3-0

Kansas City is 3-0 with wins over Denver (31-10), Indianapolis (33-30), and Miami (24-10), and the season has already split into two tapes: the 523-yard, 37:00 possession explosion against the Colts, and the 334-yard, 25:39 grind in Miami. The Raiders game is where the staff decides which tape is the identity.

What we need to work on

Early-down run identity

Walker’s 18 carries for 70 yards did not drag the team total past 88. Against Indianapolis the club ran for 152. Las Vegas will load the box until Bieniemy proves inside zone is a threat, not a tell.

Third down and the clock

3-of-7 on third down is how you lose 34:21 to 25:39 even when you win 24-10. A division road game punishes that math. The Chiefs have to stay on schedule so Spagnuolo is not rationing a front that just played a long one.

Finishing inside the 10

Walker got nothing on fourth down at the 12. Those are the points a one-score AFC West game will not give back.

What to think about

Is this still the 2025 offense in a 3-0 coat?

Arrowhead Pride’s rapid reaction said last year’s Chiefs almost came out in Miami (https://www.arrowheadpride.com/kansas-city-chiefs-analysis/211452/rapid-reaction-last-years-chiefs-almost-come-out-close-call-dolphins). The 6-11 hangover is not a record this year. It is a style. The Raiders will tell us if Reid has actually buried it.

How loud does Mahomes have to be for a young roster?

He already told them they cannot relax (https://www.arrowheadpride.com/kansas-city-chiefs-news/211488/patrick-mahomes-reminds-young-chiefs-team-cant-relax). The next test is whether that message hits before kickoff at Allegiant or after a slow first quarter.

Market versus the West

ESPN FPI sits at 72.6 percent for Las Vegas and DraftKings has Kansas City -5.5 on 46.5. Polymarket still prices the Chiefs at 8.6 percent to win the Super Bowl. That gap is the season in one number: favored this week, untrusted for January, until the run game and the clock catch up to the record.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Las Vegas Raiders

Week 4 is the first AFC West road game of the 3-0 start: Kansas City at the Raiders, CBS, Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT. Miami just showed every division opponent the blueprint — run it, convert third down, sit on the ball, and make Mahomes beat you in a short-possession game.

How they match up

Kansas City’s leverage is still Mahomes, Kelce, Rice, and a Spagnuolo call sheet that just produced a Sneed fumble recovery and a Karlaftis interception while holding Miami to 10. Las Vegas’ leverage is the same one Miami used: physical AFC West snaps, a crowd that will live on third-and-long, and a Chiefs run game that just posted 88 yards. If Walker and the interior cannot win first down, the Raiders do not have to blitz Mahomes to bother him — they can just make him operate left-handed on the road. If Kansas City establishes inside zone, play-action and boot become the same explosives that opened Miami (Kelce 48, Gray 24, Rice 34) without handing Las Vegas the clock.

Get to 100 as a team on the ground

Walker had 70 of Kansas City’s 88 at Miami. The Colts game (152 rushing yards, 37:00) is the target. Inside zone on early downs is how you keep the Raiders from turning this into a passing-down hunt.

Do not donate short yardage

The Nourzad-eligible Walker stuff at the Miami 12 cannot travel. Reid has to have a quarterback sneak, a gap scheme, or a play-action boot ready on fourth-and-1 so a division game does not stall at the 12.

Make Las Vegas one-dimensional, then steal it

Bolton just played an 11-tackle game against 119 rushing yards. Spagnuolo should live in Cover-2 while the run is a threat, then zone-blitz Jones and Karlaftis the first time the Raiders get greedy on early downs. The Miami takeaways were the win. Repeat that, do not repeat the 34:21.

Explosives without the pick

The Rodriguez interception on the deep middle to Rice is the one Mahomes has to leave in Florida. Four verts and the deep over are still live against a split-safety look — just not into a bracket when the Chiefs are already ahead.

  1. Open in 12 personnel and run inside zone at Walker until Las Vegas puts an eighth man in the box.
  2. Play-action boot off that look to Kelce and Noah Gray; Mahomes was surgical rolling and throwing in Miami (20-of-24).
  3. Mesh to Rice/Kelce on second-and-medium, the down Kansas City has to stay out of third-and-7.
  4. Walker spacing screen when the Raiders pin their ears back; punish the same aggressive rush Miami tried to live on.
  5. Cover-2 on early downs to take away the deep shot and let Bolton and the second level rally.
  6. Zone blitz Jones/Karlaftis with a dropping end on second-and-long — the look that helped produce the Willis interception.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

The 2026 Chiefs are unbeaten, and the Miami tape is the first one that feels like a warning instead of a launch. Patrick Mahomes carved Hard Rock Stadium at 20-of-24 for 246 yards and two touchdowns, Kenneth Walker III added 70 yards and a score on 18 carries, and Kansas City still walked out with 18 first downs against 19, 3-of-7 on third down, 88 rushing yards as a team, and 25:39 of possession against Miami’s 34:21. That is a 24-10 win with last year’s footprints on it, and the next Sunday is already a division road game that will not grade on completion percentage.

Andy Reid and Eric Bieniemy got the start they wanted. Travis Kelce ripped a 48-yarder down the middle on the opening series, Kansas City punched a 10-yard touchdown, and a 5-yard score early in the second quarter made it 14-7. Rodriguez took the other way, and a defense that had to keep answering because the offense could not stay on the field. Arrowhead Pride’s rapid reaction put it plainly — after two games that looked reinvented, this one looked like the frustrating 2025 club, and it still won (https://www.arrowheadpride.com/kansas-city-chiefs-analysis/211452/rapid-reaction-last-years-chiefs-almost-come-out-close-call-dolphins).

Steve Spagnuolo’s unit did the quiet winning. L’Jarius Sneed forced and recovered a Malik Willis fumble in the second quarter, George Karlaftis intercepted Willis in the fourth, and Miami’s 329 yards and 8-of-16 on third down never became more than 10 points. Nick Bolton led the tackle sheet with 11. That is complementary football in the scoreboard column and a problem in the clock column: you cannot ask that front to live in 34-minute games every week and also ask it to travel to Allegiant Stadium.

Patrick Mahomes already said the quiet part to a young roster. That message now has a date stamp: Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT, at the Raiders, with the Chargers already three games back in the West according to Arrowhead Addict (https://arrowheadaddict.com/chargers-nightmare-start-is-already-giving-chiefs-massive-afc-west-edge?utm_source=RSS). The division will not wait for the run game to remember the Indianapolis tape (152 rushing yards, 37:00 of possession). It will play the Miami tape until Kansas City rips it up.

Film room

The X’s & O’s

Concepts drawn up on the board — what it is, why it works, and the coaching point.

Offense

Walker downhill, first-down identity

Situation 1st-and-10, opening script at Allegiant, 12 personnel vs. the Raiders

Kansas City just ran for 88 as a team in Miami after 152 against Indianapolis. Walker’s 18-70-1 is volume without a dent. Las Vegas will sit on Mahomes until inside zone forces a safety down. This is how Bieniemy keeps the Chiefs out of the 3-of-7 third-down hole that handed Miami 34:21.

Coaching point Walker has to press the line and pick one cut — the Miami fourth-down stuff at the 12 was a no-gain because the crease never got defined. Creed Humphrey and the guards owe him a vertical double; Reid should keep Nourzad-as-eligible in the short-yardage package but not as a give-up run into a loaded front.

Offense

Boot off the run fake

Situation 2nd-and-6 after a Walker inside-zone look, Raiders overhang chasing the back

Mahomes was 20-of-24 because he was throwing on time, not because Kansas City was under center grinding. Play-action boot is how you turn 88 rushing yards into the same explosives that hit Kelce for 48 and Gray for 24 without asking the line to win a five-man protection on obvious pass.

Coaching point Mahomes has to sell the mesh point and get his eyes to Kelce on the bootleg flat-to-corner before the Raiders’ backside end can flatten. Gray trails as the late dig. Do not scramble into a throwback; the Rodriguez interception came when Kansas City got greedy down the middle.

Offense

Rice/Kelce rub vs. man

Situation 2nd-and-5 / 3rd-and-4 vs. Raiders man coverage, Rice aligned inside

Rice’s 7-for-88 and Kelce’s ability to win in traffic (Arrowhead Pride’s icing touchdown) are the man-beating answers if Las Vegas dares to play the run with extra bodies. Miami already showed that Kansas City will go 3-of-7 when the easy access throws disappear. Mesh puts a ball in Mahomes’ hand before the rush can pin him.

Coaching point Rice has to rip across Kelce’s hip and not balloon the crossing route. Kelce sets the rub, then sits if the linebacker jumps Rice. Walker is the checkdown — the outlet Mahomes did not need often in Miami (20-of-24) but will need on the road if the first crosser is trailed.

Offense

Walker screen, punish the heat

Situation 2nd-and-7 or 3rd-and-5 when Las Vegas walks a safety down and rushes five

Kansas City cannot live in dropback on the road after a 25:39 possession game. A Walker spacing screen turns the Raiders’ aggressiveness into the missing explosive run (team rush was 88; Walker had 70 of it between the tackles). It also protects a young group Mahomes already told not to relax.

Coaching point The left side of the line has to get out in space — no holding downfield, no lunging. Walker must set the rush with a pass-pro hint, then get north. Rice and Worthy block the first white jerseys in the perimeter; this is not a hero ball for Mahomes, it is a possession snap.

Defense

Spagnuolo two-deep, take the shot away

Situation 1st-and-10 / 2nd-and-long vs. the Raiders, safeties split, Bolton in the hole

Cover-2 is how Spagnuolo keeps a division quarterback from that same over route while Bolton (11 tackles) and the linebackers rally to a run game that just posted 119 on this defense. With Chamarri Conner inactive in Miami, the safe shell also protects a shuffled secondary on the road.

Coaching point Corners have to funnel, not gamble — L’Sneed already made his splash by forcing and recovering the Willis fumble, not by jumping a double move. Safeties split the hashes and drive on the catch. Bolton fits the alley; do not let Las Vegas turn early downs into the clock game Miami just played.

Defense

Simulated heat, drop the end

Situation 2nd-and-8 vs. the Raiders, Jones inside, Karlaftis showing, linebacker adding

Karlaftis intercepted Willis on a short throw with Jones on the play; that is the payoff of Spagnuolo making a quarterback throw into a disappearing lane. Las Vegas will see the Miami 8-of-16 third-down tape and try to stay ahead of the chains. A zone blitz on second-and-long is how Kansas City gets a negative snap without emptying the coverage behind Bolton.

Coaching point Show five, rush four. Jones collapses the pocket; Karlaftis either comes or drops into the throwing lane he just undercut in Miami. The adding linebacker has to be the fifth rusher or the hook defender — not both, not late. Sneed stays home after the fumble recovery; do not blitz him into a glance route.

Chess match

Coaching & adjustments

Reid / Bieniemy vs. the Miami drive chart

The openers worked (10-yard touchdown, 5-yard touchdown, Kelce’s 48-yarder). Allegiant is not the week to get cute on fourth-and-1 or to abandon inside zone after two stuffed runs.

Spagnuolo’s two-tape problem

Ten points and two takeaways (Sneed fumble force/recovery, Karlaftis interception) is a win. 119 rushing yards and 34:21 is a warning. Cover-2 on early downs and the zone blitz on second-and-long is how he keeps Las Vegas from copying Miami’s clock.

Mahomes as the locker-room coach

He already told a young roster it cannot relax after the Miami win. The staff’s job is to script a first quarter that looks like the 7-0 start, not like the 25:39 possession line, so that speech is not required again at 0-0 in the fourth.
