What the tape said

A real review of the most recent result — not a box-score dump, and not a skip-ahead.

W
Las Vegas Raiders Week 4 · Sun, Oct 4 · 3:25 PM CT
KC 30–27

Kansas City beat Las Vegas 30-27 at Allegiant Stadium. The scoring sequence opened with a Las Vegas touchdown of 27 yards at Q1 8:10 that made it 0-7, then a Kansas City touchdown of 2 yards at Q1 2:23 that made it 7-7. Las Vegas added a 32-yard field goal at Q2 3:19 (7-10) and a 48-yard field goal at Q2 0:00 (10-13) around a Kansas City 28-yard field goal at Q2 1:22 (10-10).

The second half of the scoreboard was a Kansas City surge that still leaked points. A Kansas City touchdown of 8 yards at Q4 14:50 made it 17-13. Las Vegas answered with a 44-yard field goal at Q4 11:12 (17-16) and a 48-yard field goal at Q4 4:53 (17-19). A Kansas City touchdown of 2 yards at Q4 3:21 moved the board to 23-19. Kenneth Walker III’s 22-yard rush, right end, at Q4 3:02 made it 30-19. Brock Bowers caught a 20-yard touchdown at Q4 1:46, and the two-point throw to Cody White succeeded, leaving 30-27. X. Nwankpa intercepted Kirk Cousins at Q4 0:10.

Cousins hit Ian Thomas on a 27-yard touchdown, deep left, at Q1 8:10. Mahomes hit Xavier Worthy for 41 yards to the Las Vegas 7, then Tyquan Thornton for 42 to the Las Vegas 5 and, later, 55 to the Las Vegas 5, with Quay Walker flagged for unnecessary roughness on that last catch. Walker’s listed line is 22 carries, 177 yards, and 2 rushing touchdowns, on 23 touches. Team rushing was 189 on 26 attempts.

The win was real, and it was narrow. Las Vegas won the clock and the down chart. Possession was 35:42 for Las Vegas and 24:18 for Kansas City. Las Vegas had 72 rushing yards on 26 attempts, 437 total yards, and 28 first downs. Ashton Jeanty had 21 touches. Michael Mayer had 8 catches, Bowers 6. Kansas City had 399 total yards, 18 first downs, 12 drives, and zero turnovers. A Creed Humphrey fumble at Q4 1:35 was recovered by Mahomes, so it stayed off the turnover line. Las Vegas also turned it over on downs at Q3 11:37 and lost the kickoff fumble forced by J. Bassa and recovered by J. Cochrane.

Zero sacks on Cousins despite 10 quarterback hits. Mahomes absorbed 8 hits and 2 sacks. The pass-rush tape is hits without sacks. Cousins was sacked zero times on 52 attempts. Kansas City recorded 10 quarterback hits. George Karlaftis shows up in the pressure notation on several incompletions, including the interception throw, and that is pressure detail, not a sack. Mahomes was hit 8 times and sacked twice. Third down at 5-of-13 is the lagging chapter of an otherwise winning afternoon.

What held up

  • Walker’s 23 touches and the 189-yard team rush total
  • Thornton’s 5 catches for 111 yards and 2 touchdowns before the ankle injury
  • Zero turnovers on 12 drives, plus Nwankpa’s interception and Cochrane’s recovery
  • A 97.2 rating on a 15-of-30 day because the touchdowns were efficient

What broke

  • 5-of-13 on third down and 18 first downs against a defense that allowed 399 yards anyway
  • 24:18 of possession against 35:42 for Las Vegas
  • Kansas City penalties that wiped plays: N. Williams twice, P. Woods illegal formation, F. Anudike-Uzomah roughing, and L. Sneed holding

The run column traveled

Walker carried 22 times for 177 yards inside a team total of 189 on 26 attempts. That is the identity Bieniemy can hand Herbert, not a one-play spike.

Explosives covered a 15-of-30 day

Mahomes was 15-of-30 for 225 yards and 2 touchdowns with a 97.2 rating, while team net passing was 210. Worthy and Thornton created the chunk gains the completion percentage did not.

Takeaways had names

Nwankpa intercepted the throw intended for Mayer. Bassa forced the kickoff fumble and Cochrane recovered it. Kansas City’s turnover count stayed at zero.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Los Angeles Chargers

Host Justin Herbert at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium on Sun Oct 18, 3:25 PM CT, stay unbeaten, and do it with a receiver room that no longer includes Thornton’s 111-yard afternoon.

How they match up

Kansas City brings a 189-yard rushing identity, multiple tight-end volume, and a quarterback who just lived on explosives inside a 15-of-30 day. Los Angeles brings Herbert, a quarterback who will see a defense that can record hits — 10 of them against Cousins — without recording sacks. The 41.5 total says the market expects fewer points than 30-27. DraftKings -7.5 and ESPN FPI at 74.4 percent say home favorite, not a waltz. Peter Woods limited and Rice’s missed practices tilt the stress toward protection and the tight ends.

Hand Herbert two-deep

Cousins produced 365 net passing yards with zero sacks. Cover-2 takes the deep shot away and asks the second level to tackle. That is the shell, independent of any takeaway credit.

Steal series with Walker

Early downs to Walker keep a 24:18-style clock from becoming the home story again. Las Vegas held it for 35:42. Arrowhead should not volunteer that script.

Easy answers for 8 hits

Screens, mesh, and tight-end rubs are the response to a limited Woods practice and a pocket that was hit 8 times. Mahomes does not need another 15-of-30 if the menu is shorter.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Walker and a tight-end surface, the personnel Arrowhead Addict says this offense already leans on.
  2. On second and medium, mesh Kelce and Gray and let Worthy sit if the corner chases.
  3. If the Chargers rush up the field, throw the running-back screen and let the line get to space.
  4. Take a vertical shot to Worthy once the run is respected, the same deep-middle stress he won for 41 yards in Las Vegas, without labeling it play-action.
  5. Call Cover-2 on Herbert’s early dropbacks and rally Delane’s tackling standard — he had 10 stops against Las Vegas.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

Andy Reid’s club is 4-0 because Kenneth Walker III and a clean turnover sheet kept arriving after Patrick Mahomes went 15-of-30.

Eric Bieniemy’s group won the math that shows up in the rushing column and lost the math that shows up on third down. Kansas City was 5-of-13, posted 18 first downs, and ran 12 drives. Las Vegas matched the 12 drives, added 28 first downs, and was 5-of-14. Kirk Cousins was 31-of-52 with a rating of 85.8 and zero sacks. Mahomes finished 15-of-30 for 225 yards and 2 touchdowns, took 2 sacks, and posted a 97.2 rating. Kansas City recorded 10 quarterback hits; Las Vegas recorded 8 hits on Mahomes.

The receiver room that hosted that stat line is not the room that hosts Justin Herbert. Tyquan Thornton’s line was 5 catches, 111 yards, and 2 touchdowns, then an ankle injury on the 55-yard catch. Arrowhead Pride reports he is out for the foreseeable future. Arrowhead Addict reports Rashee Rice has missed two straight practices out of the bye. Travis Kelce and Noah Gray, at 2 catches each in Las Vegas, are the sturdy answers while Mahomes tests younger wideouts.

Steve Spagnuolo’s problem is finishing. Arrowhead Pride has him calling the pass rush a step away, with 4 sacks and a 2.6 percent sack rate through Week 5. Cousins absorbed 10 hits and was not sacked. The interception belongs to X. Nwankpa, on a short left throw intended for Michael Mayer at Q4 0:10. The fumble on the kickoff belongs to J. Bassa as the forcer and J. Cochrane as the recoverer. Those are player credits.

Sunday’s assignment at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium is to keep Walker’s identity, give Mahomes easier answers than 15-of-30, and make Herbert throw into two-deep shells with simulated pressure behind them. The 41.5 total is a lower-scoring price than the 30-27 tape. Treat 4-0 as a sample, then go win the division game.

The X’s & O’s

Concepts drawn up on the board — what it is, why it works, and the coaching point.

Offense

Walker inside zone on the opening series

Situation Early down at Arrowhead against the Chargers, before Herbert’s offense can stack the clock the way Las Vegas did for 35:42

Walker just posted 177 yards on 22 carries inside a 189-yard team rush day, and Bieniemy needs that identity while Thornton is out and Woods is limited.

Coaching point Zone steps, one cut, and let Walker press the line of scrimmage. Kelce and Gray fit the extra-tight-end surface this offense already uses. Do not ask Mahomes to throw 30 times to steal the series.

Offense

Mesh for Kelce and Gray

Situation Third and medium with Thornton unavailable and Rice’s practice week unresolved

Kansas City was 5-of-13 on third down, and the two tight ends are the healthy rub answers Mahomes can throw on time after taking 8 hits.

Coaching point Kelce and Gray cross, Worthy sits if the corner trails, and Walker is the checkdown on his one-catch role from last week expanded into a real outlet. Reid wants the ball out before the limited line has to hold.

Offense

Walker screen when the rush declares

Situation After a Chargers pressure look, with Woods limited and Mahomes coming off 2 sacks

Eight hits on Mahomes say the easy offense has to be on the call sheet, and a 41.5 total rewards moving the chains without a hero throw.

Coaching point Let the line release and lead Walker in space. Emmett Johnson, who had 1 catch in Las Vegas, can be the changeup. This is a pressure answer, not a trick.

Offense

Four verts once the run is honored

Situation After Walker has drawn the second level, against a Chargers coverage that spins a single deep middle

Worthy already won a 41-yard deep middle against Las Vegas, and the thinner receiver room still has that outside speed if the seams bend.

Coaching point Worthy and a young outside receiver run past the corners. A tight end bends to the hash. Mahomes throws the open vertical. The call is four verts.

Defense

Cover-2 on Herbert’s early dropbacks

Situation Standard downs at Arrowhead, with Las Vegas having just posted 365 net passing yards and 28 first downs

Spagnuolo cannot ask a 2.6 percent sack rate to erase deep shots. Two deep and five under take the explosive away and let Delane, who had 10 tackles, rally.

Coaching point Corners sink, safeties stay on top, and linebackers fit the tight end and the checkdown. The goal is to make Herbert drive, the way the hits on Cousins did not, because zero sacks left the deep ball available.

Defense

Zone blitz as a Herbert call

Situation Chargers third down, when four rushers have been a step away and Cousins just survived 10 hits with zero sacks

This is a call to use against Herbert: show heat, drop a defensive lineman, and rush a second-level player so the pocket has to move.

Coaching point Spagnuolo picks the dropper before the snap and tells the rusher to come from the second level. Karlaftis can still be the finisher on the edge. Judge the call by a hurried Herbert throw, not by a takeaway from a different game.
