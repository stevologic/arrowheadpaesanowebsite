Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

8-2

The club is 8-2 with three straight wins, tied with Denver for the AFC West lead, and walking into Buffalo as a three-point underdog. The Arizona afternoon was efficient.

What we need to work on

Tackle availability before an improved Bills rush

Arrowhead Pride's matchup questions lead with whether the offensive line can hold up against Buffalo's improved pass rush, and note coverage issues on the other side of that defense. Fred Johnson's promotion to the active roster is the concrete response to tackle concerns, not a finished answer. Mahomes took one sack and five hits against Arizona; Buffalo will not be that pocket.

Early-down run fits for Cook

Jones has named Cook the priority. Arizona gained 75 rushing yards on 21 attempts. That number does not travel. Spagnuolo has to decide how often the second level rallies versus how often Karlaftis and Jones stay in the rush, because Allen punishes a defense that sells out on one or the other.

Red-zone and third-down identity without assuming Walker

Johnson just carried 23 times. Bieniemy needs a script that does not require a second featured back to be available on a short week.

What to think about

Is 46.6 percent the honest read?

FPI has Kansas City at 46.6 and Buffalo at 53.1, with DraftKings at Bills minus 3 and a 46.5 total. The Arizona margin can talk fans into a favorite. The market and the model did not.

How much of the West is this game?

Arrowhead Pride has Denver holding a slight edge in a tied AFC West. A loss in Buffalo does not end the division. It does hand the Broncos another week of separation before Kansas City plays the Rams on Thu Dec 3, 7:15 PM CT.

Downs as a real piece, not a box-score extra

He had 4 catches against Arizona. The Week 12 power-rankings roundup on Arrowhead Pride flags him as an extension candidate while ESPN's sampling has Kansas City at 3. That is a usage question for Buffalo's coverage issues, not a trophy.

Looking ahead

How to travel into a Bills building that has owned the regular season

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Buffalo Bills

Win the Cook downs, keep Allen out of the easy middle, and give Mahomes a moving picture against a pass rush Arrowhead Pride calls improved. Kansas City is catching three on Thu Nov 26, 7:20 PM CT at Buffalo on NBC. The rivalry note from Arrowhead Pride is the stakes: Buffalo has won five consecutive regular-season meetings.

How they match up

Buffalo's identity in this matchup, as framed by the club's own voices and the Pride preview, is Allen plus Cook, with a defense that can rush and has coverage issues to attack. Kansas City just put up 435 yards and 7-of-13 on third down with zero giveaways, led by Mahomes' 3 touchdown passes and Johnson's 27 touches. The mismatch risk is not Arizona's 130 net passing yards repeating. It is Cook stressing the same linebacker fits that still allowed McBride 10 touches, and Allen holding the ball against a rush that has not sacked him often when Jones is on the field. Spagnuolo's Cover-2 and simulated pressure are the structural answers. Bieniemy's answers are inside zone on early downs, screens if the rush sells out, and mesh or a boot if Buffalo plays man and heat.

Cook before Allen

Jones said it. Fit the run with Bolton and the second level so Allen is throwing on schedule, not off a plus-run down. Do not treat a sack total against Allen as the grade.

Make the Bills' coverage play the intermediate crossers

Pride's questions piece says the Bills defense has coverage issues even with a better rush. Kelce (6 for 74 and 2 scores vs Arizona), Rice (6 catches), and Downs (4) are the crossers and sits. Worthy is the vertical alert, not the first read.

Protect the tackle spots

Johnson is on the active roster because tackle concerns are real. One sack and five hits was the Arizona pressure line. Boot and screen are the calls if that edge speed shows up early, not a hero dropback on every third-and-long.

Do not donate the explosive

Arizona's one touchdown was a 2-yard score after a 25-yard catch by Harrison Wallace. Buffalo does not need a 14-play drive if Allen gets a free shot. Two-deep is the shell until the down-and-distance says otherwise.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Johnson, the same family as the up-the-middle and right-end carries that built the 14-0 lead, and keep Ott as the change-up rather than a featured split.
  2. If Walker is active off a limited Tuesday, use him as a series change, not a required early-down starter. The proven carry count is Johnson's.
  3. On second-and-manageable, call mesh with Kelce and Rice crossing and Downs as the sit, with Johnson as the checkdown. That is an answer to man coverage and to a rush that Pride says has improved.
  4. Steal a possession with a screen to Johnson or Ott if the edge is crashing. Mahomes took five hits with one sack against Arizona; do not wait for a third-and-12 to take the pressure off.
  5. Use a boot as a called concept to move the pocket, not as a label for anything on the Arizona tape. Put Kelce on the shallow cross and Worthy on the wheel or alert.
  6. Defensively, start Cover-2 on early downs to cap Allen's deep middle, then call a zone blitz on a clear passing down — show heat, drop a lineman, rush the second level. That card is a call.
Last game

What the 34-7 Arrowhead afternoon actually showed

The previous result, with the details that matter to the next assignment.

W
Arizona Cardinals Week 11 · Sun, Nov 22 · 12:00 PM CT
KC 34–7

Kansas City beat Arizona 34-7 at Arrowhead on Sun Nov 22, 12:00 PM CT. Patrick Mahomes found Travis Kelce for an 8-yard touchdown at Q1 9:46 to make it 7-0. Emmett Johnson's 3-yard run at Q1 0:48 made it 14-0. Arizona answered with a 2-yard touchdown at Q2 9:48 to make it 14-7. Kansas City then added a 40-yard field goal, Travis Kelce's 22-yard touchdown at Q2 1:54, a 44-yard field goal at the half, and an 18-yard touchdown at Q4 14:54.

The run game was Johnson's afternoon, not a committee showcase. He had 23 rushes and 4 catches, 27 touches, 105 yards, and the 3-yard score up the middle at Q1 0:48. The early script put him on the edge and inside: a 16-yard right-end gain on the first snap of the series at Q1 14:55, then a string of up-the-middle carries that moved the chains into the red zone. Jaydn Ott finished with 5 rushes, Nate Carter 1, and Justin Fields 2 rushes plus an 0-of-1 throw. That is a defined lead back, with the change-of-pace work arriving after the game was in hand.

Mahomes' 27-of-39, 324-yard, 3-touchdown line sat on top of a team net of 317 passing yards. Kelce had 6 catches for 74 yards and 2 touchdowns, including the 22-yard score down the middle at Q2 1:54. Rashee Rice also had 6 catches, Josh Downs 4, Noah Gray 3, Cyrus Allen 2 — including a 26-yard catch to the Arizona 18 at Q3 0:04 — and Xavier Worthy 2. The miss that shows up on the penalty list is intentional grounding on Mahomes at Q2 0:16, enforced at the Arizona 16, before an incompletion to Worthy. One sack and five quarterback hits is the pressure count. It is not a messy pocket tape.

Steve Spagnuolo's group held Arizona to 205 yards and 5-of-13 on third down. Josh Johnson was 18-of-31 with 3 sacks and an 82.1 rating. George Karlaftis was credited with 2.5 sacks and, at Q3 10:30, forced a fumble on a 9-yard sack that J. Fryar recovered for Arizona. The box lists zero turnovers for both teams, so that strip stayed with the offense. Trey McBride still got 10 touches (1 rush, 9 catches) in a game the visitors could not sustain. Bam Knight had 8 touches, Tyler Allgeier 7 rushes. Arizona also failed on downs at Q2 3:05 and Q4 4:04. Kansas City recorded 7 quarterback hits.

Third down was 7-of-13, a real step from the Atlanta game's 4-of-13, and it should be kept on that prior tape rather than blended into this box. The afternoon closed 34-7. The leverage question for Buffalo is whether 118 team rushing yards and a defined Johnson workload still travel when the back on the other sideline is James Cook.

What held up

  • Mahomes at 27-of-39, 324 yards, 3 touchdowns, a 120.0 rating, one sack, and five hits, with team net passing of 317.
  • Kelce's 6 catches, 74 yards, and 2 touchdowns, including the 22-yard score at Q2 1:54.
  • Karlaftis' 2.5 sacks and 7 team quarterback hits, with Arizona at 205 total yards.
  • Zero giveaways and 7-of-13 on third down across 10 drives.

What broke

  • Team rushing stayed at 118 yards on 31 attempts even with Johnson at 105.
  • Mahomes' intentional grounding at Q2 0:16 backed the offense off the Arizona 16 before the half.
  • McBride still logged 10 touches; volume to a tight end is a warning even when the opponent finishes at 130 net passing yards.
  • J. Hicks' illegal block above the waist at Q4 13:30 was a self-inflicted flag after the lead was already 34-7.

Johnson was the carry count

Twenty-three rushes, four catches, 105 yards, and the 3-yard score at Q1 0:48. Ott, Carter, and Fields were complementary touches, not a split backfield. If Walker is limited, this is the proven Week 11 workload.

The strip was not a takeaway

Karlaftis forced the fumble on the sack at Q3 10:30. Fryar recovered it for Arizona. Both clubs finished with zero turnovers. Credit the pressure, not a turnover.

Third down moved

Kansas City was 7-of-13 after 4-of-13 in Atlanta. Arizona was 5-of-13 and stalled on downs twice. That down-and-distance jump is the useful carry into a Bills game, not the final margin.

Johnson inside zone to open the series

Defense

Cover-2 to cap Allen and rally to Cook

Situation 1st-and-10, Bills in 11 personnel, Cook the declared priority

Jones said the No. 1 goal is Cook. Two deep takes the free shot away from Allen, the shot Arizona never really had while finishing at 130 net passing yards. The five underneath have to tackle. McBride's 10 touches are the reminder that a tight end or back can still eat in a low-yardage game.

Coaching point Spagnuolo: corners sink, safeties stay on top of Allen's deep middle. Bolton and the linebackers rally to Cook without vacating the hook. Karlaftis rushes his side but the call is coverage first. Do not turn this shell into a one-gap guess the play-by-play has not named.

Zone blitz on a clear passing down

Situation 2nd-and-long or 3rd-and-6, Allen in the gun, Cook not the primary threat on that down

This is a call to use against Allen, not a claim about the Arizona strip. Karlaftis forced a fumble that Fryar recovered; the box still shows zero turnovers. Simulated pressure lets Spagnuolo show heat, drop a lineman, and rush a second-level player so Allen throws before Cook can leak out.

Coaching point Show four, rush three, drop Jones or the end into the hook Cook would use as a checkdown. The second-level rusher has to be on time. If Allen pulls it down, the dropped lineman is the force player. Grade the call on whether Allen is on schedule, not on a takeaway.
