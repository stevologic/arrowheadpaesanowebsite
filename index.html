What the tape said

A real review of the most recent result — not a box-score dump, and not a skip-ahead.

Las Vegas Raiders Week 4 · Sun, Oct 4 · 3:25 PM CT
KC 30–27

At Allegiant Stadium on Sun Oct 4, 3:25 PM CT, Kansas City beat Las Vegas 30-27. The Chiefs had 18 first downs, 26 rushes for 189 yards, 210 net passing yards, 399 total yards, a 5-of-13 third-down line, zero turnovers, 12 drives, and 24:18 of possession. The Raiders had 28 first downs, 26 rushes for 72 yards, 365 net passing yards, 437 total yards, a 5-of-14 third-down line, two turnovers, 12 drives, and 35:42 of possession.

Las Vegas scored at Q1 8:10, a 27-yard touchdown pass from Cousins to Ian Thomas, and led 7-0. Kansas City answered at Q1 2:23 with a 2-yard touchdown to tie it 7-7. The second quarter was a field-goal trade: Las Vegas from 32 at Q2 3:19, Kansas City from 28 at Q2 1:22, and Las Vegas from 48 as the half expired. Halftime was 13-10 Raiders. The third quarter produced no points.

The fourth quarter decided it. Kansas City took a 17-13 lead on an 8-yard touchdown at Q4 14:50. Matt Gay's field goals of 44 and 48 put the Raiders back in front, 19-17, at Q4 4:53. Kansas City then scored a 2-yard touchdown at Q4 3:21 to lead 23-19. On the kickoff, Harrison Butker kicked 59 yards, Dareke Young returned it, Bassa forced the fumble, and Cochrane recovered at the Las Vegas 22. Walker ran right end for 22 yards and a touchdown at Q4 3:02. The extra point made it 30-19.

Cousins still had an answer. Felix Anudike-Uzomah was flagged for roughing the passer at Q4 2:38, then L'Jarius Sneed was flagged for defensive holding at Q4 2:10. Bowers caught a 20-yard touchdown at Q4 1:46 and Cousins hit White for the two-point conversion to make it 30-27. Nwankpa intercepted a short left throw intended for Michael Mayer, with George Karlaftis in the rush picture on that snap.

Walker was the offense: 22 carries, 177 yards, two rushing touchdowns, plus one catch, 23 touches. Mahomes added two rushes. Khyiris Tonga and Matt Araiza had one rush each, including Araiza's 12-yard right-end gain out of punt formation. Emmett Johnson had one catch and no rushes. Thornton had five catches for 111 yards and two touchdowns. Worthy had four catches, including a 41-yarder to the Las Vegas 7. Kelce and Noah Gray had two catches each. The early drive that died on downs was a Walker rush up the middle for 1 yard to the Las Vegas 49.

What held up

  • Walker at 22 carries, 177 yards, and two rushing touchdowns, with the 22-yard score coming off the Cochrane recovery.
  • Explosives to Thornton (42 and 55) and Worthy (41), plus a clean turnover line at zero.
  • Nwankpa interception at Q4 0:10, and Mansoor Delane's 10 tackles in a secondary that had to tackle after the catch all afternoon.

What broke

  • Third down at 5-of-13, and a Q1 turnover on downs on a 1-yard Walker rush.
  • Two sacks and eight quarterback hits on Mahomes, against a 15-of-30 passing line.
  • Ten quarterback hits and zero sacks on Cousins, plus 28 Raiders first downs and a 13-10 halftime deficit.

The run bought the score, not the clock

Kansas City rushed for 189 on 26 attempts and still held the ball for 24:18. Las Vegas rushed for 72 on 26 attempts and held it for 35:42. Walker can win a down. He cannot, by himself, shrink a 31-of-52 passing day.

Hits without sacks left Cousins upright

Kansas City recorded 10 quarterback hits. Cousins was not sacked. He finished 31-of-52 with a rating of 85.8 and 365 net passing yards behind him as a team line. Pressure that does not end the play is a conversation, not a stop.

The special-teams snap changed the fourth

Bassa forced the fumble on Young's return. Cochrane recovered it. Walker scored from 22 yards on the next snap, at Q4 3:02.

Current state

Where we stand

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

4-0

Kansas City is 4-0 after the Week 5 bye, coming off a 30-27 road win that looked like a track meet in the passing game for the other sideline and a power afternoon for Walker. The next assignment is a home division game against an 0-5 Chargers team, with the passing room missing Rice and Thornton and the line missing Simmons.

What we need to work on

Finish the rush

Ten hits and zero sacks is the Allegiant residue Spagnuolo has to clean up before Herbert. Simulated pressure has to end drives, not just decorate the stat sheet.

Early-down and short-yardage identity

The 1-yard failure on downs at Q1 10:48 is the clip. Walker can rip explosives. The staff still needs a trusted look inside the three-yard window, which is why the short-yardage conversation is live.

Replace Thornton's 111 without asking Mahomes for 40 throws

Thornton had five catches, 111 yards, and two touchdowns on a 15-of-30 day. Worthy, Kelce, Gray, Johnson, and Jalen Royals have to turn fewer dropbacks into the same explosives. Mahomes was hit eight times.

What to think about

How thin is too thin at receiver

Arrowhead Addict has Royals as the opportunity created by Rice and Thornton. Arrowhead Pride wants Worthy to carry the outside. Reid can also lean on the tight-end rate the same site has tracked. The debate is volume, not philosophy.

Whether 4-0 should quiet the protection worry

Simmons is out. Mahomes took two sacks and eight hits on 30 attempts. A -7.5 home number does not retire that tape. Bieniemy has to script answers before Herbert's rushers do.

Looking ahead

The next-game plan

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

Los Angeles Chargers

Host the Chargers at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium on Sun Oct 18, 3:25 PM CT, stay in front of an 0-5 team that still has Herbert, and do it without Rice, Thornton, or Simmons. The price is Chiefs -7.5. The football job is smaller than the price.

How they match up

Kansas City wants to run through Walker, then throw off that action to Kelce, Gray, Worthy, and Royals. Los Angeles will try to make Mahomes hold the ball behind a line missing Simmons, the way the Raiders landed eight hits. On defense, Spagnuolo cannot live with another 365-net-passing afternoon. Two-deep shells take the shot away. Zone blitzes are a call when the down says Herbert has to find the dropper. Give the Chargers credit: a winless division rival with Herbert is still a quarterback problem, not a scrimmage.

Make Walker the down, not the gadget

Feed the 22-carry identity early. Inside zone on early downs keeps Herbert's offense on the sideline longer than the 24:18 Allegiant clock allowed.

Protect the 30-attempt version of Mahomes

Screens and defined reads cut the eight-hit problem.

Two-deep, then the second-level rush

Cover-2 takes the Thomas-and-Bowers shot profile away. The zone blitz is the change-up on third-and-medium.

Win the red-area snap with the eligible tight end

Tonga reported as eligible on two different first-quarter snaps at Allegiant, not as one repeated formation. Use that menu in short yardage instead of hoping a solo Walker dive solves it.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Walker, then a boot off that action toward Kelce in the flat.
  2. On the second drive, mesh Kelce and Gray against man, with Royals as the sit and Worthy clearing.
  3. If the rush declares, throw the Walker screen and let the line lead.
  4. On third-and-medium, check to four verticals if the Chargers spin a single-high look, with Worthy on the outside vertical.
  5. Defensively, open in Cover-2. Call the zone blitz once Herbert is in a known protection, and rally the second level to Jeanty-style checkdowns rather than hunting a highlight.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

The 4-0 record is real. The Allegiant tape is a split decision. Kansas City scored 30, turned it over zero times, and rode Kenneth Walker III for 177 yards on 22 carries, and still spent the afternoon chasing a Raiders offense that posted 28 first downs, 365 net passing yards, and 35:42 of possession.

Kirk Cousins was 31-of-52 with a rating of 85.8 and zero sacks against him. Kansas City recorded 10 quarterback hits and did not turn those hits into a sack. Patrick Mahomes was 15-of-30 for 225 yards and two touchdowns, took two sacks, and absorbed eight quarterback hits. Net passing was 210. The explosives were real — Xavier Worthy for 41, Tyquan Thornton for 42 and then 55 — and the down-to-down pass volume was not.

The fourth quarter saved the afternoon. Kansas City trailed 13-10 at halftime, went scoreless in the third, then stacked a touchdown at Q4 14:50, another at Q4 3:21, and Walker's 22-yard rush for a touchdown at Q4 3:02 after Jack Cochrane recovered a fumble forced by J. Bassa on the kickoff return. Las Vegas answered with Brock Bowers from 20 yards and a successful two-point pass to Cody White. Xavier Nwankpa intercepted Cousins at Q4 0:10.

Week 6 is the correction window, not a coronation. Los Angeles is 0-5 and visits GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium on Sun Oct 18, 3:25 PM CT, on CBS. Rashee Rice and Josh Simmons are out. Thornton, who led the team with five catches, 111 yards, and two touchdowns, is sidelined. Andy Reid and Eric Bieniemy have Walker, Travis Kelce, Worthy, and a tight-end-heavy menu. Steve Spagnuolo has to make Justin Herbert's protection identification harder than Cousins' was.

The X’s & O’s

Concepts drawn up on the board — what it is, why it works, and the coaching point.

Offense

Walker's downhill zone

Situation 1st-and-10 early, before Herbert's offense can stack the 28-first-down script Las Vegas used

Walker just posted 177 yards on 22 carries in a 189-yard team rushing day, and Kansas City still needs that identity with Simmons out and the dropback count held near 30.

Coaching point Bieniemy: zone steps, one cut, Walker pressing the point of attack. Tonga can report eligible on a separate snap, not as a copy of the Nourzad-and-Tonga look.

Offense

Boot off the Walker sell

Situation 2nd-and-6 after a successful zone carry, Mahomes moving away from the eight-hit picture

Sell 189 rushing yards, move the pocket, and give Kelce the flat so Simmons' absence does not become another two-sack day.

Coaching point Reid: Mahomes sells the inside zone, boot to the boundary, Kelce settling, Worthy clearing the corner. If the end crashes, throw the flat. If he sits, keep it.

Offense

Kelce-Gray mesh

Situation 3rd-and-5 against man, with Rice and Thornton unavailable

Kelce and Gray are the reliable catchers left in the 15-completion sample, and Royals needs a defined sit rather than a hero route on his expanded chance.

Coaching point Bieniemy: cross Kelce and Gray, Royals sits at the second level, Walker checks down. Mahomes throws the rub, not a 30-yard guess.

Offense

Walker screen as the protection answer

Situation 2nd-and-8 when the Chargers rush declares, same hit profile that produced eight hits and two sacks on Mahomes

A line without Simmons should not be asked to hold up for another deep incomplete to the middle. The screen lets Walker work in space off a 23-touch afternoon.

Coaching point Mahomes: one-step throw, line releasing to the perimeter, Walker catching on the move. Johnson is the change-of-pace outlet, not the featured touch.

Defense

Cover-2 against the shot

Situation 1st-and-10 after a Kansas City score, before Herbert can steal a 27-yard answer like Thomas did at Q1 8:10

Las Vegas posted 365 net passing yards on 20-to-27-yard completions to Thomas, White, Tucker, and Bowers. Two deep, five under takes that shot off the table and asks Delane and Williams to rally.

Coaching point Spagnuolo: safeties at landmark depth, corners sinking then driving. Delane tackles the dig. Do not bail into single-high just because the Chargers are 0-5.

Defense

Zone blitz as the third-down call

Situation 3rd-and-6 to 3rd-and-8, Herbert in the gun, protection declared

Cousins faced 10 hits and zero sacks and still went 31-of-52. The call this week is to rush a second-level defender, drop a lineman, and make Herbert throw the checkdown into numbers.

Coaching point Spagnuolo: show heat, drop the end, rush the backer. Karlaftis owns the contain.
