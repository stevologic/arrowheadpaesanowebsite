Current state

A 5-2 club still patching the late-game picture

After the last kickoff: the work list, and the questions the building should be arguing.

5-2

Kansas City is 5-2 and home for the Jets after an offense that moved the ball at Denver and a defense that did not finish a 24-13 lead. The next kickoff is Sun Nov 8, 12:00 PM CT on CBS.

What we need to work on

Finish a two-score lead

Arrowhead Pride framed the defense's week around killer instinct after Kansas City converted to open the fourth quarter ahead 24-13. The Jets game plan has to include what happens after the scoreboard looks safe: down, distance, and who is on the field for Coleman-type runs and Sutton-type shots.

Protect Mahomes without emptying the run

Ten hits and two sacks came with Walker at 21 carries and the team at 24 rushes for 90 yards. Bieniemy can keep that carry count and still steal a few dropbacks with screens to Walker and Johnson so the 41-attempt day is not the default.

Short yardage that is not a hope throw

Arrowhead Addict's short-yardage piece lands on the same tape as Walker's no-gain at the Denver 1. Nourzad reported eligible once, at Q4 15:00. If that look is the answer, it has to be called on the snap that needs a yard, not stored for a different series.

What to think about

Is minus 10 a finish number or a box-score number?

FPI at 80.3 percent and DraftKings at minus 10 describe the Jets matchup, not the Denver finish. Fans should weigh whether 399 yards and 6-of-14 on third down are the identity, or whether 24-28 is the identity until a lead survives the fourth quarter.

How much roster churn is real help?

Means on the practice squad and De Jesus back as a return option, with Nikko Remigio practicing in full on Thursday, are depth notes. They do not change Rice, Kelce, Worthy, or Johnson for Sunday unless the injury sheet says otherwise. Cap space at $1.6 million limits a bigger swing.

Looking ahead

How Kansas City should play the Jets at home

How they match up, what Kansas City should script, and the keys that decide it.

New York Jets

Host the Jets, cash a 10-point home number, and treat the fourth quarter as the assignment Denver already graded. Kickoff is Sun Nov 8, 12:00 PM CT at Arrowhead on CBS.

How they match up

Kansas City brings a quarterback at 30-of-41 and 120.1, a back who just handled 25 touches, and a rookie receiver-runner in Johnson who posted 128 yards. The Jets get a defense that can shrink a game — Denver was held to 2-of-11 and 294 yards — and an offense that just gave up 10 hits and the last two touchdowns. Style edge is Kansas City's if the script stays on schedule. Leverage flips if penalties and the 1-yard line travel from Empower Field to Arrowhead.

Stay ahead of the sticks with Walker

Twenty-one carries for 69 yards was the floor, not a featured day. Early downs to Walker, with Johnson's 15-yard inside run as the changeup, keep Mahomes out of the 10-hit pocket the Broncos found.

Use Rice and Kelce before the shot

Rice's 7 catches and Kelce's 5 are the layup menu. Worthy and Cyrus Allen can take the verticals once the Jets squeeze the crossers. Do not open with the deep middle incompletion Allen saw against Denver.

Rally tackles in a two-deep shell

Sutton's 23 and Waddle's 35 were the explosives. Cover 2 takes the top off similar shots and asks the second level — Nick Bolton, Drue Tranquill, Justin Hicks — to tackle the checkdown the way they did not always tackle Coleman (15 touches).

Change the picture on obvious passing downs

Spagnuolo's zone blitz is the call when the Jets need a third down, the same down Denver converted only twice in 11 tries. Karlaftis, Felix Anudike-Uzomah, and the second-level rushers show heat and drop a lineman. It is a picture change for this week, built off the sack Karlaftis already owns.

  1. Open with under-center inside zone to Walker and live with a modest gain if the fit is sound.
  2. On the first second-and-medium, throw mesh to Rice and Kelce with Johnson as the checkdown.
  3. If the Jets rush four and squeeze, screen to Walker behind the 10-hit lesson from Denver.
  4. Once the run is fit, boot Mahomes to the perimeter with Kelce as the flat option.
  5. In the red zone, call the Nourzad eligible look on the down that needs a yard, not a series earlier.
  6. After a score, check to Cover 2 and rally. Do not sit in a single-high look that Sutton and Waddle just stressed.

Mile High slipped from 24-13 to 24-28

Denver Broncos Week 8 · Sun, Nov 1 · 3:25 PM CT
KC 24–28

At Empower Field, Denver led 3-0 on a 39-yard field goal at Q1 9:43, 5:17 into the game. Kansas City answered with an 11-yard touchdown at Q1 6:04, trailed 7-6 after a 45-yard Denver field goal, then led 14-6 on a 73-yard touchdown at Q2 7:40. A 1-yard Denver touchdown at Q2 1:15 made it 14-13, Harrison Butker's 45-yard field goal at Q2 0:19 made it 17-13, and a 4-yard touchdown at Q3 1:38 made it 24-13. Denver's 2-yard touchdown at Q4 8:55 and 5-yard touchdown at Q4 1:56 finished it 24-28.

The box is not a stalled offense. Kansas City had 22 first downs, 6-of-14 on third down, 90 rushing yards on 24 attempts, 309 net passing yards, 399 total yards, 11 drives, zero turnovers, and 31:29 of possession. Denver had 21 first downs, 2-of-11 on third down, 77 rushing yards on 23 attempts, 217 net passing yards, 294 total yards, 11 drives, zero turnovers, and 28:31. Bo Nix was 20-of-32 with 1 sack and a 103.9 rating. Kansas City recorded 2 quarterback hits. Denver recorded 10 hits on Mahomes, who was sacked twice.

Walker touched the ball 25 times (21 rushes, 4 catches) and was the run identity: right-end gains of 10 and 10 in the second quarter, then a holding flag on Trey Smith that wiped the second of those. Johnson added a 15-yard run up the middle and a 6-touch day, plus the receiving line that won him Pepsi NFL Rookie of the Week. Rice had 7 catches, Kelce 5, Worthy 3. The explosive throws were there. So were the 10 hits.

George Karlaftis had the sack total (1) and forced the fumble on the Nix sack at the Kansas City 46. Adam Trautman recovered for Denver. That is the tape: pressure created a loose ball Denver kept. Jonah Coleman finished with 15 touches and the 5-yard touchdown run up the middle at Q4 1:56. Marvin Mims Jr. had a 14-yard right-end run to the Kansas City 4 on that drive. Courtland Sutton's 23-yard catch and Jaylen Waddle's 35-yard catch were the deep answers Spagnuolo did not erase.

The finish was special teams and the goal line. Butker's 54-yard try at Q4 2:36 was no good, wide right. Walker then ran right end to the Denver 12 and the Denver 1, and up the middle to the 1 for no gain before the turnover on downs at Q4 0:00. Hunter Nourzad's eligible report was the Q4 15:00 snap, a 1-yard Walker carry, not those late snaps. Alex Palczewski reported eligible on the Q2 2:00 Nix run to the 1, among other Denver reports. Flags piled up on Kansas City: Kristian Fulton, Jack Cochrane, Kelce, R Mason Thomas, Fulton again, Trey Smith, Drue Tranquill, Alohi Gilman twice, Jadon Canady, and Jaylon Moore.

  • Third-down defense: Denver 2-of-11, 294 total yards, 28:31 possession
  • Karlaftis's sack and forced fumble, even with Trautman recovering for Denver

What broke

  • 10 quarterback hits and 2 sacks allowed on Mahomes
  • Butker's 54-yard miss and the turnover on downs at the Denver 1

399 yards, still 24-28

Mahomes at 30-of-41 and 321 yards with 3 touchdowns was enough to lead 24-13. It was not enough once Denver scored at Q4 8:55 and Q4 1:56.

Ten hits, two sacks

Denver hit Mahomes 10 times and sacked him twice. Kansas City hit Nix twice and sacked him once, the Karlaftis play that also forced the fumble Trautman recovered. The pass rush showed up. It did not close the game.

The 1-yard line and the 54-yard miss

Walker reached the Denver 1 twice on the last drive and was stopped. Butker missed from 54, wide right, at Q4 2:36. Those two snaps sit next to the 6-of-14 third-down line as the points that did not come.

The story so far

Always looking ahead

The assignment is the New York Jets at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium, Sun Nov 8, 12:00 PM CT on CBS, with Kansas City a 10-point home favorite. Denver already showed what a one-score road game can do to a 24-13 lead.

Patrick Mahomes went 30-of-41 for 321 yards and 3 touchdowns with a 120.1 rating, and the offense posted 22 first downs, 399 yards, and 31:29 of possession. Kenneth Walker III carried 21 times for 69 yards inside a 24-carry, 90-yard team rush. Emmett Johnson caught 5 passes for 128 yards and a touchdown. That is a real afternoon of offense. It still ended 24-28.

Steve Spagnuolo's unit held Denver to 294 yards, 2-of-11 on third down, and 28:31 of possession, with zero turnovers on either side. The fourth quarter did not match that control. Arrowhead Pride's look at the defense's search for a killer instinct starts from the opening of that quarter, Kansas City ahead 24-13. Denver then scored on a 2-yard touchdown at Q4 8:55 and a 5-yard touchdown at Q4 1:56.

Home against the Jets is the reset. ESPN FPI has Kansas City at 80.3 percent. DraftKings has the Chiefs minus 10 with a 47.5 total. That number assumes the 6-of-14 third-down line and the Mahomes accuracy travel, and that the late collapse does not. Andy Reid and Eric Bieniemy still have a short-yardage problem on film: Walker was stopped at the Denver 1, Harrison Butker missed a 54-yard field goal wide right, and the last drive ended on downs.

The roster around that plan is thin on cap room and busy on the practice squad. Arrowhead Pride lists about $1.6 million in space as of Thursday, November 5. Bub Means and Jacob De Jesus are practice-squad adds, not a change to the Sunday skill rotation of Rashee Rice, Travis Kelce, Xavier Worthy, and Johnson.

Walker downhill before the Jets squeeze the box

Situation Early first and second down at Arrowhead, before Denver's 10-hit pocket becomes the Jets' plan

The team rushed 24 times for 90 yards and Walker had 21 carries for 69. That identity has to show up before Mahomes is asked to drop back 41 times again. Johnson's 15-yard inside carry is the same family of run, a one-cut answer if the Jets spill Walker.

Coaching point Bieniemy: zone steps, one cut, no dancing. Walker presses the point of attack and bends only after the linebacker declares. If the fit stacks the interior, hand the next one to Johnson rather than asking Mahomes to scramble into a hit.

Boot off the Walker sell

Situation Play-action call on an early down once Walker has already carried inside zone

This is the install for the Jets, using Walker's 21-carry volume as the sell. Kelce in the flat and Rice on the intermediate cross give Mahomes a throw away from the interior hits Denver landed.

Two-deep after Kansas City scores

Situation Jets with the ball after a Kansas City score, similar to Denver trailing 24-13 and still throwing deep

Sutton caught a 23-yard deep ball and Waddle a 35-yarder. A two-deep, five-under shell takes that shot away and asks Bolton, Tranquill, and Hicks to tackle. Denver still ran Coleman 11 times; the rally has to be real.

Zone blitz on the Jets' third down

Situation Third-and-medium at Arrowhead, Jets needing a throw

Denver was 2-of-11 on third down while Karlaftis posted the sack and forced a fumble Trautman recovered. The call this week is simulated pressure: show heat, drop a lineman, rush from the second level, and make the Jets' quarterback throw before a Sutton-length route develops.
